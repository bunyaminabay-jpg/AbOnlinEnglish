Adverbs of Frequency — Sıklık Zarfları

Bir şeyi ne sıklıkla yaptığını anlatmak için sıklık zarfları kullanılır. Türkçe'deki "her zaman, genellikle, bazen, nadiren" kelimeleri gibi — ama cümledeki yerleri Türkçe'den farklıdır!

📌 Sıklık Skalası — % ile

ZarfTürkçeYüzdeÖrnek
alwaysher zaman100%I always have breakfast.
usually / normallygenellikle~80%She usually walks to school.
often / frequentlysık sık~60%He often reads before bed.
sometimesbazen~40%They sometimes eat out.
rarely / seldomnadiren~20%I rarely eat fast food.
neverhiçbir zaman0%She never skips breakfast.

📌 Cümledeki Yeri — Çok Önemli!

Fiil TürüKuralÖrnek
to be (am/is/are)to be'den SONRAShe is always tired. ✅
Diğer fiillerfiilden ÖNCEI usually eat breakfast. ✅
sometimes/oftencümle sonunda da olabilirI eat vegetables sometimes. ✅
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Yer hatası!
❌ I always am late. → ✅ I am always late. (am'den sonra!)
❌ She eats usually breakfast. → ✅ She usually eats breakfast. (fiilden önce!)
❌ Never I eat meat. → ✅ I never eat meat.

Are Young People Living Healthily?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Our school newspaper asked 300 students about their health habits. Here are the most interesting results:

Exercise: Most students rarely exercise after school. Only 20% go to the gym or play sport regularly — three times a week or more. Many students say they usually stay at home and watch TV instead. Experts say that even a 30-minute walk is good for your health.

Food: 60% of students often eat junk food for lunch. They sometimes eat fruit and vegetables — but not enough. 70% of students never eat breakfast before school. Many doctors say a healthy breakfast helps you concentrate in class.

Sleep: Students usually go to bed after midnight. They always feel tired in the morning. Often, they look at their phones in bed. This is bad for sleep.

What about you? How often do you exercise? Do you always eat breakfast? Small changes can make a big difference to your health!

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How often do most students exercise after school?

Rarely — only 20% exercise three or more times a week.

2What percentage of students never eat breakfast before school?

70%.

3How do students feel in the morning, and why?

They always feel tired, because they usually go to bed after midnight and often look at their phones in bed.

4Give 2 pieces of advice from the text.

Even a 30-minute walk is good for your health / eat a healthy breakfast / don't look at your phone in bed.

1Sağlık anketi

Write 5 sentences about your own healthy (or unhealthy!) habits. Use: always / usually / often / sometimes / rarely / never Example: I always drink water in the morning. I rarely eat fast food.
0 kelime
I always drink a glass of water when I wake up — it's a great habit. I usually eat fruit for breakfast. I often walk to school instead of taking the bus. I rarely eat fast food. I never skip breakfast because it gives me energy for the day.

2Sağlıklı rutin öner

Write 4 health tips for a friend using should/shouldn't + adverbs of frequency.
0 kelime
You should always eat breakfast — it gives you energy and helps you focus. You should often exercise, at least three times a week. You shouldn't stay up very late on school nights. You should rarely drink cola — it is full of sugar and bad for your teeth.
