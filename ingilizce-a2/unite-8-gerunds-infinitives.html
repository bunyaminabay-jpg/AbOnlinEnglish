Gerund (V-ing) ve Infinitive (to + V1)

Bazı fiillerden sonra ikinci fiil -ing alır (gerund), bazılarından sonra to + fiil gelir (infinitive). Bu kural fiile göre değişir; bu yüzden fiilleri grupları ile birlikte öğrenmek en kolay yoldur. Sevmek/nefret etmek bildiren fiiller çoğunlukla -ing, istek/plan bildiren fiiller çoğunlukla to + V1 alır.

📌 Özet tablo

FiilYapıÖrnek
enjoy / love / like / hate / don't mind+ V-ing (gerund)I enjoy watching films. / She loves reading.
want / would like / would love / plan / decide+ to + V1 (infinitive)I want to see that film. / She'd like to visit London.
go + activitygo + V-ingLet's go swimming! / He went shopping.
⚠️enjoy + V-ing: ❌ "I enjoy to watch." ✅ "I enjoy watching." | want + to: ❌ "I want watching." ✅ "I want to watch." | like = hem V-ing hem to-inf (anlam aynı) | STOP: 'stop to do' (durdu ve yaptı) ≠ 'stop doing' (bıraktı)

🔍 Örneklerle

❤️ -ing alan fiiller
I enjoy watching documentaries. → Belgesel izlemekten keyif alırım.
She doesn't mind waiting. → Beklemek onun için sorun değil.
They finished doing their homework. → Ödevlerini yapmayı bitirdiler.
🎯 to + V1 alan fiiller
I want to learn Spanish. → İspanyolca öğrenmek istiyorum.
We decided to stay at home. → Evde kalmaya karar verdik.
He'd like to visit Japan. → Japonya'yı ziyaret etmek istiyor.
🔁 İkisi de olur (like, love, hate, start)
I like swimming. = I like to swim. → Yüzmeyi severim.
It started raining. = It started to rain. → Yağmur yağmaya başladı.
🚨
IMPORTANT — would like her zaman “to” alır
like + -ing / to (genel zevk) ama would like + to (şu anki istek):
✅ I like dancing.  |  ✅ I'd like to dance now.
❌ I'd like dancing now.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Edattan sonra -ing
Edatlardan (about, of, at, in, without...) sonra fiil gelirse her zaman -ing alır:
✅ I'm good at drawing.   ✅ She's interested in making videos.
❌ I'm good at draw.
💡
TIP — go + -ing ile aktiviteler
Spor ve boş zaman etkinliklerinde: go swimming, go shopping, go camping, go skiing, go running.

What Are You Watching?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

People don't have to go to the cinema any more — they enjoy watching everything at home. But what do young people prefer watching? We asked three students.

Selin: "I love watching animations and fantasy series. I hate watching horror films — I can't sleep afterwards! I'm planning to watch a new Turkish sci-fi series this weekend. My friend says it's incredible."

Mert: "I enjoy watching documentaries. I want to learn about nature, history and space. I also like reading reviews before I decide to watch something — I hate wasting time on bad films!"

Zeynep: "I used to enjoy watching long series, but I've stopped watching them. They take too long! Now I prefer to watch short films and YouTube videos. I'd like to make my own videos one day."

What about you? Do you enjoy watching films alone or with friends? What type of films do you like watching?

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What type of films does Selin hate watching?

Horror films — she can't sleep afterwards.

2What does Mert like doing before he watches something?

He likes reading reviews.

3Why has Zeynep stopped watching long series?

They take too long.

4What would Zeynep like to do one day?

She'd like to make her own videos.
