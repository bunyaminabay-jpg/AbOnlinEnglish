Comparatives & Superlatives — Karşılaştırma

İki şeyi karşılaştırmak için comparative (karşılaştırmalı), birden fazla şey arasında en üstünü ifade etmek için superlative (üstünlük derecesi) kullanılır.

📌 Kısa sıfatlar (1 hece) — er / est

SıfatComparative (+er)Superlative (the +est)
tall (uzun)taller thanthe tallest
short (kısa)shorter thanthe shortest
fast (hızlı)faster thanthe fastest
big (büyük) *bigger thanthe biggest
hot (sıcak) *hotter thanthe hottest
nice (güzel) **nicer thanthe nicest

* son harf çiftlenir  |  ** -e'yi at, -er/-est ekle

📌 Uzun sıfatlar (2+ hece) — more / most

SıfatComparativeSuperlative
beautiful (güzel)more beautiful thanthe most beautiful
expensive (pahalı)more expensive thanthe most expensive
interesting (ilginç)more interesting thanthe most interesting
difficult (zor)more difficult thanthe most difficult

📌 Düzensiz — Ezbere!

SıfatComparativeSuperlative
good (iyi)better thanthe best
bad (kötü)worse thanthe worst
far (uzak)further / farther thanthe furthest / farthest
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE
❌ more taller → ✅ taller (kısa sıfata +er, more yok!)
❌ the most tallest → ✅ the tallest
❌ more good → ✅ better
❌ She is taller then me. → ✅ taller than me (then değil, than!)
💡
TIP — as ... as (eşit derece)
İki şey eşit derecede → as + sıfat + as:
She is as tall as her brother. → O kadar uzun ki kardeşiyle aynı.
Eşit değilse → not as ... as: Istanbul is not as cold as Ankara.

My Best Friend vs My Cousin

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

My best friend is Selin and my cousin is Dila. They are both great people, but they are very different!

Selin is taller than Dila. She is the tallest girl in our class — 1.72 metres! Dila is shorter, but she says: "The best things come in small packages!"

Selin is more outgoing than Dila. She talks to everyone and loves parties. Dila is shyer, but she is more creative. She draws the most beautiful pictures I have ever seen.

Both girls are hard-working, but Selin is better at Maths and Science. Dila is better at Art and Literature. I think they are equally intelligent — just in different ways!

The most important thing is that both of them are honest, kind and reliable. Selin has the best sense of humour in our friend group — she makes everyone laugh! In my opinion, good personality is more important than good looks. Don't you agree?

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How tall is Selin?

She is 1.72 metres — the tallest girl in the class.

2Who is more creative — Selin or Dila?

Dila is more creative.

3Who is better at Maths?

Selin is better at Maths and Science.

4What does the writer think is more important than looks?

Good personality.

1Karşılaştırma

Compare two people you know (friends, family members). Write 4 sentences using comparative adjectives. Example: My sister is taller than me. She is more patient than me, but I am funnier!
0 kelime
My sister is taller than me, but I am faster than her. She is more patient than me — she never gets angry. However, I am funnier than her! We are both hard-working students and we get along very well.

2En iyim, en kötüm

Write 3 sentences using superlatives about your class/family/city. Example: ___ is the most hard-working student in our class. ___ is the best...
0 kelime
Selin is the most hard-working student in our class — she always gets top marks. Istanbul is the biggest and most exciting city in Turkey. My father has the best sense of humour in our family. He always makes everyone laugh.
