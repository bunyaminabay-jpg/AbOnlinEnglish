Simple Past Tense — Geçmiş Zaman

Geçmişte başlayıp biten eylemler için kullanılır. Türkçe'deki "-di/-dı/-du/-dü" eki gibi: "gittim, gördüm, yedim." İngilizce'de iki tür fiil var: düzenli (regular) ve düzensiz (irregular).

📌 Düzenli (Regular) Fiiller — sadece +ed

Sade fiilGeçmişÖrnek
visitvisitedWe visited Cappadocia last year.
watchwatchedI watched a film yesterday.
playplayedHe played football on Saturday.
walkwalkedShe walked to school this morning.
traveltravelledThey travelled to Istanbul in 2022.

📌 Düzensiz (Irregular) Fiiller — ezbere!

Sade (V1)Geçmiş (V2)TürkçeÖrnek
gowentgitmekWe went to the beach.
eatateyemekI ate pizza for lunch.
seesawgörmekShe saw a dolphin!
buyboughtsatın almakHe bought a souvenir.
havehadsahip olmakWe had a great time.
taketookalmakI took many photos.
meetmettanışmakI met new friends.
comecamegelmekShe came home late.

📌 Olumsuz & Soru — DID kullan!

YapıKuralÖrnek
Olumsuzdidn't + V1 (sade)I didn't go to school. / She didn't eat breakfast.
SoruDid + özne + V1Did you enjoy the trip? / Did she call you?
Kısa cevapYes/No + did/didn'tYes, I did. / No, she didn't.
🚨
IMPORTANT — didn't + sade fiil!
❌ She didn't went. → ✅ She didn't go.
❌ Did you ate? → ✅ Did you eat?
didn't veya Did kullandığında fiil asla -ed veya V2 almaz — sade hali!
💡
TIP — Geçmişi gösteren zaman ifadeleri
yesterday — dün  |  last week — geçen hafta
last year — geçen yıl  |  ago — önce (two days ago = iki gün önce)
in 2020 — 2020'de  |  this morning — bu sabah (geçti)
Bu ifadeleri görünce Simple Past kullanman gerektiğini bilirsin!

My Holiday in Cappadocia

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Last summer, my family went on holiday to Cappadocia. It was an amazing trip! We stayed there for five days.

On the first day, we visited the underground cities. They were incredible — people lived there thousands of years ago! We walked for hours and took lots of photos.

On the second day, we got up very early — at 4:30 in the morning! We went on a hot air balloon ride. I was scared at first, but it was the most beautiful experience of my life. We saw the fairy chimneys from the sky!

We also ate fantastic local food. We had pottery kebab — they cooked it inside a clay pot! It was delicious. We bought some souvenirs from the local market too.

On the last day, I was sad because I didn't want to leave. Cappadocia is a magical place. I wrote postcards to my friends and told them: "You must visit Cappadocia!"

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How long did the family stay in Cappadocia?

They stayed for five days.

2What did they do on the second day?

They got up very early and went on a hot air balloon ride.

3What is pottery kebab?

Kebab cooked inside a clay pot — local food from Cappadocia.

4How did the writer feel on the last day, and why?

Sad, because they didn't want to leave Cappadocia.
