Gelecek Zaman — going to & will

İngilizce'de gelecekten bahsetmenin iki temel yolu vardır. going to = planladığın, karar verdiğin şeyler. will = anlık karar, tahmin veya söz. Türkçe'deki "-acak/-ecek" ile "-yacak/-yecek" arasındaki nüansa benzer.

📌 1. going to — Plan & Karar

ÖzneYapıÖrnekTürkçesi
II am going to + V1I 'm going to study medicine.Tıp okuyacağım. (plan)
He/SheHe is going to + V1She 's going to visit Istanbul.İstanbul'a gidecek. (plan)
You/We/TheyThey are going to + V1They 're going to move.Taşınacaklar. (plan)

📌 2. will — Anlık Karar, Tahmin, Söz

KullanımÖrnekTürkçesi
Anlık kararI will help you! (şu an karar verdim)Sana yardım edeceğim!
TahminI think it will rain tomorrow.Bence yarın yağmur yağacak.
Söz/vaatI won't be late, I promise.Geç kalmayacağım, söz.
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — going to vs will
going to → önceden planlandı: "I'm going to study tonight." (bugün okuyacağım — sabahtan planlamıştım)
will → şu an karar: "OK, I'll study tonight." (şu an karar verdim)
will → tahmin: "I think technology will change everything."
İkisi de gelecek zamanı anlatır ama bağlam farklıdır!
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE
❌ I am going to will study. → İkisini birden kullanma!
❌ She going to study. → ✅ She is going to study. (is unutulmuş!)
❌ I will to go. → ✅ I will go. (will + to olmaz)
💡
TIP — İpucu kelimeler
going to ile: tonight, next week, soon, this summer, in the future
will ile: I think..., probably, maybe, I'm sure..., I promise

Class Survey: Our Future Plans

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Our English teacher asked us: "What are you going to do in the future?" Here are some of our answers:

Selin (13): "I am going to be a doctor. I will study medicine at university. I want to help sick people and make a difference. I'm definitely going to work hard to achieve my goal."

Can (12): "I'm not sure yet. Maybe I'll be an engineer, or perhaps a footballer! I think football will always be my passion. But I'm going to study hard first — my parents say education is important."

Ayşe (13): "I'm going to be a computer programmer. I already know how to code a little. I think technology will change the world completely. I hope I will create something amazing one day!"

Our teacher: "What wonderful ambitions! Remember: you won't achieve your dreams without hard work. But I believe you will all do something amazing. Your generation is going to change the world!"
