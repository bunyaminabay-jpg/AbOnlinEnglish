must / mustn't / have to / don't have to — Zorunluluk

Bu modallar zorunluluk ve yasak ifade eder. Birbirinden farklı güçlerde kullanılırlar. Aralarındaki farkı iyi anlamak çok önemlidir.

📌 Temel Karşılaştırma Tablosu

ModalAnlamTürkçeÖrnek
mustzorunluluk (güçlü)-malı/-meli (yapman şart)You must wear a seatbelt.
mustn'tyasak (kesinlikle yapma)yapmamalısın — yasak!You mustn't run near the pool.
have tozorunluluk (dışarıdan kural)yapmak zorundayımI have to wear a uniform at school.
don't have togerekli değilyapmak zorunda değilimYou don't have to bring food.
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — mustn't vs don't have to
mustn't = YASAK! Kesinlikle yapma:
"You mustn't smoke here." → Sigara içmen yasak!

don't have to = zorunlu değil, ama yapabilirsin:
"You don't have to bring food." → Yemek getirmek zorunda değilsin. (istersen getirebilirsin)

Bu ikisi Türkçe'ye aynı çevrilebilir ama İngilizce'de anlamları çok farklıdır!
🚨
IMPORTANT — He/She için have to
must → hiç değişmez (she must, he must — hep aynı)
have to → He/She için has to:
She has to wear a uniform. ✅ (has to, not have to)
❌ She have to wear a uniform.

Camping Rules — Read Before You Go!

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Congratulations! You have booked a place on our three-day camping adventure in the Kaçkar Mountains. Please read these rules carefully before you come.

SAFETY RULES — YOU MUST:
• You must always stay on the marked hiking trails.
• You must carry a first aid kit, a compass and a torch.
• You must tell someone where you are going before you leave camp.
• You must wear appropriate footwear — no flip-flops!

YOU MUSTN'T:
• You mustn't feed the wild animals. It's dangerous.
• You mustn't light fires outside the designated areas.
• You mustn't leave litter anywhere in the forest.

YOU DON'T HAVE TO (but it's recommended):
• You don't have to bring your own tent — we provide them.
• You don't have to be an experienced hiker — but you should be fit.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
• You should pack warm clothes — nights are cold at 2,000 metres!
• You shouldn't hike alone. Always go with a partner.

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What three items must campers always carry?

2Why mustn't you feed wild animals?

Because it is dangerous.

3Do campers have to bring their own tent?

No, they don't have to — tents are provided.

4Why should campers pack warm clothes?

Because nights are cold at 2,000 metres.

1Kamp kuralları

Write 6 camp rules using must / mustn't / should / shouldn't / have to / don't have to. Example: You must bring a torch. You mustn't leave litter.

You must always bring a torch and a first aid kit on any mountain hike. You must wear proper boots — never flip-flops. You mustn't leave litter in the forest. You mustn't feed wild animals — it is dangerous. You should pack warm clothes. You don't have to bring a tent if one is provided.
