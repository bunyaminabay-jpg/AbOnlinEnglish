Present Perfect — have / has + V3

Present Perfect İngilizce'nin en "farklı" zamanıdır. Türkçe'de tam karşılığı yoktur. Geçmişte yaşanan ama şimdiki zamanla bağlantısı olan deneyimler, yakın geçmiş olaylar ve hayat tecrübeleri için kullanılır.

📌 Yapı: have/has + past participle (V3)

ÖzneYardımcıV3Örnek
I / You / We / Theyhave ('ve)visitedI have visited Paris. → Paris'e gittim (hayatımda).
He / She / Ithas ('s)eatenShe has eaten sushi. → Suşi yemiş (hayatında).

📌 Anahtar kelimeler — ne ile kullanılır?

KelimeAnlamÖrnek
everhiç (soru)Have you ever tried sushi?
neverhiç (olumsuz)I have never been to Japan.
alreadyzaten, çoktanI have already done my homework.
yethenüz (olumsuz/soru)Have you done it yet? / I haven't done it yet.
justaz önceShe has just arrived.
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — Present Perfect vs Simple Past
Present Perfect → zaman belirsiz / deneyim: I have visited London. (ne zaman — bilinmiyor)
Simple Past → zaman belirli: I visited London in 2022. (ne zaman — belli)

✅ I have seen that film. (genel deneyim)
✅ I saw that film last Monday. (belirli zaman → Simple Past!)

Kural: yesterday / last week / in 2020 / ago → Simple Past kullan!
🚨
IMPORTANT — V3 listeleri
Bazı önemli V3 formları:
go → gone/been · see → seen · eat → eaten · write → written
do → done · take → taken · speak → spoken · buy → bought
"have been to" = oraya gitmiş ve dönmüş | "have gone to" = oraya gitmiş, hâlâ orada

Has Technology Changed School?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Technology has changed schools a lot in the last twenty years. Students today use tablets, interactive boards and educational apps every day. But has technology made learning better? Let's look at some students' experiences.

Mia: "I have just downloaded an amazing maths app. I've already finished ten lessons! Before, I never understood algebra. Now, with AI explanations, I've finally understood it."

Can: "Have you ever used VR in class? We have! Last week, we virtually visited ancient Rome. I've never experienced anything like it. It was incredible."

Ayşe: "I'm worried about screen time. I haven't put my phone down yet today — it's 6 PM! I haven't done my homework yet either. Technology is great but we need to be careful."

The truth is: technology is a tool. Students who use it wisely have improved dramatically. But some students have become addicted. Have you ever thought about how you use technology?

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What app has Mia just downloaded?

An amazing maths app.

2What did Can's class do last week with VR?

They virtually visited ancient Rome.

3What is Ayşe worried about?

Screen time — she hasn't put her phone down all day and hasn't done her homework yet.

4According to the text, what is the key to using technology well?

Using it wisely.
