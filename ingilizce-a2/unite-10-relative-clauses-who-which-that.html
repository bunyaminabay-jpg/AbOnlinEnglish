Relative Clauses — who, which, that, where

Relative clauses bir ismi tanımlayan, ona bilgi ekleyen yan cümleciklerdir. Türkçe'deki "-an/-en" eki veya "ki" bağlacı gibi çalışır. "The man who lives next door" = "Yan komşu adam."

📌 Hangi zamiri ne zaman?

ZamirNe için?ÖrnekTürkçesi
whokişiler içinThe teacher who teaches us is kind.Bize ders veren öğretmen nazik.
whichnesneler/hayvanlar içinThe book which I bought is great.Aldığım kitap harika.
thatkişi de nesne de (her ikisi)The film that I saw was amazing.İzlediğim film harikaydı.
whereyerler içinThe city where I live is beautiful.Yaşadığım şehir güzel.
whoseaitlik (kimin)The girl whose bag is red is my friend.Çantası kırmızı olan kız arkadaşım.

🔍 Adım adım nasıl kurulur?

İki cümleyi birleştir
The girl is my sister. + She plays violin.
→ The girl who plays violin is my sister.
I read a book. + The book was about history.
→ The book that I read was about history.
where kullanımı
This is the café. + I met her there.
→ This is the café where I met her.
I love the town. + I grew up there.
→ I love the town where I grew up.
💡
TIP — who vs which vs that
who = sadece insanlar: the man who helped me
which = nesneler/hayvanlar: the car which broke down
that = her ikisi için de kullanılabilir (daha az resmi)
Pratikte: konuşmada genellikle that kullanılır.

Young Heroes of the Environment

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Around the world, young people who care about the planet are making a real difference. Here are two young people making a big difference.

Boyan Slat (Netherlands): Boyan is the engineer who invented a system to clean the ocean. He was only 18 when he created The Ocean Cleanup — a project which uses ocean currents to collect plastic. The system, which is now working in the Pacific Ocean, has already removed hundreds of tonnes of plastic waste.

Greta Thunberg (Sweden): Greta is the teenager who started a global movement. In August 2018, she began sitting outside the Swedish parliament with a sign — first every school day, then every Friday. It was a protest that made her famous worldwide. She gives speeches which make world leaders listen. At the World Economic Forum in 2019, her message was simple but powerful: "Our house is on fire."

These young people show that even a single person who has a clear vision can change the world. What action can YOU take to protect our planet? Even small things — like recycling, using less plastic, or choosing renewable energy — can make a difference.

4Name 3 small actions individuals can take for the planet.

Recycling / using less plastic / choosing renewable energy.
