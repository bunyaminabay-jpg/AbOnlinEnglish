Sayılabilen ve sayılamayan isimler

Sayılabilen isimlerin tekil ve çoğul hâli vardır: an apple → two apples. Sayılamayan isimler (sıvılar, toz/tanecikli yiyecekler, soyut kavramlar) çoğul yapılmaz ve önlerine a/an gelmez: water, rice, money, advice. Miktar sorarken sayılabilenle How many, sayılamayanla How much kullanılır.

📌 Özet tablo

QuantifierCountable (sayılabilir)Uncountable (sayılamaz)
some (olumlu)some applessome water / some bread
any (soru/olumsuz)any apples?any water?
manymany apples ✅many water ❌
muchmuch apples ❌much water ✅
a lot ofa lot of apples ✅a lot of water ✅

a littlea little apples ❌a little water ✅
⚠️Sayılamaz isimler: water, milk, bread, rice, sugar, coffee, information, advice, money, furniture, weather. | "How much water?" / "How many apples?" | some = olumlu cümle; any = soru ve olumsuz

🔍 Örneklerle

🍎 Sayılabilen (countable)
There are some eggs in the fridge. → Buzdolabında biraz yumurta var.
We don't have many tomatoes. → Çok domatesimiz yok.
How many bottles do we need? → Kaç şişeye ihtiyacımız var?
🥛 Sayılamayan (uncountable)
Is there any milk? → Hiç süt var mı?
I don't drink much coffee. → Çok kahve içmem.
How much sugar do you want? → Ne kadar şeker istersin?
🧂 Birim ile saymak
a bottle of water / two bottles of water → bir şişe su / iki şişe su
a loaf of bread → bir somun ekmek
a piece of advice / information → bir tavsiye / bir bilgi
🚨
IMPORTANT — Teklif ve ricada “some”
Soru cümlesinde normalde any kullanılır, ama bir şey teklif ederken veya isterken some kullanılır:
✅ Would you like some tea?  |  ✅ Can I have some water, please?
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Sayılamayan isim çoğul olmaz
❌ informations, advices, furnitures, moneys
✅ some information, some advice, a lot of furniture, much money
❌ a bread → ✅ a loaf of bread / some bread
💡
TIP — Olumlu cümlede “a lot of” daha doğal
Olumlu cümlelerde much ve many yerine günlük dilde genellikle a lot of kullanılır: She has a lot of friends. We drink a lot of water.

A Night at the Restaurant

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Last Saturday, my family went to a nice Turkish restaurant for my grandmother's birthday. There were some empty tables when we arrived, but within an hour the restaurant was full — there weren't any free tables left!

The waiter brought us some menus and a lot of bread to start. I didn't have much time to choose — the food all looked amazing! My mother asked: "Is there any vegetarian food?" The waiter said: "Yes, we have many vegetarian options."

I ordered a lamb chop for my main course. The waiter asked: "How would you like it?" I said: "Medium, please." My little brother had a lot of trouble deciding — he changed his order three times! He finally ordered a chicken dish.

We had some baklava for dessert — there wasn't much left, but we had just enough for everyone. We asked for the bill. My father said: "Is service included?" It was. We also left a little tip for the excellent service. It was a perfect evening!

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1Were there any free tables left after an hour?

No, there weren't any free tables — the restaurant was full.

2What did the mother ask the waiter?

She asked if there was any vegetarian food.

3How did the writer order their lamb chop?

Medium.

4Was service included in the bill?

Yes, it was. They also left a little tip.
