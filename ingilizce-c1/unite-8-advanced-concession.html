İleri düzey tavizli yapılar

although ve despite'ın daha güçlü ve resmi karşılıkları, akademik yazıda karşı görüşü kabul edip sınırlarını göstermek için kullanılır. Bu yapılarda sıfat ya da fiil öne alınır (Compelling though it is) veya resmi edatlar kullanılır (Notwithstanding, For all). Anlam hep aynıdır: …olmasına rağmen / …e karşın.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
Adj + though + subject + verbAdjective + though + subject + verb, main clauseSıfatı öne çeker — 'although it is + adj' güçlü versiyonuCompelling though the argument IS, it overlooks key evidence. / Important though these issues ARE, they are not the only ones.
Much as + subject + verbMuch as + subject + verb, main clauseLiterary concession — ne kadar ... olsa daMuch as we may wish for rapid change, democratic systems resist it. / Much as I admire his courage...
Notwithstanding + noun/clauseNotwithstanding + noun phrase / the fact that + clauseFormal/legal rağmen — 'despite' resmi versiyonuNotwithstanding the difficulties, the project succeeded. / Notwithstanding the opposition...
Be that as it mayBe that as it may, main clauseHer ne olursa olsun — acknowledging without fully acceptingBe that as it may, we must proceed with the plan. / Concedes the point but moves on.
For all + noun/V-ingFor all + noun phrase / V-ing, main clauseTüm ... a rağmen — 'despite all its + noun'For all its imperfections, democracy remains the best system available. / For all his intelligence, he made a serious error.
⚠️⚠️ 'Compelling though it IS' = 'Although it IS compelling' — adj ÖNCE, 'though' sonra, fiil gerekli. ❌ 'Though compelling it is' YANLIŞ. | 'Notwithstanding' = very formal — legal/academic documents. | 'Be that as it may' = fixed expression — concedes but does not accept the consequence.

🔍 Örneklerle

✨ Sıfat/zarf + though/as
Strange though it may seem, I enjoy exams. → Garip görünebilir ama sınavlardan hoşlanırım.
Try as I might, I couldn't remember her name. → Ne kadar uğraşsam da adını hatırlayamadım.
Tired as she was, she kept working. → Yorgun olmasına rağmen çalışmaya devam etti.
📄 Resmi rağmen yapıları
Notwithstanding these concerns, the law was passed. → Bu kaygılara rağmen yasa kabul edildi.
For all its faults, the city is a wonderful place to live. → Tüm kusurlarına rağmen şehir yaşamak için harika.
Be that as it may, we still need a decision today. → Her ne kadar öyle olsa da bugün bir karar gerekiyor.
💡
TIP — Dengeli paragraf kalıbı
1) Karşı görüşü kabul et: Compelling though this argument is, ... 2) Sınırını göster: it overlooks ... 3) Kendi tezine dön: For all its appeal, therefore, ...

The Crisis of Democratic Institutions

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Compelling though the case for rapid democratic reform may be, the mechanisms of institutional change are notoriously slow — by design. Much as we may wish for swift transformation, democratic systems are intentionally resistant to sudden change. This is, simultaneously, their greatest strength and their most frequently criticised limitation.

Notwithstanding the recent erosion of trust in democratic institutions, surveys consistently show that most citizens in liberal democracies still prefer their system to any available alternative. For all its inefficiencies, the rule of law represents something genuinely worth defending — not because it is perfect, but because the alternatives are measurably worse.

Be that as it may, complacency is not an option. The threats to democratic governance today come not only from authoritarian states or foreign interference, but from within — from elected leaders who exploit legal loopholes to undermine the very institutions they were chosen to lead.

Important though the formal structures of democracy undoubtedly are, they are insufficient without an engaged citizenry. Admirable though the constitution of any democracy may be, it is only as strong as the collective will to defend it.

Notwithstanding the pessimism that characterises much contemporary political commentary, the historical record suggests grounds for cautious optimism. Democracies have faced crises before and, more often than not, have emerged with their core institutions strengthened rather than weakened. Much as the current moment may feel uniquely precarious, we should be careful not to confuse difficulty with inevitability.

ℹ️ Görüş yazısı.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1Why are democratic systems 'resistant to sudden change' — and what is the paradox?

It is by design — their greatest strength (stability, rule of law) is also their most criticised limitation (slowness to reform).

2What does the text say is the source of the greatest current threat to democracy?

Threats from within — elected leaders who exploit legal loopholes to undermine the institutions they lead.

3Find 3 advanced concessive structures and write each one out fully.

e.g. 'Compelling though the case for rapid reform may be...' / 'Much as we may wish for swift transformation...' / 'Notwithstanding the recent erosion of trust...' / 'For all its inefficiencies...' / 'Be that as it may'

4What reason for optimism does the text give despite acknowledging the current crisis?

The historical record shows democracies have faced crises before and often emerged with institutions strengthened, not weakened.

Notwithstanding the very real failures of current democratic systems, they remain the most accountable form of governance ever devised. For all its inefficiencies, democracy protects fundamental rights that authoritarian alternatives do not.
