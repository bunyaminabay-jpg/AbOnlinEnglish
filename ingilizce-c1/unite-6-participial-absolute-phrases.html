Sıfat-fiil (participle) ve mutlak (absolute) yapılar

Participle yapıları, iki cümleyi bağlaç kullanmadan birleştirip metni daha akıcı ve yoğun hâle getirir: She opened the letter and read it slowly. → Opening the letter, she read it slowly. V-ing etken, V3 edilgen, Having + V3 önce tamamlanmış eylem anlamı verir. Kural: yapının öznesi, ana cümlenin öznesiyle aynı olmalıdır. Farklı bir özne gerekiyorsa absolute phrase kullanılır.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
Active participial V-ingV-ing phrase at start/end = same subject as main clauseAynı özne + eş zamanlı veya hemen önceki eylemWalking through the forest, SHE heard a sound. (= she was walking AND heard) / He left, slamming the door.
Passive participial V3Past participle phrase = passive meaning, same subjectAynı özne + pasif anlam (edilgen)Found guilty, HE was sentenced to five years. / Exhausted by the journey, SHE fell asleep immediately.
Perfect participial Having + V3Having + V3 = completed BEFORE main verbÖnce yapılan eylem — aynı özneHaving finished the report, SHE went home. / Having studied abroad, HE returned with new perspectives.
Absolute phrase With + N + V-ingWith + noun + V-ing/V3 = accompanying circumstance (DIFFERENT subject possible)Farklı özne mümkün — eşlik eden durumWith the sun setting, we headed back. / With her eyes closed, she listened carefully.
⚠️⚠️ Participial phrase'de özne ANA CÜMLE ile AYNI olmalı! ❌ 'Walking through the park, a dog barked.' (dog yürümedi!) ✅ 'Walking through the park, I heard a dog bark.' | 'Having + V3' = önceden tamamlanan. | 'With + noun + V-ing' = farklı özne olabilir.

🔍 Örneklerle

🏃 V-ing (etken, eş zamanlı)
Looking out of the window, I saw the storm coming. → Pencereden bakarken fırtınanın geldiğini gördüm.
She left the room, closing the door quietly. → Kapıyı sessizce kapatarak odadan çıktı.
📦 V3 (edilgen) / Having V3 (önce)
Built in 1890, the bridge is still in use. → 1890'da inşa edilen köprü hâlâ kullanılıyor.
Having finished the exam, he went straight home. → Sınavı bitirdikten sonra doğruca eve gitti.
Having been warned, we took an umbrella. → Uyarıldığımız için şemsiye aldık.
🌅 Absolute phrase (farklı özne)
The work done, we went for a walk. → İş bitince yürüyüşe çıktık.
With prices rising, many families are cutting back. → Fiyatlar artarken birçok aile harcamalarını kısıyor.
Weather permitting, the match will start at five. → Hava izin verirse maç beşte başlayacak.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — “Sarkan” participle (dangling participle)
❌ Having finished dinner, the dishes were washed. (bulaşıklar yemek yemedi!)
✅ Having finished dinner, we washed the dishes.
❌ Walking home, it started to rain. → ✅ As I was walking home, it started to rain.
💡
TIP — Olumsuz participle
not participle'ın önüne gelir: Not knowing what to say, she stayed silent. / Not having a car, I took the bus.

The Return of the Wild

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Driven to extinction in Britain for centuries, the wolf has not roamed freely in the Scottish Highlands for hundreds of years. Yet rewilding movements — having gained remarkable momentum across Europe in recent decades — are now challenging this absence in ways that were unimaginable just twenty years ago.

Having studied the ecological effects of the wolves reintroduced to Yellowstone National Park in 1995–96, researchers described one of the most astonishing chain reactions in conservation history: what ecologists call a "trophic cascade." With elk populations controlled by predation, overgrazing declined in some areas. With willows and aspens returning along some riverbanks, birds and beavers came back. However, other ecologists who have examined the same data argue that the size of this effect has been overstated: bears, human hunters and drought also changed the ecosystem.

Faced with this evidence, conservation bodies across Europe are reconsidering assumptions that have remained unchallenged for generations. The debate is no longer purely ecological — it is deeply cultural, political, and philosophical.

Walking through a genuinely rewilded landscape, visitors describe something they struggle to articulate — an encounter with what the Romantics called the sublime: a beauty so vast and indifferent that it is almost frightening. Sensing something ancient and unhurried, many report feeling the weight of their ordinary concerns dissolve.

Having lost so much of the world's wildness in barely two centuries — driven by the combined forces of agriculture, industry, and urban expansion — we may, at last, be beginning to understand what we have lost. And having understood, perhaps, what we must do next.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What is a 'trophic cascade'? Give the example from the text.

A chain reaction through an ecosystem caused by a top predator: in Yellowstone, wolves reduced elk grazing, and willows, aspens, birds and beavers returned in some areas — though the size of the effect is debated.

2Find a past participial phrase (V3) at the beginning of a sentence.

'Driven to extinction in Britain...' / 'Faced with this evidence...' — past participial, passive meaning, same subject as main clause.

3Find a 'Having + V3' structure and explain what happened earlier.

'Having studied the ecological effects...' — they studied first, THEN documented the results.

4Find an absolute phrase with 'With + noun + V3' and explain its function.

'With elk populations controlled by predation...' — it gives the circumstance/cause for the next event.
