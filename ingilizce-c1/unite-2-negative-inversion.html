Negative Inversion — Devrik Cümleler

Olumsuz veya sınırlayıcı bir ifade cümle başına geldiğinde, yardımcı fiil öznenin önüne geçer. Bu yapı güçlü vurgu sağlar ve C1 düzeyi yazılı ve sözlü İngilizcede akademik ağırlık katar.

📌 Inversion tetikleyicileri

İfadeInversion yapısıÖrnek
NeverNever + aux + S + VNever have I witnessed such chaos.
Rarely / SeldomRarely + aux + S + VRarely does a single factor explain everything.
Not onlyNot only + aux + S + VNot only did they fail, but they also lied.
No soonerNo sooner + aux + S + V + thanNo sooner had she left than it rained.
Hardly / ScarcelyHardly + aux + S + V + whenHardly had he sat down when she arrived.
LittleLittle + aux + S + VLittle did he know what lay ahead.
On no accountOn no account + aux + S + VOn no account should you disclose this.
🚨
IMPORTANT — No sooner → than / Hardly → when
No sooner kullandığında → than ile biter:
"No sooner had I arrived than the phone rang."

Hardly/Scarcely kullandığında → when ile biter:
"Hardly had she sat down when the alarm went off."
❌ No sooner had I arrived when...

Who Controls the Story?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Little did early internet pioneers know that the medium they were building with such idealism would become the 21st century's primary battlefield for truth. They believed they were creating the world's greatest library. Seldom in history has a technology been so rapidly repurposed for ends its creators never imagined.

No sooner had social media platforms achieved global dominance than their capacity for industrial-scale disinformation became frighteningly apparent. Not only did they amplify false and inflammatory content, but their algorithms actively and measurably rewarded it with greater reach.

Rarely has a technology so fundamentally reshaped the epistemological landscape of public life in such a short period. Under no circumstances should we accept, as some now seem tempted to, a world in which truth is merely a matter of opinion — where facts and fiction carry equal weight because both generate engagement.

Never before had the power to publish at scale been so widely distributed. This was supposed to democratise information. The outcome has been more complicated: a world where everyone can speak, but seldom has it been harder to know what is true.

Not only is this a philosophical problem, but it represents a direct and measurable threat to democratic governance, public health, and social cohesion. Little do most people realise how thoroughly the information environment around them has been engineered to capture attention rather than to inform.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What did internet pioneers not know when they were building the internet?

That it would become the 21st century's primary battlefield for truth.

2What happened as soon as social media achieved global dominance?

Its capacity for industrial-scale disinformation became apparent — algorithms rewarded inflammatory content.

3Find 'No sooner...than' in the text. What two events does it connect?

No sooner had social media achieved dominance THAN its capacity for disinformation became apparent.

4Find 'Not only...but also' in the text and write it out in full.

'Not only did they amplify false content, but their algorithms actively rewarded it.' — also 'Not only is this a philosophical problem, but it represents a direct threat...'
