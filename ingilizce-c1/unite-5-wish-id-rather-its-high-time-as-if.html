Gerçek dışı geçmiş zaman: wish, I'd rather, it's time, as if

Bu yapıların ortak noktası, gerçek olmayan ya da henüz gerçekleşmemiş bir durumdan bahsederken zamanı bir adım geriye kaydırmalarıdır. Şimdiki bir durum için Past Simple, geçmişteki bir durum için Past Perfect kullanılır. Yani buradaki geçmiş zaman, zamanı değil gerçek dışılığı gösterir.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
I'd rather + subject + PSI'd rather + subject + Past Simple (not present/infinitive)Birinin bir şey yapmasını tercih etmeI'd rather you came tomorrow. / I'd rather she didn't know about this yet.
It's high time / It's about timeIt's high time + subject + Past SimpleGecikmeli zorunluluk — ŞIMDI'yi kasteder ama Past SimpleIt's high time we addressed this. / It's about time he took responsibility.
as if / as though + PSas if/as though + Past Simple = contrary to present factŞimdiki gerçeğe uymayan benzetmeHe talks as if he were the only expert. / She acts as though she owned the place.
as if / as though + PPas if/as though + Past Perfect = contrary to past factGeçmişteki gerçeğe uymayan benzetmeShe looked as if she had seen a ghost. / He spoke as though he had prepared for years.
wish + would (annoyance)I wish + subject + would/wouldn't + V1Birinin tekrarlayan davranışına sinirlilik veya değişim isteğiI wish he would stop interrupting me. / I wish you wouldn't do that.
⚠️⚠️ 'I'd rather + PS' (başkası için): 'I'd rather you CAME tomorrow.' (NOT 'come') | 'It's high time' + PS ama ŞIMDIKI anlamda: 'It's high time she LEFT.' = she should leave now. | 'as if he were' (resmi; günlük dilde 'was' da kullanılır). | I wish + PP (geçmiş pişmanlık) ≠ I wish + PS (şimdiki hayali dilek).

🔍 Örneklerle

🌠 wish / if only
I wish I had more free time. → Keşke daha fazla boş vaktim olsa. (şimdi)
If only I hadn't said that! → Keşke bunu söylemeseydim! (geçmiş)
I wish you would stop shouting. → Keşke bağırmayı bıraksan. (rahatsızlık)
🙏 would rather / it's time
I'd rather stay at home tonight. → Bu akşam evde kalmayı tercih ederim. (aynı özne → V1)
I'd rather you didn't tell anyone. → Kimseye söylememeni tercih ederim. (farklı özne → V2)
It's high time we left. → Artık gitme vaktimiz geldi de geçiyor.
🎭 as if / as though
He talks as if he knew everything. → Her şeyi biliyormuş gibi konuşuyor. (bilmiyor)
You look as if you've seen a ghost. → Hayalet görmüş gibisin. (olabilir → gerçek zaman)
She acted as though nothing had happened. → Hiçbir şey olmamış gibi davrandı.
🚨
IMPORTANT — was mı were mı?
I wish I were taller / as if he were the boss — resmi ve sınav İngilizcesinde were tercih edilir; günlük konuşmada was da yaygındır ve yanlış sayılmaz. Ancak If I were you kalıbı sabittir.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — wish + would kendin için
❌ I wish I would be rich. → ✅ I wish I were rich.
wish + would başkasının davranışından şikâyet için kullanılır: ✅ I wish he would listen.

📝 Kendine sor — pratik yap

I wish I ___ (live) closer to the sea. lived
I'd rather you ___ (not / smoke) in the car. didn't smoke
It's high time you ___ (get) a new phone. got
She looked as if she ___ (not / sleep) for days. hadn't slept

Can We Be Held Responsible?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Philosophers have been arguing about free will for millennia, yet the debate feels no closer to resolution. It is as though they had been walking in circles for centuries without noticing.

Hard determinists argue that every thought, decision, and action is the inevitable product of prior physical causes — genes, neural architecture, upbringing, circumstance. If this were true, it would be as though we had never genuinely chosen anything at all. We would be, in effect, extraordinarily complex physical systems — nothing more.

It's high time philosophy engaged more seriously with what neuroscience has revealed: that, in some experiments, brain activity can predict a simple decision several seconds — in a 2008 study, up to about ten seconds — before we become consciously aware of "making" it. The implications are profound — and deeply unsettling.

Compatibilists would rather we reframed the question entirely. They argue that even if determinism is true, we can still meaningfully speak of free will — not as freedom from causation, but as freedom from coercion and mental illness. I wish this were simpler. But as if to confirm the complexity, every apparent answer generates five new questions.

It's about time we acknowledged that these questions matter practically, not merely academically. Criminal justice systems operate as though individuals were fully autonomous agents capable of genuine choice. Sentencing decisions are made as if the concept of moral responsibility were philosophically unproblematic. I wish policymakers would engage with the philosophy more seriously. It would make for both better justice and better policy.

ℹ️ Gerçek olay ve araştırmalara dayanır; ders amacıyla sadeleştirilmiştir.

📚 Kaynaklar

  • Soon vd. (2008), Nature Neuroscience — Unconscious determinants of free decisions

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What do hard determinists argue about decisions and actions?

That they are the inevitable products of prior physical causes — genes, neural architecture, upbringing, and circumstance.

2What have some neuroscience experiments shown about decision-making?

That brain activity can predict a simple decision several seconds (in a 2008 study, up to about ten seconds) before people are aware of making it.

3How do compatibilists reframe the free will debate?

They say free will doesn't mean freedom from causation, but freedom from coercion and mental illness.

4Find one 'as if / as though' with Past Simple and one with Past Perfect in the text. Explain what each expresses.

'as though individuals were fully autonomous agents' (Past Simple — imagined present situation) / 'as though they had been walking in circles' (Past Perfect Continuous — imagined past situation)
