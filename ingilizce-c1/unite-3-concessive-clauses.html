Tavizli (concessive) yapılar

Concessive yapılar bir karşı görüşü ya da engeli kabul edip yine de ana fikri savunmaya yarar: Ne kadar zor olursa olsun, ... / Kabul etmek gerekir ki ..., ancak ... C1 düzeyinde although / despite dışına çıkıp however + sıfat, whatever, much as, granted (that) gibi yapıları kullanmak, yazıya denge ve olgunluk katar.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
However + adj/advHowever + adjective/adverb + subject + verbNe kadar...olursa olsun — NO COMMA before 'however' here!However difficult the question may be, we must engage with it. / However confidently she speaks...
Whatever/Whoever/Wherever/WheneverWhatever + subject + verb / Whatever + nounNe/kim/nerede/ne zaman...olursa olsunWhatever she decides, I support her. / Whatever the outcome, we must accept it.
Much as + subject + verbMuch as + subject + verb, main clauseNe kadar...olsa da — literary, formal concessionMuch as I admire her commitment, I disagree with her methods.
Granted (that)Granted (that) + clause, main clauseKabul ederek ama reddederek devam etmeGranted that the evidence is limited, we should still take the risk seriously.
Supposing / Assuming (that)Supposing + Past Simple / that + clauseHayali durumu varsaymak — 'if' alternatifiSupposing you were right — what would that imply? / Assuming he refuses, what then?
⚠️⚠️ 'However' = 'No matter how' (ne kadar olursa): 'However hard it is' = 'No matter how hard it is.' Bu farklı 'however' (=bununla birlikte)! | 'Much as' = literary/formal, sınav İngilizcesi. | 'Whoever' = 'any person who' (relative) veya 'no matter who' (concessive).

🔍 Örneklerle

💪 However / No matter how
However hard she tried, she couldn't open it. → Ne kadar uğraştıysa da açamadı.
No matter how late it is, call me. → Saat kaç olursa olsun beni ara.
❓ Whatever / Whoever / Wherever
Whatever you decide, I'll support you. → Ne karar verirsen ver, seni desteklerim.
Wherever you go, take a map. → Nereye gidersen git, harita al.
Whoever calls, tell them I'm busy. → Kim ararsa arasın, meşgul olduğumu söyle.
🤝 Much as / Granted / Admittedly
Much as I like him, I can't agree. → Onu ne kadar sevsem de katılamam.
Granted, the plan is risky, but it's our best option. → Kabul, plan riskli; ama en iyi seçeneğimiz.
Admittedly, the data is incomplete. → Kabul etmek gerekir ki veriler eksik.
🚨
IMPORTANT — İki farklı “however”
However, (virgüllü) = Bununla birlikte: It was cheap. However, it broke quickly.
However + sıfat/zarf (virgülsüz) = Ne kadar ... olursa olsun: However cheap it is, I won't buy it.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Despite ile cümle
❌ Despite it was raining, we went out.
✅ Despite the rain / Despite the fact that it was raining / Although it was raining, we went out.

The Ship of Theseus and Personal Identity

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

The Ship of Theseus is one of philosophy's oldest puzzles: if every single plank of a ship is gradually replaced, is it still the same ship? Whatever your intuitive answer, the question has profound implications for our understanding of personal identity and consciousness.

Granted that many of the cells in our bodies are replaced over the years, are you genuinely the same person you were a decade ago? However confidently you might assert continuous identity across time, neuroscience increasingly challenges this certainty.

Much as we are attached to the idea of a fixed, stable, singular self that persists through time, accumulating evidence suggests that the self is better understood as a narrative construction — a story we tell about ourselves, moment by moment, to create the illusion of continuity. Wherever we go and whatever we experience, we carry this narrative with us, constantly revising it.

Supposing memory were entirely erased — every childhood experience, every relationship, every skill — would the surviving body be the same person? Most people intuitively say no. But whoever decides this question must grapple with what we mean by "same."

Even if we accept the continuity of physical matter, the philosophical puzzle remains. Much as we might prefer a neat answer, consciousness — its nature, its origins, its relationship to physical processes — remains what philosopher David Chalmers called "the hard problem." However sophisticated our neuroscience becomes, explaining why there is subjective experience at all is a question of a different order entirely.

3What is Chalmers' 'hard problem' of consciousness?

Why is there subjective experience at all? Explaining why physical processes give rise to inner experience.

4Find one example of each: 'however + adj', 'much as', 'whatever', 'granted that'.
