The Subjunctive — Dilek-Şart Kipi

C1'de I wish / If only / It's high time yapılarını görmüştün. C2 seviyesinde subjunctive, çok daha geniş ve resmi bir sisteme yayılır: mandative subjunctive (zorunluluk bildiren fiillerden sonra) ve formulaic subjunctive (kalıplaşmış, edebi/resmi ifadeler). Bu yapıların ortak özelliği: fiil, özneden bağımsız olarak her zaman yalın hâlde (base form) kalır — 3. tekil şahısta bile "-s" almaz.

📌 Mandative subjunctive — zorunluluk bildiren fiil/sıfatlardan sonra

TetikleyiciYapıÖrnek
essential / vital / imperativeIt is + adj + that + S + base verbIt is essential that every voice be heard.
recommend / insist / demand / proposeS + verb + that + S + base verbThe committee insisted that the report be revised.
suggest / require...that + S + (not) + base verbHistorians suggest that the archive not be destroyed.

📌 Formulaic (kalıplaşmış) subjunctive

Edebi / resmi kalıplar
Suffice it to say that the truth was far more complicated.
Be that as it may, the decision still stands.
Come what may, the archive will be preserved.
Dilek / tören ifadeleri
So be it — we accept the consequences.
Long live the memory of those who resisted.
If truth be told, the record was never neutral.
💡
TIP — Neden "-s" yok?
Subjunctive fiil her zaman base formdır, özne ne olursa olsun:
"It is vital that he attend" (attends DEĞİL)
"It is vital that she be present" (is DEĞİL)
Bu, C2 sınavlarında ve akademik/hukuki metinlerde çok sık görülen, ama günlük konuşmada nadir kullanılan çok resmi bir yapıdır.

Whose History Is It?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Every nation, be it ancient or newly formed, constructs a version of its own past that it can live with. Historians increasingly insist that this process be examined as carefully as the events it claims to record — for collective memory is rarely a neutral archive. It is, more often, a contested narrative, shaped as much by what a society chooses to forget as by what it remembers.

It is essential that this distinction be understood: history is what happened; collective memory is what a society has decided is worth enshrining. Monuments are raised, anniversaries observed, textbooks written — and in each of these acts, a quiet argument is made about which version of events deserves to survive. Where a nation's past includes episodes of violence or injustice, officials frequently propose that the record not dwell on them, preferring instead a narrative of unbroken progress.

Suffice it to say that this selective amnesia rarely serves the descendants of those who suffered. Truth commissions in dozens of countries have demanded that painful chapters be reckoned with rather than whitewashed, arguing that reconciliation without honesty is merely amnesia dressed up as healing. Be that as it may, the political appetite for such reckoning is often thin; comfortable myths, once deeply ingrained, are not easily dislodged.

Come what may in the decades ahead, the argument over whose history gets told — and whose gets quietly edited out — will remain one of the most consequential a society can have with itself. If truth be told, a nation that cannot reckon honestly with its past is unlikely to navigate its future with any greater clarity.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1According to the text, what is the difference between history and collective memory?

History is what happened; collective memory is what a society has chosen to enshrine and remember, which is often selective.

2Why do truth commissions insist that painful chapters be reckoned with rather than whitewashed?

Because they argue reconciliation without honesty is simply amnesia disguised as healing.

3Find 3 examples of the mandative subjunctive (after essential/insist/propose/demand) and 2 formulaic subjunctive expressions in the text.

Mandative: \"that this process be examined\", \"that this distinction be understood\", \"that the record not dwell\", \"that painful chapters be reckoned with\". Formulaic: \"Suffice it to say\", \"Be that as it may\", \"Come what may\", \"If truth be told\".
