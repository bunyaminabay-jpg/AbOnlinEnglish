Discourse Markers & Advanced Linkers

B2 düzeyinde yazma ve konuşmada metni organize etmek için güçlü bağlaçlar ve discourse marker'lar kullanılır. Bunlar fikirleri mantıklı şekilde birbirine bağlar, yazıyı akıcı ve akademik yapar.

🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — despite vs although
despite / in spite of + noun/gerund (-ing):
✅ Despite being tired, she finished the work.
✅ In spite of the cost, they proceeded.

although / even though + subject + verb:
✅ Although she was tired, she finished the work.
❌ Despite she was tired...
💡
TIP — however'ın yeri
however cümle başında, ortasında veya sonunda kullanılabilir:
However, the results were poor.
The results, however, were poor.
The results were poor, however.
Virgülle ayrılır — "but" ile karıştırma! (But cümle ortasında bağlaç olarak kullanılır)

Is Artificial Intelligence Art?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

In 2022, an image created with the AI program Midjourney — Théâtre D'opéra Spatial by Jason M. Allen — won first prize in the digital arts category of the Colorado State Fair's art competition. Consequently, questions about creativity, authorship, and the nature of art intensified dramatically.

AI can produce visually stunning images in seconds. Nevertheless, many artists and philosophers argue that it fundamentally lacks the human intention, lived experience, and emotional depth that define genuine artistic expression. Furthermore, the economic impact on working artists is significant: their styles, training data, and creative output are used without consent or compensation.

In contrast, defenders of AI art point out that tools have always transformed art. The camera, when it first appeared, was dismissed as a threat to painting. Whereas earlier critics predicted it would destroy art, the camera actually liberated painting from the obligation of pure representation, giving rise to Impressionism and beyond.

Despite this historical precedent, the current situation feels qualitatively different in scale and speed. Whereas a photographer still makes countless deliberate choices — lighting, angle, timing, framing — an AI produces outputs based on statistical patterns in training data. Admittedly, the boundary between human creativity and pattern recognition is blurring.

To a certain extent, it could be argued that all art involves absorbing and recombining patterns learned from others. Nevertheless, the question of whether a machine can truly be said to create — rather than merely generate — remains genuinely open.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What happened at the Colorado State Fair in 2022?

An AI-generated image won first prize at the art competition.

2What historical comparison does the text make?

The camera was also once dismissed as a threat to art, but it actually liberated painting.

3Find one 'contrast' marker, one 'concession' marker, and one 'addition' marker in the text.

Contrast: 'Nevertheless' / 'Whereas' / Concession: 'Admittedly' / 'Despite' / Addition: 'Furthermore'

4What open question does the text end with?

Whether a machine can truly 'create' rather than merely 'generate.'

1Discourse marker egzersizi

Write a paragraph about art or creativity (traditional vs digital, human vs AI, etc.) using at least 6 different discourse markers.
0 kelime
Traditional art requires physical skill and years of practice, whereas digital art demands technical knowledge and software fluency. Nevertheless, both forms are capable of producing deeply emotional and technically brilliant work. Furthermore, the boundary between the two has become increasingly blurred in recent years. Despite this convergence, many established galleries continue to distinguish between them.

2Karşılaştırma

Compare two types of art (e.g. photography and painting, music and visual art, street art and gallery art). Use: whereas, nevertheless, furthermore, however, despite.
0 kelime
Photography and painting are both powerful forms of visual art with distinct demands. Whereas painting requires manual technique developed over years, photography relies on an acute sense of timing and compositional instinct. Nevertheless, both mediums can capture human emotion with extraordinary power. Furthermore, many contemporary artists deliberately combine the two. Despite their surface differences, they share the same fundamental ambition: to make the viewer feel something meaningful.
