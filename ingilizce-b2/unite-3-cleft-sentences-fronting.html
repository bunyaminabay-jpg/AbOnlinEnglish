Vurgu için cleft cümleler ve öne alma (fronting)

Türkçe'de vurgulamak istediğimiz öğeyi yüklemin hemen önüne koyarız. İngilizce'de sözcük sırası daha sabittir; bu yüzden vurgu için cümle ikiye bölünür (cleft = yarılmış): It was Maria who called. veya What I need is a break. Olumsuz ya da kısıtlayıcı bir ifade cümle başına alınınca ise soru düzeni (inversion) kullanılır.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
It-cleftIt + was/is + FOCUS + that/who + restBelirli bir unsuru vurgular — kim/ne üzerindeIt was Steve Jobs WHO changed everything. / It was the internet THAT transformed communication.
Pseudo-cleft (What-cleft)What + clause + is/was + FOCUSSonucu, ihtiyacı, sürprizi vurgularWhat surprised everyone WAS the speed. / What we need IS more funding.
Inversion (negative fronting)Never/Rarely/Not until + auxiliary + subject + verbDramatik vurgu — negatif ifade öne çıkarNever before HAD technology advanced so fast. / Not until 2007 DID the smartphone take off.
Only/Seldom/HardlyOnly when / Seldom / Hardly ever + inversionKısıtlama/nadiren vurgusuOnly when I saw the data DID I believe it. / Seldom HAS such progress been made.
⚠️⚠️ Inversion: auxiliary + subject + verb (soru sırası). ❌ 'Never I have seen' ✅ 'Never have I seen' | What-cleft: 'What matters IS...' (singular verb!). | It-cleft + zamir: günlük dilde 'It was them who...', resmi yazıda 'It was they who...'.

🔍 Örneklerle

🎯 It-cleft
It was the price that surprised me, not the design. → Beni şaşırtan tasarım değil, fiyattı.
It is in the evening that I study best. → En iyi akşamları çalışırım.
It wasn't until I moved abroad that I really learned English. → İngilizceyi ancak yurt dışına taşınınca gerçekten öğrendim.
💬 What-cleft (pseudo-cleft)
What I love about this city is the food. → Bu şehirde sevdiğim şey yemekleri.
What happened was (that) the system crashed. → Olan şu: sistem çöktü.
All I want is a quiet weekend. → Tek istediğim sakin bir hafta sonu.
🔄 Negatif öne alma + inversion
Never have I seen such a crowd. → Hiç böyle bir kalabalık görmedim.
Only then did we realise the danger. → Tehlikeyi ancak o zaman fark ettik.
Not only is it cheap, but it's also fast. → Sadece ucuz değil, aynı zamanda hızlı.
🚨
IMPORTANT — Zamir ile it-cleft
Günlük dilde It was me who called. denir; çok resmi yazıda It was I who called. tercih edilebilir. Fiil, vurgulanan kişiye uyar: It is I who am responsible (resmi) / It's me who's responsible (günlük).
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Inversion unutmak
❌ Only then we realised the danger. → ✅ Only then did we realise the danger.
❌ What I need are a holiday. → ✅ What I need is a holiday.
💡
TIP — Konuşmada “What-cleft”
Sunum ve tartışmalarda dikkat çekmek için çok kullanışlıdır: What I'd like to talk about today is... / What we need to remember is...

📝 Kendine sor — pratik yap

It was my sister ___ told me the news. who / that
What I need ___ a long holiday. is
Never ___ I heard such a beautiful song. have

The Smartphone Revolution

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

It was not until 2007 that the smartphone transformed from a niche device into the central object of modern life. Before the iPhone, mobile phones existed — but it was Apple who changed everything.

What Jobs understood was not simply that people needed a phone. What he grasped was something far more profound: that people needed a computer in their pocket. It was this insight that created a trillion-dollar industry.

Never before had a single consumer product disrupted so many industries simultaneously — music, photography, navigation, banking, communication. Not until companies began losing billions did many traditional industries realise the scale of the threat.

Only when we examine the speed of this change do we appreciate how remarkable it was. In 2005, relatively few people owned a smartphone. By 2015, well over a billion people did. What is striking is not merely the technology — it is the speed at which human behaviour adapted.

Seldom has a technology been adopted so rapidly across so many different cultures, income levels, and age groups simultaneously. It was not the hardware that made the iPhone revolutionary — it was the App Store that turned the phone into a platform.

ℹ️ Gerçek olay ve araştırmalara dayanır; ders amacıyla sadeleştirilmiştir.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1According to the text, what was Jobs' key insight?

That people needed a computer in their pocket — not just a phone.

2What industries did smartphones disrupt according to the text?

Music, photography, navigation, banking, and communication.

3Find one It-cleft and one What-cleft in the text.

It-cleft: 'It was Apple who changed everything.' / What-cleft: 'What Jobs understood was...' or 'What is striking is...'

4Find one example of inversion in the text and explain why it is used.

e.g. 'Never before had a single consumer product disrupted...' — for dramatic emphasis on how unprecedented this was.
