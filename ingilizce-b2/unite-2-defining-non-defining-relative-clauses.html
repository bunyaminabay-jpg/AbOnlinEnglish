Relative Clauses — Defining & Non-defining

B2 düzeyinde relative clauses iki türe ayrılır: defining (tanımlayan — virgülsüz) ve non-defining (ek bilgi veren — virgüllü). Bu ayrım hem anlam hem de noktalama açısından kritiktir.

📌 Defining vs Non-defining

TürKuralÖrnekAnlam
DefiningVirgül yok · that kullanılabilirThe man who called was rude.Hangi adam? — arayan adam
Non-definingVirgüllü · that KULLANILMAZMy father, who is 60, loves jazz.Ek bilgi — babam zaten bilinen biri

⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Non-defining'de that yasak!
❌ My sister, that lives in London, is a chef.
✅ My sister, who lives in London, is a chef.

❌ The Eiffel Tower, that was built in 1889, is in Paris.
✅ The Eiffel Tower, which was built in 1889, is in Paris.
💡
TIP — Preposition + which/whom
Resmi yazıda preposition relative pronoun'dan önce gelir:
Informal: The company (that) I work for is expanding.
Formal: The company for which I work is expanding.

Informal: The person (who) I spoke to was helpful.
Formal: The person to whom I spoke was helpful.

The Right to Be Forgotten

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

In May 2014, the Court of Justice of the European Union made a landmark ruling which changed internet privacy law forever. Mario Costeja González, whose name appeared in old newspaper articles that linked him to debt problems, had requested that Google remove the search links.

The court ruled that individuals who believe their outdated data harms their privacy have the right to request its removal. Companies that fail to comply may face significant fines.

This ruling, which only applies within the EU, has been deeply controversial. Journalists, whose work depends on public access to historical information, argued it threatened press freedom. Historians expressed concern about a world where inconvenient facts could simply be deleted.

A system whereby anyone could request the erasure of legally published information was described by critics as "digital amnesia." The company whose search results triggered the original case — Google — lost.

The debate continues: the right to privacy versus the right to information — two fundamental values that cannot always coexist. The year when this ruling was made marked a turning point in how the internet, which had long operated as an unlimited archive, was forced to face questions of accountability.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1Who was Mario Costeja González and what did he request?

A Spanish man whose name appeared in old debt-related articles — he requested Google remove the links.

2What right did the court rule that individuals have?

The right to request removal of outdated data that harms their privacy.

3Why were journalists and historians concerned?

They feared it threatened press freedom and could allow inconvenient historical facts to be deleted.

4Find one defining and one non-defining relative clause in the text. Explain the difference.

Defining: 'individuals who believe...' (no comma, defines which individuals) / Non-defining: 'this ruling, which only applies within the EU,' (comma, extra info about the ruling)
