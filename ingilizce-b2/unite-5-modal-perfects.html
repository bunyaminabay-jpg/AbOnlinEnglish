Modal Perfects — Geçmişteki Yorumlar

Modal perfects, geçmişte olan (veya olmayan) eylemler hakkında tahmin, pişmanlık, eleştiri veya imkânsızlık ifade eder. Yapı: modal + have + V3

📌 Tüm Modal Perfects

YapıAnlamÖrnek
must have + V3kesin tahmin (geçmiş)She's not here. She must have left early.
can't have + V3imkânsız (geçmiş)He can't have stolen it — he wasn't there.
might/could have + V3belirsiz ihtimalShe might have forgotten about the meeting.
should have + V3yapılması gerekendi ama yapılmadıYou should have called me! (aramadın)
shouldn't have + V3yapılmaması gerekendi ama yapıldıI shouldn't have eaten so much. (yedim)
could have + V3yapabilirdi ama yapmadıShe could have helped but she didn't.
needn't have + V3gerekmiyordu ama yapıldıYou needn't have bought flowers! (ama aldın)
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — must have vs should have
must have → tahmin (sen karar veriyorsun):
"She must have been tired." (Çok yorgun olmalı — bence kesin)

should have → pişmanlık/eleştiri (yapılmadı):
"She should have rested." (Dinlenseydi ya — ama dinlenmedi)

needn't have → gereksiz eylem (yapıldı ama gerekmiyordu):
"You needn't have cooked — we ordered pizza." (Pişirdin ama gerek yoktu)

The 2008 Financial Crisis — What Went Wrong?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

The 2008 global financial crisis devastated economies worldwide. Millions lost their homes, jobs, and savings. In 2011, the official US inquiry concluded that the crisis was avoidable. In other words, it should not have happened — at least not at this scale.

Financial regulators should have acted much sooner. Warning signs had been visible for years. Banks ought to have been required to hold much larger capital reserves as insurance. Rating agencies should have scrutinised the toxic mortgage-backed securities far more rigorously. The fact that they must have known about the risks — given the data available to them — makes their failure particularly troubling.

Governments could have intervened earlier. Some economists, such as John Taylor, argue that the Federal Reserve might have cooled the housing bubble if it had not kept interest rates so low in 2002–2004. Others believe even different policies could not have avoided a crash completely.

The economic consequences of the crisis fell hardest on those with the least. The wealth gap, already large in 2007, widened dramatically. Working-class families should never have been the ones bearing the burden of a crisis they did not create.

We should have learned from the Great Depression of the 1930s. But it seems we hadn't. The same patterns of deregulation, unchecked speculation, and short-term thinking that caused problems in the 1930s caused them again, seventy years later.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What did the official US inquiry conclude in 2011?

That the crisis was avoidable.

2What do some economists, such as John Taylor, argue about the Federal Reserve?

That it might have cooled the housing bubble if it had not kept interest rates so low in 2002–2004.

3Who bore the heaviest consequences of the crisis?

Working-class families — those with the least, who did not create the crisis.

4Find 3 modal perfects in the text and explain what each expresses (criticism / missed opportunity / certain past deduction / etc.).

1Modal perfects

Write 4 sentences about a past event or historical moment using: 1-) should have (criticism) 2-) could have (missed opportunity) 3-) might have (uncertain past) 4-) must have (certain deduction)

The 2008 financial crisis should have been prevented — the warning signs were clearly visible for years. Regulators could have intervened much earlier and should have done so. The Federal Reserve might have raised interest rates more aggressively in 2005 to cool the housing bubble. Senior politicians must have known about the systemic risks but chose to ignore them for political reasons.

2Tarihsel hata

Choose a historical mistake (financial crisis, political failure, environmental disaster). Write 4–5 sentences about what should/could have been done differently.
0 kelime
During the 2008 financial crisis, governments around the world should have acted far more decisively and far sooner. Major banks could have been regulated much more strictly in the years preceding the collapse. Millions of ordinary homeowners might have kept their properties if more protective policies had been in place. World leaders must have understood the severity of the situation — they simply lacked the courage to act.
