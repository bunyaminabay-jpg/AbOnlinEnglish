Gerund mı, infinitive mi? Anlamı değiştiren fiiller

B2 seviyesinde asıl zorluk, hem -ing hem to + V1 alabilen ama anlamı değişen fiillerdir. Genel bir ipucu: -ing çoğunlukla önceden olmuş / gerçekleşmiş bir eylemi, to + V1 ise sonra yapılacak / amaçlanan bir eylemi gösterir. remember doing (yaptığını hatırlamak) ↔ remember to do (yapmayı unutmamak).

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
Always V-ing (group 1)enjoy / avoid / consider / deny / mind / risk / suggest / finish / keep / practiseBu fiillerden sonra her zaman gerund → -ingI enjoy learning. / She avoided answering. / He denied taking it.
Always to-inf (group 2)want / decide / plan / hope / fail / refuse / afford / agree / manage / offerBu fiillerden sonra her zaman infinitiveShe decided to leave. / He failed to arrive. / They refused to comment.
Meaning changes!stop / remember / forget / try / regret / go on / meanFARKLI anlam — dikkat!stop to smoke ≠ stop smoking / remember to call ≠ remember calling
need + V-ing'need' + V-ing = passive meaningThe car needs washing. = The car needs to be washed.Your essay needs checking. / This needs fixing urgently.
⚠️⚠️ ANLAM DEĞİŞİMİ kritik! 'I stopped to smoke' = Durdum ve sigara içtim (amaç). 'I stopped smoking' = Sigara bıraktım. | 'I tried to open it' = Çabaladım (ama belki yapamadım). 'I tried opening it' = Denedim, baktım ne oldu.

🔍 Örneklerle

🧠 remember / forget
I remember locking the door. → Kapıyı kilitlediğimi hatırlıyorum. (geçmiş)
Remember to lock the door. → Kapıyı kilitlemeyi unutma. (gelecek)
I'll never forget meeting her. → Onunla tanıştığımı asla unutmayacağım.
⏸️ stop / try
He stopped smoking last year. → Geçen yıl sigarayı bıraktı.
He stopped to buy some water. → Su almak için durdu.
Try restarting the phone. → Telefonu yeniden başlatmayı dene (bak işe yarayacak mı).
I tried to open the jar, but I couldn't. → Kavanozu açmaya çalıştım ama açamadım.
😔 regret / go on / mean
I regret saying that. → Bunu söylediğime pişmanım.
We regret to inform you that... → Size ... bildirmekten üzüntü duyarız. (resmi)
She went on talking for an hour. → Bir saat konuşmaya devam etti.
Taking the job means moving to Izmir. → İşi kabul etmek İzmir'e taşınmak demek.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — look forward to / be used to
Buradaki to bir edattır, bu yüzden arkasından -ing gelir:
✅ I look forward to hearing from you.   ❌ look forward to hear
✅ I'm used to getting up early.
💡
TIP — Algı fiilleri: see / hear + V1 ya da V-ing
I saw him cross the road. (eylemin tamamını gördüm) / I saw him crossing the road. (eylemin bir anını gördüm).

📝 Kendine sor — pratik yap

Don't forget ___ (call) your grandmother tonight. to call
I'll never forget ___ (see) the sea for the first time. seeing
She stopped ___ (eat) sugar two months ago. eating
I'm looking forward to ___ (meet) you. meeting

Can You Think in Another Language?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Many language learners report beginning to think in their new language at a certain level — a remarkable cognitive shift. But what does it actually mean to think in a language, and does it change how we see the world?

Researchers have tried investigating this connection for decades. Some linguists avoid making strong claims, preferring to acknowledge complexity. Others have stopped trying to separate language from thought entirely.

What is clear is that successful language acquisition involves resisting the urge to translate word-for-word from your first language. You must try forgetting your mother tongue as a crutch. Top learners tend to stop relying on translation and start engaging directly with the new language.

They remember learning their first language unconsciously — without grammar rules, without translation, without effort. They try recreating that same natural process. Some even risk making embarrassing mistakes in public, understanding that this discomfort is essential for growth.

Many learners regret not starting earlier. But they also admit to having found unexpected joys: the moment of dreaming in a new language, of making a joke that lands, of understanding a film without subtitles.

ℹ️ Görüş yazısı — gerçek bilgiler içerir, yorumlar yazara aittir.

Beginning to think in their new language — a cognitive shift.

2What does the text say successful learners must stop doing?

Relying on translation and using their mother tongue as a crutch.

3What risk do top learners take?

Making embarrassing mistakes in public.

4Find 3 verbs followed by gerund (V-ing) and 3 verbs followed by infinitive (to + V1) in the text.

V-ing: avoid making, resist translating, risk making / to-inf: try to separate, tend to stop, prefer to acknowledge
