Advanced Passive — İleri Edilgen Yapılar

B2 seviyesinde edilgen yapı sadece "is/was + V3" değildir. Modal passives, continuous passives, perfect passives ve özellikle akademik yazıda sıklıkla görülen impersonal passive (It is believed/said/thought) yapıları öğrenilir.

📌 Tüm Zamanlarda Passive

ZamanPassive YapıÖrnek
Present Simpleis/are + V3Thousands of trees are cut down daily.
Past Simplewas/were + V3The vaccine was developed in 2020.
Present Continuousis/are being + V3The bridge is being repaired.
Past Continuouswas/were being + V3The suspect was being watched.
Present Perfecthave/has been + V3Three people have been arrested.
Past Perfecthad been + V3The letter had been opened before she arrived.
Futurewill be + V3The results will be announced tomorrow.
Modalmodal + be + V3This must be checked immediately.

The Microbiome Revolution

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

It has recently been established that the human body contains roughly 38 trillion bacteria — about the same order as the number of human cells (around 30 trillion). These microorganisms, collectively known as the microbiome, have been shown to influence digestion, immunity, and even mood and mental health.

For decades, bacteria were regarded simply as pathogens — things to be eliminated. This understanding has been completely revised. A groundbreaking new field — psychobiotics — is being developed. Links have been found between gut bacteria and depression, anxiety, and cognitive performance — so far mostly in animal studies.

The term "psychobiotic" was first put forward by researchers in Cork, Ireland, in 2013. At first, the idea was treated with scepticism by many scientists. Since then, many of the animal findings have been replicated, although evidence from human trials is still considered limited.

It is now widely believed that diet plays a major role in shaping the microbiome. Processed food and antibiotics are thought to disrupt the gut ecosystem significantly. Whether probiotic supplements can genuinely help remains to be established by further research.

More funding must be directed towards this field. The results, experts agree, could prove transformative for our understanding of mental health.

ℹ️ Gerçek olay ve araştırmalara dayanır; ders amacıyla sadeleştirilmiştir.

📚 Kaynaklar

  • Sender, Fuchs & Milo (2016), PLOS Biology — Revised estimates
  • Dinan, Stanton & Cryan (2013), Biological Psychiatry — Psychobiotics

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How many bacteria does the human body contain?

Roughly 38 trillion — about the same order as the number of human cells (around 30 trillion).

2What is 'psychobiotics'?

A new field linking gut bacteria to mental health, cognitive performance, depression, and anxiety.

3How was the idea initially received, and how has this changed?

Many scientists were sceptical at first; many animal findings have since been replicated, but human evidence is still limited.

4Find 4 passive structures in the text from different tenses.

e.g. has been established (PP), were regarded (past), is being developed (present cont.), must be directed (modal passive)

1Bilimsel keşif

Write a short paragraph (4–5 sentences) about a scientific discovery using at least 4 passive structures from different tenses. Start: It has recently been discovered that... / The findings were...
0 kelime
It has recently been discovered that gut bacteria can significantly affect our mood. The theory was first proposed in 2013 and has since been widely accepted. The findings have been replicated in dozens of peer-reviewed studies. New treatments are currently being developed based on this groundbreaking research.

0 kelime
It is widely believed that artificial intelligence will transform medicine within the next decade. It has been shown that AI can detect certain cancers more accurately than doctors in some cases. It is generally accepted that this powerful technology must be carefully regulated to protect patients.
