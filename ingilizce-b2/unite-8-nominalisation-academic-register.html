İsimleştirme (nominalisation) ve akademik üslup

Akademik ve resmi metinlerde eylemler çoğu zaman isme dönüştürülür. Böylece cümle daha kısa, nesnel ve bilgi yoğun olur: Prices increased rapidly, so people protested. → The rapid increase in prices led to protests. Fiildeki zarf, isimde sıfata dönüşür (rapidly → rapid); özne ya da nesne ise çoğunlukla of ile bağlanır.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
Verb → NounCommon suffixes: -tion/-sion, -ment, -ance/-ence, -al, -ureFiilden isim türetmeintegrate→integration / develop→development / fail→failure / arrive→arrival
Adjective → NounSuffixes: -ity, -ness, -ance, -cySıfattan isim türetmecomplex→complexity / stable→stability / frequent→frequency / aware→awareness
Why nominalise?Academic/formal register — less personal, more objective, more concise'People move' = informal / 'Migration increases' = academicThe achievement of equality remains distant. (formal) vs. 'It's hard to make people equal.' (informal)
The + noun + ofThe + V-N + of + noun phrase = formal action phraseİsimlesen fiilin nesnesini göstermeThe development OF new technologies / The reduction OF poverty / The integration OF markets
⚠️⚠️ Common nominalisation errors: ❌ 'the increasing of trade' ✅ 'the increase in trade' | ❌ 'the failing of the talks' ✅ 'the failure of the talks' | Check the correct noun form — some are irregular!

🔍 Örneklerle

🔁 Günlük → Akademik
Scientists discovered the virus in 1983.
→ The discovery of the virus in 1983... → Virüsün 1983'te keşfi...
The government decided to cut taxes.
→ The government's decision to cut taxes... → Hükümetin vergileri düşürme kararı...
🧩 Sık kullanılan kalıplar
an increase in / a decrease in / a rise in → ...de artış / azalış
the impact of X on Y → X'in Y üzerindeki etkisi
the need for reform → reform ihtiyacı
a lack of evidence → kanıt eksikliği
🔗 Nominalisation + bağlayıcı fiil
Poor diet contributes to illness. → Kötü beslenme hastalığa katkıda bulunur.
The drop in sales resulted from higher prices. → Satışlardaki düşüş yüksek fiyatlardan kaynaklandı.
Better training led to an improvement in safety. → Daha iyi eğitim güvenlikte iyileşmeye yol açtı.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Yanlış isim ya da edat
❌ an increase of prices → ✅ an increase in prices
❌ the analyse → ✅ the analysis   ❌ the fail → ✅ the failure
❌ the explaining of → ✅ the explanation of

TIP — Aşırıya kaçma
Tek cümlede üç-dört isimleştirme metni ağırlaştırır. İyi akademik yazı, isimleştirmeyi ana fikri özlü vermek için kullanır; geri kalanı canlı fiillerle anlatır.

The Promise and the Problem of Globalisation

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

The integration of global markets was supposed to benefit everyone. The expansion of trade has indeed created wealth — but also widening inequality. The development of communication technology has accelerated this process to a speed that was unimaginable even thirty years ago.

The establishment of international institutions — the WTO, the IMF, the World Bank — was intended to provide economic stability. But the resistance of many communities to rapid economic change has been far greater than policymakers anticipated. Migration — both economic and forced — has increased dramatically, generating profound political consequences.

The outsourcing of manufacturing to lower-wage countries created complex ethical questions. The concentration of wealth in the hands of a small global elite represents perhaps globalisation's greatest failure. Despite its promises, the achievement of genuine global economic equality remains a distant aspiration.

The reduction of extreme poverty in some regions — particularly in East Asia — represents a genuine success. But this has sometimes come at the cost of de-industrialisation elsewhere, generating resentment and political instability.

The complexity of these trade-offs cannot be overstated. There are no simple solutions. What is required is not the abandonment of global cooperation, but its more careful, equitable, and democratic management.

1Nominalizasyon dönüşümü

Rewrite these informal sentences in a more academic/formal style using nominalisation: 1-) 'People are integrating into global markets.' 2-) 'Governments are failing to act.' 3-) 'The economy is developing rapidly.'
0 kelime
The rapid integration of developing economies into global markets has accelerated significantly over the past three decades. The failure of governments to address growing inequality has deepened poverty in large regions of the Global South. The uneven development of the economy has clearly not benefited all populations equally or fairly.

2Akademik paragraf

Write a formal paragraph (4–5 sentences) about globalisation or another social issue. Use at least 5 nominalisations.
0 kelime
Globalisation has led to the unprecedented integration of financial markets and the dramatic expansion of global trade. However, the concentration of extraordinary wealth in a very small number of hands has been a major and growing failure. Despite the genuine reduction of extreme poverty in some regions, the development of truly equitable global economic systems has remained frustratingly slow.
