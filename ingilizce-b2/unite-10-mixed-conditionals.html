Karışık koşul cümleleri ve resmi devrik koşul

Normal koşul cümlelerinde iki taraf aynı zamandan bahseder. Karışık (mixed) koşulda ise if tarafı ile sonuç tarafı farklı zamanlardadır: geçmişteki bir olayın şimdiki sonucu, ya da şu an doğru olan bir durumun geçmişteki sonucu. Resmi yazıda if atılıp Had / Were / Should başa alınabilir.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
Mixed: Past IF → Present ResultIf + had + V3, would + V1 (now)Geçmişteki farklı eylem → Şimdiki farklı durumIf we had invested in clean energy in the 1970s, we wouldn't be facing a climate emergency now.
Mixed: Present IF → Past ResultIf + Past Simple, would have + V3Şimdiki farklı durum → Geçmişteki farklı sonuçIf she were more careful (now), she wouldn't have made that mistake (yesterday).
Formal 3rd (no if)Had + subject + V3 + ... + would have + V3'Had' = 'If had' — inversion without 'if'Had they acted sooner, the outcome would have been very different.
Were + subject + toWere + subject + to + V1 = formal if + subject + V1Düşük ihtimalli formal koşulWere AI to achieve general intelligence, the implications would be profound.
⚠️⚠️ Mixed conditional = iki farklı zaman dilimi. 'If we had known (PAST), we would be (NOW) better prepared.' | 'Had they known' = 'If they had known' (formal inversion). | 'Were this to happen' = low probability / formal.

🔍 Örneklerle

⏪➡️⏺️ Geçmiş koşul → şimdiki sonuç
If I had taken the job, I would be living in London now. → O işi alsaydım şimdi Londra'da yaşıyor olurdum.
If she hadn't missed the bus, she would be here. → Otobüsü kaçırmasaydı burada olurdu.
⏺️➡️⏪ Şimdiki durum → geçmiş sonuç
If I spoke French, I would have understood the film. → Fransızca bilsem filmi anlardım (anlamadım).
If he weren't so shy, he would have asked her out. → Bu kadar utangaç olmasa ona çıkma teklif ederdi.
🎩 Devrik (if'siz)
Had we left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train. → = If we had left earlier...
Were it not for your help, I would fail. → Yardımın olmasa başaramazdım.
Should the weather change, the match will be cancelled. → Hava değişirse maç iptal edilir.
🚨
IMPORTANT — Zaman ipuçlarına bak
now, today, still → sonuç tarafı şimdi: would + V1 / would be + V-ing
yesterday, last year, then → sonuç tarafı geçmiş: would have + V3
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — Devrikte if kalmaz, olumsuzda kısaltma olmaz
❌ If had I known... → ✅ Had I known...
❌ Hadn't we left early... → ✅ Had we not left early...

What Future Are We Building?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

If we had taken climate warnings seriously in the 1970s, we would not be facing the emergency we face today. That is a textbook mixed conditional: a past failure with a present consequence.

Had governments acted on the scientific consensus of the 1990s, the trajectory of warming would have been radically different. That is a pure third conditional — a past imaginary.

Were artificial intelligence to achieve general human-level intelligence this century, the implications would be among the most profound in human history. We cannot yet know if this will happen — but we can prepare.

Had humanity invested as heavily in pandemic preparedness as in military technology, COVID-19 might have claimed far fewer lives. This is another past conditional — a tragic one.

The question is not only what we should have done. It is what we will choose to do now. If we were to take seriously the existential risks we face — climate change, biodiversity loss, AI safety, pandemic risk — our priorities as a civilisation would look very different from today's.

The future is not predetermined. But every day that passes without adequate action makes the best outcomes less achievable — and the worst, more likely. Had we understood this earlier — would we have acted? Perhaps. The more pressing question: now that we understand it — will we?

ℹ️ Görüş yazısı.

'Were artificial intelligence to achieve general human-level intelligence...'

4What is the text's central question at the end?

Now that we understand the existential risks — will we actually act?
