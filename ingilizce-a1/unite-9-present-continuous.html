Present Continuous — Şimdiki Zaman

Şu anda, tam konuşurken olan eylemler için kullanılır. Türkçe'deki "-yor" eki gibidir: "yağıyor, yapıyorum, gidiyoruz." Yapısı: am/is/are + fiil-ing

📌 Temel Yapı

Özneam/is/areV-ingÖrnek
IamrainingIt is raining. → Yağmur yağıyor.
He/She/ItisworkingShe is working. → Çalışıyor.
You/We/TheyareplayingThey are playing. → Oynuyorlar.

📌 -ing ekleme — Hatırla!

DurumKuralÖrnek
Çoğu fiil+ ingeat→eating · play→playing · read→reading
-e ile bitenlere'yi at + ingmake→making · write→writing · dance→dancing
kısa + sessiz-sesli-sessizson harf çift + ingsit→sitting · run→running · swim→swimming

📌 Olumsuz & Soru

❌ Olumsuz
I am not sleeping. → Uyumuyorum.
She isn't watching TV. → TV izlemiyor.
They aren't playing. → Oynamıyorlar.
❓ Soru
Is it raining? → Yağıyor mu?
Are you listening? → Dinliyor musun?
What are you doing? → Ne yapıyorsun?
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — Simple Present vs Present Continuous
Simple Present → alışkanlık, her gün: I eat breakfast every morning.
Present Continuous → şu an: I am eating breakfast right now.
İpucu kelimeleri: now, right now, at the moment, look!, listen! → Present Continuous
every day, always, usually → Simple Present

Four Seasons in Turkey

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Turkey has four beautiful seasons. Each season has different weather and activities.

In spring, it is warm and sometimes rainy. Flowers are growing everywhere. Students are playing outside at break time. Farmers are working in the fields. The temperature is about 15-20 degrees.

In summer, it is very hot and sunny. Families are going to the beach. Children are swimming in the sea. The temperature can reach 40 degrees in some cities! People are wearing T-shirts, shorts and sunscreen.

In autumn, it is cool and windy. Leaves are falling from the trees. Students are going back to school. It is raining more often now.

In winter, it is cold. In the east of Turkey, it is snowing heavily. Children are making snowmen and throwing snowballs! In Istanbul, it sometimes snows too.

ℹ️ Bilgilendirici metin — Türkiye'nin genel iklim özelliklerini anlatır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What is the weather like in spring?

It is warm and sometimes rainy.

2What are families doing in summer?

They are going to the beach and swimming in the sea.

3Where does it snow heavily in winter?

In the east of Turkey.

4What temperature can it reach in some Turkish cities in summer?

40 degrees.
