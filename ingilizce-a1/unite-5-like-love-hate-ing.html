like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing (Gerund)

Sevmek, nefret etmek veya hoşlanmak ifade ederken bu fiillerin ardından başka bir fiil gelirse o fiil -ing alır. Bu yapıya gerund denir. Türkçe'de "yüzmeyi seviyorum" → "I love swimming."

📌 Temel Kural

FiilTürkçe+ -ing ile örnek
loveçok sevmekI love swimming. → Yüzmeyi çok seviyorum.
likesevmek, hoşlanmakShe likes reading. → Okumayı seviyor.
enjoyzevk almakHe enjoys cooking. → Yemek yapmaktan zevk alıyor.
hatenefret etmekI hate waking up early. → Erken kalkmaktan nefret ederim.
don't mindumursamıyorum / sorun değilI don't mind waiting. → Beklemeyi umursamıyorum.

📌 -ing ekleme kuralları

DurumKuralÖrnekler
Çoğu fiilsadece +ingread→reading · eat→eating · play→playing
-e ile bitenlere'yi at + ingmake→making · write→writing · dance→dancing
Kısa heceli + sessiz+sesli+sessizson harfi çift + ingswim→swimming · run→running · sit→sitting
🚨
IMPORTANT — enjoy her zaman -ing ile!
❌ I enjoy to watch films.
✅ I enjoy watching films.
enjoy + to-infinitive hiçbir zaman kullanılmaz. Her zaman enjoy + V-ing!
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — like + -ing vs like + to
like + -ing → genel tercih: I like swimming. (genelde yüzmeyi severim)
like + to → belirli bir an: I'd like to swim now. (şu an yüzmek istiyorum)
A1 seviyesinde: like/love/hate/enjoy + -ing kullan — güvenli!
💡
TIP — He/She için unutma!
He/She ile fiil -s alır ama -ing almaz:
✅ She likes swimming.
✅ He enjoys cooking.
✅ She hates waking up early.

What Do Turkish Students Eat?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

In Turkey, breakfast is a very important meal. Turkish students usually have bread, cheese, olives, eggs and tomatoes for breakfast. Many students love eating honey and butter too. They drink tea or milk.

At school, students have lunch in the canteen. The menu changes every day. On Mondays, there is often chicken and rice. On Fridays, many schools serve fish. Most students like the food, but some students don't like vegetables!

After school, many students are hungry. They like eating fruit, biscuits or toasted sandwiches. Some students love chips and cola, but teachers say junk food is not healthy.

In the evening, Turkish families have a big dinner together. They eat soup, a main course and sometimes a dessert. Baklava and rice pudding are popular Turkish desserts. What do you eat for dinner?

ℹ️ Bilgilendirici metin — genel gözlemlere dayanır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What do Turkish students usually have for breakfast?

Bread, cheese, olives, eggs, and tomatoes. They also drink tea or milk.

2What is served at school on Fridays?

Many schools serve fish on Fridays.

3Why do teachers say chips and cola are bad?

Because junk food is not healthy.

4Name two popular Turkish desserts mentioned in the text.

Baklava and rice pudding.
