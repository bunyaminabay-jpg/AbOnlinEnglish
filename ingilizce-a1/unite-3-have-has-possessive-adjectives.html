have / has & İyelik Sıfatları (my, your, his...)

Bu ünitede iki şey öğreniyorsun: (1) "sahip olmak" için have/has, (2) "kimin?" sorusunu yanıtlayan iyelik sıfatları. Bunlar hep birlikte kullanılır.

🚨
IMPORTANT — Kural
He / She / It → HAS (diğer hepsi → HAVE)
❌ She have a sister.    ✅ She has a sister.
❌ My mother have a car.    ✅ My mother has a car.

Olumsuz — Don't/Doesn't have
I don't have a brother.
She doesn't have a pet.
They don't have a garden.
Do you have any cousins?
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — his / her / its
his → erkek hakkında konuşurken: Ali has a bike. His bike is red.
her → kız hakkında konuşurken: Ece has a cat. Her cat is orange.
its → nesne/hayvan hakkında: The school has a garden. Its garden is big.
Türkçe'de hepsi "onun" — İngilizce'de cinsiyete göre değişir!

Ece's Family

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

My name is Ece. I have a big family. My family has five people: my mother, my father, my grandmother, my brother and me.

My mother's name is Ayşe. She is a doctor. My father's name is Mehmet. He is a teacher. My brother's name is Ali. He is eight years old. He has curly, brown hair.

My grandmother lives with us. Her name is Fatma. She is 72 years old. She has white hair and kind eyes. I love her very much!

I also have a cousin. Her name is Deniz. Her mother is my aunt — my father's sister. Deniz and I are best friends. We have the same favourite colour — purple!

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.
