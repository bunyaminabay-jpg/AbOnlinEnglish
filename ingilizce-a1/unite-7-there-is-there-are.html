There is / There are & Yer Edatları

There is / There are = "vardır, bulunmaktadır." Türkçe'deki "var" sözcüğünün karşılığıdır. Nerede olduğunu söylemek için ise yer edatlarını (prepositions of place) kullanırsın.

📌 There is / There are

YapıNe zaman?ÖrnekTürkçesi
There is (There's)tekil isimThere is a book on the desk.Masada bir kitap var.
There areçoğul isimThere are 30 students.30 öğrenci var.
There isn'ttekil — yokThere isn't a TV.Televizyon yok.
There aren'tçoğul — yokThere aren't any chairs.Sandalye yok.
Is there...?tekil soruIs there a garden?Bahçe var mı?
Are there...?çoğul soruAre there any shops?Dükkân var mı?

EdatTürkçeÖrnek

⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE
❌ There is many students. → Çoğul → ✅ There are many students.
❌ There are a book. → Tekil → ✅ There is a book.
❌ Is there any books? → Çoğul → ✅ Are there any books?
💡
TIP — any kullanımı
Soru ve olumsuzlarda "herhangi" anlamında any kullanılır:
Is there any milk? / There isn't any milk.
Olumlu cümlede some: There is some milk in the fridge.

Ece's Apartment

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

I live in an apartment in Ankara. It is on the fifth floor. There are four rooms in our apartment: a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. There isn't a garden, but there is a big balcony.

In the living room, there is a large sofa, a coffee table and a TV. There are some books on the bookshelf. The curtains are blue — my favourite colour!

My bedroom is small but comfortable. There is a bed, a desk and a wardrobe. There are many posters on my walls — my favourite singer and my football team. My desk is next to the window. My backpack is under the desk.

The kitchen is my mother's favourite room. There is a big table and four chairs. On the table, there are always fresh fruits and flowers. Our apartment is small, but it is our home and I love it!

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How many rooms are in Ece's apartment?

There are four rooms (living room, kitchen, two bedrooms, bathroom).

2Is there a garden?

No, there isn't a garden, but there is a big balcony.

3Where is Ece's desk?

It is next to the window.

4What is always on the kitchen table?

Fresh fruits and flowers.
