can / can't — Yetenek & İstek

can = yapabilmek (yetenek). can't = yapamamak. Bu ikisi İngilizce'nin en kolay yardımcı fiilleridir çünkü hiçbir özne ile değişmezler — he/she/it için bile!

📌 Temel Tablo

YapıAnlamÖrnekTürkçesi
özne + can + V1yapabilmekI can swim.Yüzebiliyorum.
özne + can't + V1yapamamakShe can't drive.Araba kullanamıyor.
Can + özne + V1?soruCan you dance?Dans edebiliyor musun?
Yes, I can.kısa olumlu cevapYes, he can.Evet, yapabilir.
No, I can't.kısa olumsuz cevapNo, she can't.Hayır, yapamaz.
🚨
IMPORTANT — can ASLA değişmez!
❌ She cans swim.    ✅ She can swim.
❌ He can swims.    ✅ He can swim.
can'dan sonra fiil daima sade haliyle (V1) gelir — asla -s, -ing veya -ed almaz!

📌 want to / would like to — İstek ifadesi

YapıAnlamÖrnek
want to + V1yapmak istiyorum (güçlü istek)I want to learn guitar. → Gitar öğrenmek istiyorum.
would like to + V1yapmak isterim (nazik)I'd like to try surfing. → Sörf denemek isterim.
💡
TIP — want to vs would like to
want to → daha doğrudan, günlük konuşmada
would like to (='d like to) → daha nazik, resmi
İkisi de doğru, ama resmi ortamda 'd like to tercih et.
🌍
REAL ENGLISH — Günlük konuşmada
"Can you help me?" → Bana yardım edebilir misin?
"I can't believe it!" → İnanamıyorum!
"I'd like a coffee, please." → Bir kahve istiyorum, lütfen. (kafede sipariş)
can ayrıca izin istemek için de kullanılır: "Can I go now?" → Gidebilir miyim?

The School Talent Show

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Our school has a talent show every year. Students can show their special abilities. It is very exciting!

Mia can play the piano beautifully. She can also sing. But she can't dance — she says she has two left feet! Her friend Zeynep can dance very well. She does folk dancing every Saturday.

Can rides his bike to school every day. He can also do karate. He has a black belt! But he can't swim. He wants to learn swimming next summer.

My hobby is drawing. I can draw animals and people, but I can't draw buildings — they are too difficult for me! I would like to take an art class next year.

What can YOU do? Can you play an instrument, do a sport, or draw? Everyone has a special talent!

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What can Mia do?

She can play the piano and sing.

2What can't Mia do?

She can't dance.

3What does Can want to learn?

He wants to learn swimming.

4What can the writer draw?

Animals and people, but not buildings.
