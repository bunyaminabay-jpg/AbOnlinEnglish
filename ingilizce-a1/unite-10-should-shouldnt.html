should / shouldn't — Tavsiye & Öneri

should = yapmalısın / yapman iyi olur. shouldn't = yapmamalısın / yapman iyi olmaz. Tavsiye ve öneri verirken kullanılır. Türkçe'deki "-malı/-meli" anlamı taşır ama daha naziftir.

📌 Temel Yapı

YapıAnlamÖrnekTürkçesi
özne + should + V1tavsiye / öneriYou should sleep early.Erken uyuman iyi olur.
özne + shouldn't + V1yapmaması önerisiYou shouldn't eat junk food.Abur cubur yememeli­sin.

🚨
IMPORTANT — should sonrası sade fiil!
should, can gibi bir modal fiildir — ardından fiil asla -s, -ing veya -ed almaz:
❌ She should goes.    ✅ She should go.
❌ You should to rest.    ✅ You should rest.

🏥 Sağlık tavsiyeleri — Gerçek kullanım

✅ should — yapmalısın
You should drink water. → Su içmelisin.
You should rest at home. → Evde dinlenmelisin.
You should eat vegetables. → Sebze yemelisin.
❌ shouldn't — yapmamalısın
You shouldn't stay up late. → Geç yatmamalısın.
You shouldn't eat sugar. → Şeker yememeli­sin.
You shouldn't skip meals. → Öğün atlamamalısın.
💡
TIP — should vs must
should → tavsiye, öneri (soft): You should see a doctor. (öneririm)
must → zorunluluk (strong): You must see a doctor! (gitmen şart!)
Günlük tavsiyelerde should çok daha doğal ve kibarca gelir.

At the Doctor's

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Ali isn't at school today. He feels terrible. His mother calls the doctor's office and makes an appointment.

At the doctor's office, the doctor asks: "What's wrong, Ali?" Ali says: "I have a terrible headache. My throat hurts and I have a fever too." The doctor checks his temperature: 38.5 degrees. "You have a cold," says the doctor.

"What should I do?" asks Ali. The doctor answers: "You should drink lots of water and hot soup. You should take this medicine twice a day. You should rest and stay at home for two days. You shouldn't go to school tomorrow. And you shouldn't eat too much."

Ali's mother is worried. "Is it serious?" she asks. "No, don't worry. He should feel better in two days," says the doctor.

Two days later, Ali feels much better. He is ready to go back to school!

2Sağlıklı tavsiyeler

Write 4 health tips using SHOULD and SHOULDN'T. Example: You should eat vegetables every day. You shouldn't eat too much sugar.
0 kelime
You should drink eight glasses of water every day. You should eat vegetables with every meal. You shouldn't eat too much sugar or junk food. You shouldn't stay up late — you should get eight hours of sleep every night.
