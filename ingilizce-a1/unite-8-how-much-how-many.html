How much / How many & Sayılar, Fiyatlar

Alışveriş yaparken, fiyat sorarken veya miktar sorarken bu yapıları kullanırsın. How much ile How many arasındaki fark şudur: sayılabiliyor mu, sayılamıyor mu?

📌 How much vs How many

SoruNe zaman?Örnekler
How muchSayılamayan isimler (money, water, bread, milk…)How much is it? / How much water?
How manySayılabilen çoğul isimler (books, students, chairs…)How many books? / How many students?

Shopping at the Market

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Every Saturday, my family goes to the local market. There are many stalls with vegetables, fruit, clothes and books. It is always very busy!

Today, I have 60 liras in my pocket. I want to buy a present for my mother. At the flower stall, I ask: "How much are these roses?" The seller says: "They're 30 liras for ten roses." That's cheap! I buy them.

Then I see a nice scarf. "How much is the blue scarf?" I ask. "It's 40 liras," says the seller. "But it's on sale today — 25 liras!" I buy the scarf too. I pay 55 liras in total, so I have 5 liras left.

My mother is very happy with her flowers and scarf. The market is a great place to shop!

3How much is the scarf on sale?

25 liras (on sale today).

4How much money does the writer start with?

60 liras.
