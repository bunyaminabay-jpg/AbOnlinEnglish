Simple Present Tense — Geniş Zaman

Geniş zaman İngilizce'nin en sık kullandığın yapısıdır. Her gün yapılan, alışkanlık olan, genel doğru olan şeyler için kullanılır. Türkçe'deki "-r, -er, -ir, -ar" ekli zamanla eşdeğerdir: "giderim, içerim, uyurum."

📌 Kural 1 — Olumlu cümle

ÖzneFiilÖrnekTürkçesi
I / You / We / Theysade fiil (V1)I wake up at 7.Saat 7'de uyanırım.
He / She / Itfiil +s / +esShe wakes up at 7.Saat 7'de uyanır.

📌 He/She/It için +s kuralları

DurumKuralÖrnekler
Çoğu fiil+ swork→works · eat→eats · read→reads · play→plays
-sh, -ch, -x, -o, -ss ile bitenler+ eswatch→watches · wash→washes · go→goes · do→does
-y ile bitenler (üstünde sessiz)y→i + esstudy→studies · fly→flies
İstisna — ezbere!düzensizhave→has

📌 Kural 2 — Olumsuz cümle

I/You/We/They → don't
I don't eat breakfast.
They don't watch TV.
We don't go to school on Sundays.
He/She/It → doesn't
She doesn't eat meat.
He doesn't like coffee.
It doesn't work. (çalışmıyor)
🚨
IMPORTANT — doesn't + sade fiil!
doesn't/don't kullandığında fiil asla -s almaz!
❌ She doesn't eats.    ✅ She doesn't eat.
❌ He doesn't likes it.    ✅ He doesn't like it.

📌 Kural 3 — Soru cümlesi

ÖzneYardımcıSoruKısa cevap
I/You/We/TheyDoDo you eat breakfast?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
He/She/ItDoesDoes she eat breakfast?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — En çok yapılan 3 hata
1️⃣ ❌ She go to school. → ✅ She goes to school.
2️⃣ ❌ He don't eat meat. → ✅ He doesn't eat meat.
3️⃣ ❌ Does she goes...? → ✅ Does she go...? (soru+doesn't → sade fiil)

Ali's Busy Day

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Ali is a busy student. He wakes up at 7 o'clock every morning. He gets dressed quickly and brushes his teeth. Then he has breakfast with his family. He usually eats bread and cheese.

Ali goes to school at 8 o'clock. His school starts at 8:30. He has six lessons every day. He has lunch at school at 12:30.

Ali comes home at 4 o'clock. He doesn't watch TV immediately. He does his homework first. He studies for about two hours. Then he watches TV or plays video games.

Ali has dinner with his family at 7 o'clock. After dinner, he reads a book or listens to music. He goes to bed at 10 o'clock. Ali is a healthy, organised boy!

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What time does Ali wake up?

He wakes up at 7 o'clock.

2What does Ali do first when he comes home?

He does his homework first.

3What time does Ali go to bed?

He goes to bed at 10 o'clock.

4What does Ali usually eat for breakfast?

He usually eats bread and cheese.
