Second Conditional & Wish

Second Conditional şu an gerçek olmayan ya da gerçekleşmesi zor olan durumları anlatır. Wish ise şu an olmayan bir şeyi dilemek için kullanılır. İkisi de geçmiş zaman kipi kullanır ama geçmiş anlamı taşımaz!

📌 Second Conditional

If clauseMain clauseÖrnek
If + Past Simplewould/could + V1If I had more time, I would travel more.
If + werewould + V1If I were you, I wouldn't accept that offer.

📌 Wish — Şu anki dilekler

YapıAnlamÖrnek
I wish + Past SimpleŞu an olmayan şeyI wish I knew the answer. (bilmiyorum)
I wish + wereŞu anki durumI wish I were taller. (değilim)
I wish + wouldDavranış değişikliği isteğiI wish he would stop smoking. (sinirlisin)
🚨
IMPORTANT — Wish ile geçmiş zaman = şimdiki anlam!
"I wish I had more money." → Şu an param yok, keşke olsaydı.
"had" geçmiş zamandır ama anlam şu andır!
❌ I wish I have more money. (Present tense — yanlış)
✅ I wish I had more money.
🌍
REAL ENGLISH — Günlük kullanım
"If only I were taller!" → Keşke daha uzun olsaydım!
"I wish the weather would change." → Hava değişse keşke.
"If I were you, I'd talk to him." → Senin yerinde olsam, onunla konuşurdum.
"I wish I could help you." → Keşke yardım edebilseydim. (edemiyorum)

Istanbul: The City That Never Sleeps

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Istanbul is extraordinary — a city where two continents meet, where 8,000 years of history breathe in every stone. But it is also a city under enormous pressure. Traffic, overcrowding, and soaring costs are testing its residents every single day.

"If the metro system were expanded to every district, I wouldn't spend three hours in traffic every day," says Mehmet, a teacher who commutes from the Asian side. "If I could afford to live in the city centre, I absolutely would — but rents have risen enormously in recent years."

Urban planner Defne has ambitious ideas. "If we converted even 20% of our roads to pedestrian zones and cycling paths, air quality would improve dramatically. If we invested properly in public transport, thousands of people would leave their cars at home. Pollution would drop overnight."

But change is slow. "If politicians listened to urban planners more seriously, our cities would be profoundly different," she says. "If I were the mayor, I would start with the most basic things: cleaner air, safer streets, affordable housing for working families."

What would your city look like if you could design it from scratch?

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What is Mehmet's main daily problem?

He spends 3 hours in traffic commuting from the Asian side.

2What two changes would Defne make to improve air quality?

Converting roads to pedestrian/cycling zones and investing in public transport.

3According to Defne, what would happen if politicians listened to urban planners more?

Cities would be profoundly different.

4Find 3 second conditionals in the text and identify: (1) the if-clause verb, (2) the result verb.

e.g. If the metro were expanded (past) + wouldn't spend (would+V1) / If we converted (past) + would improve (would+V1)

Complete these sentences with your own ideas: 1-) If I were the mayor of my city, I would... 2-) If I could live anywhere in the world, I would choose ___ because... 3-) My city would be much better if...
