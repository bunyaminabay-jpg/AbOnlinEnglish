Miktar belirteçleri (quantifiers)

Miktar belirteçleri bir ismin ne kadar / kaç tane olduğunu anlatır. Doğru olanı seçmek için iki soru sor: 1) İsim sayılabilir mi, sayılamaz mı? 2) Anlam olumlu mu (yeterli var) yoksa olumsuz mu (neredeyse yok)? Özellikle few / a few ve little / a little arasındaki fark anlamı tamamen değiştirir.

📌 Özet tablo

Yapı / KuralFormAnlamÖrnek
COUNTABLEmany / a large number of / a few / few / severalSayılabilir çoğul isimlerleMany countries / a few restaurants / several ingredients
UNCOUNTABLEmuch / a great deal of / a little / littleSayılamaz isimlerleMuch time / a great deal of effort / a little salt / little water
BOTHa lot of / lots of / plenty of / hardly any / no / some / anyHer ikisiyle deplenty of food / plenty of ideas / hardly any sugar / no time
few vs a fewfew = neredeyse yok (olumsuz anlam) / a few = birkaç tane var (olumlu)Few people came. (az geldi — kötü) / A few people came. (birkaç kişi — tamam)little = neredeyse yok / a little = biraz var
⚠️⚠️ 'few' (olumsuz — neredeyse yok) vs 'a few' (olumlu — birkaç var) | 'little' (olumsuz) vs 'a little' (olumlu) | 'a large number of' sadece countable plural! ❌ a large number of money ✅ a large amount of money

🔍 Örneklerle

✅ Olumlu anlam
I have a few friends here, so I'm not lonely. → Burada birkaç arkadaşım var, yalnız değilim.
There's a little time before the bus. → Otobüse biraz zaman var.
Don't worry, we've got plenty of food. → Merak etme, bol yiyeceğimiz var.
⛔ Olumsuz anlam
Few tourists visit the village in winter. → Kışın köyü çok az turist ziyaret eder.
There's little hope of finding it now. → Onu şimdi bulma ihtimali çok az.
We have hardly any milk left. → Neredeyse hiç sütümüz kalmadı.
📊 Resmi / yazı dili
A large number of students applied. → Çok sayıda öğrenci başvurdu.
It takes a great deal of effort. → Büyük çaba gerektirir.
Several restaurants have closed. → Birkaç restoran kapandı.
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE — number / amount
❌ a large number of money → ✅ a large amount of money
❌ less people → ✅ fewer people (sayılabilir çoğul)  |  ✅ less time (sayılamaz)
💡
TIP — too much / too many / enough
too much noise, too many cars (fazlası var, sorun) — enough chairs / enough time (yeterli). not enough = yetersiz.

📝 Kendine sor — pratik yap

Very ___ people came, so the room felt empty. (çok az) few
Could I have ___ sugar in my tea? (biraz) a little
There were too ___ cars on the road. many
We have ___ of time — the film starts at nine. (bol) plenty

Why We Eat What We Eat

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Food is never just fuel. It is culture, history, identity, and memory. Every country has a great deal of history hidden in its dishes.

Turkish cuisine, for example, has absorbed a large number of influences — from Central Asia, the Middle East, the Balkans, and the Mediterranean — across centuries of empire and migration. Take bread: there are plenty of varieties in Turkey alone — pide, simit, lavaş, bazlama, mısır ekmeği. Hardly any Turkish meal is served without it.

Korean kimchi — fermented vegetables with a variety of spices — has been made for many centuries. A large number of recent studies suggest it may have significant probiotic benefits. However, little definitive scientific consensus exists yet, and more research is needed.

The Japanese consume very little red meat compared to most Western nations — much less saturated fat overall. A few researchers have attributed Japan's exceptional life expectancy partly to diet, though genetics and healthcare also play roles.

Plenty of global food trends are emerging. A few years ago, dishes like kimchi tacos, matcha lattes, and Turkish-inspired fusion were unknown outside their home countries. Now they are everywhere.

ℹ️ Bilgilendirici metin.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What regions have influenced Turkish cuisine according to the text?

Central Asia, the Middle East, the Balkans, and the Mediterranean.

2What is kimchi and what potential health benefit does it have?

Fermented Korean vegetables with spices — may have probiotic benefits.

3How does Japanese meat consumption compare to Western nations?

They consume very little red meat — much less saturated fat overall.

4Find 5 different quantifiers in the text and say whether the noun is countable or uncountable.

e.g. a great deal of history (uncountable) / a large number of influences (countable) / plenty of varieties (countable) / little consensus (uncountable) / a few researchers (countable)
