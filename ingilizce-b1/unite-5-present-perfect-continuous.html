Present Perfect Continuous

have/has been + V-ing — Geçmişte başlayan ve hâlâ devam eden eylemleri anlatır. Genellikle "ne kadar zamandır?" sorusunu yanıtlar. Türkçe'deki "-ıyor" ile "-dır" eklerinin birleşimine benzer: "Üç saattir çalışıyorum."

📌 Yapı: have/has been + V-ing

ÖzneYapıÖrnekTürkçesi
I/You/We/Theyhave been + V-ingI have been studying for 3 hours.3 saattir çalışıyorum.
He/She/Ithas been + V-ingShe has been working since morning.Sabahtan beri çalışıyor.
Olumsuzhaven't/hasn't been + V-ingI haven't been sleeping well.İyi uyuyamıyorum.
SoruHow long have/has + V-ing?How long have you been waiting?Ne zamandır bekliyorsun?
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous
Present Perfect → tamamlanan eylem, sonuç önemli:
"I have written three emails." (3 e-posta yazdım — tamamlandı, sayı önemli)

Present Perfect Continuous → devam eden süreç:
"I have been writing emails all morning." (sabahtan beri yazıyorum — süreç önemli)

Kural: sonuç mu? → Perfect. Süreç/devamlılık mı? → Continuous.
🚨
IMPORTANT — Stative verbs continuous almaz!
know, believe, love, hate, want, understand gibi "durum bildiren" fiiller
continuous formda kullanılmaz:
❌ I have been knowing her for years.
✅ I have known her for years.
❌ She has been wanting to leave.
✅ She has wanted to leave.

The Loneliness Epidemic

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

In 2023, the United States Surgeon General issued an extraordinary warning: loneliness has become a public health crisis. The data has been building for years: according to the report, about half of American adults experience measurable levels of loneliness.

Researchers have been studying a paradox: how can a generation that has been communicating with hundreds of "friends" online feel so lonely?

The answer appears to lie in the difference between connection and communication. Social media enables communication — I've been posting, you have been liking, we have been scrolling — but genuine connection requires vulnerability, shared experience, and time. These are things screens cannot provide.

Young people have been spending much less time with friends: the report says that people aged 15–24 had 70% less social interaction with their friends than two decades earlier. Some writers have been calling this "the friendship recession."

Some communities have been developing creative solutions: phone-free cafés, community gardens, walking clubs and volunteer groups.

The evidence is clear: humans need face-to-face connection. How long have we been ignoring this?

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What did the US Surgeon General warn about in 2023?

That loneliness has become a public health crisis.

2How much less social interaction with friends did people aged 15–24 have, according to the report?

70% less than two decades earlier.

3What is 'the friendship recession'?

A name some writers use for the fall in the time people — especially young people — spend with friends.

4Find 4 Present Perfect Continuous verbs in the text and explain why PPC is used.

e.g. has been building (ongoing process), have been studying (started + still happening), have been reporting (continuing trend)
