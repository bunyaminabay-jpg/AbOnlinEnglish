Third Conditional & Geçmiş Dilekler

Third Conditional geçmişte gerçekleşmemiş durumları ve onların sonuçlarını anlatır. "Eğer onu görseydin ne olurdu?" — artık geriye dönüş yok. I wish + Past Perfect ise geçmişe duyulan pişmanlığı anlatır.

📌 Third Conditional: If + Past Perfect → would have + V3

If clauseMain clauseÖrnek
If + had + V3would have + V3If I had studied, I would have passed.
If + had + V3could/might have + V3If you had called, I could have helped.

📌 Geçmiş dilekler: I wish + had + V3

YapıAnlamÖrnek
I wish + had + V3Geçmişteki pişmanlıkI wish I had studied harder. (ama çalışmadım)
I wish + hadn't + V3Yaptığına pişmanlıkI wish I hadn't said that. (söyledim, üzgünüm)
If only + had + V3Güçlü pişmanlıkIf only I had listened!
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — 3 tip wish
Şimdiki pişmanlık → wish + Past Simple:
"I wish I knew the answer." (şu an bilmiyorum)

Geçmişteki pişmanlık → wish + Past Perfect:
"I wish I had known the answer." (o zaman bilmiyordum)

Davranış değişikliği isteği → wish + would:
"I wish he would stop talking." (şu an sinirleniyorum)

Too Little, Too Late?

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

In 2015, world leaders gathered in Paris and signed what they called a historic agreement: to keep global warming well below 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels, and to try to limit it to 1.5 degrees. They pledged action. They made commitments. They gave speeches.

Climate scientists had been warning about global warming for decades. Many of them argue that if governments had listened thirty years ago, we would have avoided much of the damage we are seeing today. If only the political will had existed in 1990, this would have been a very different story.

Researchers who have studied company documents have shown that parts of the fossil fuel industry spent decades sowing doubt about climate science. If that disinformation campaign had not been so effective, public pressure for change would have mounted far earlier.

But there is cause for cautious hope. Grassroots movements — led largely by young people — have changed the political landscape. Many young activists believe that if they hadn't started marching in 2018, governments would never have taken this seriously.

Many now ask: if we had known fifty years ago what we know today, would we have done anything differently? Perhaps. But the more important question is: knowing what we know now — what will we do next?

I wish we had started earlier. I wish governments had listened. If only the world had acted as urgently as the science demanded. But we are here now. And the choices we make today will become tomorrow's "if only."

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What did world leaders agree in Paris in 2015?

To keep global warming well below 2 °C above pre-industrial levels and to try to limit it to 1.5 °C.

2What do many climate scientists argue about the past?

That if governments had listened thirty years ago, much of today's damage would have been avoided.

3What is the 'more important question' at the end of the text?

Knowing what we know now — what will we do next?

4Find one 3rd conditional and one 'if only / I wish' structure. Explain what each expresses.

3rd conditional: 'if governments had listened..., we would have avoided...' (imaginary past) / 'I wish we had started earlier.' (regret about the past)
