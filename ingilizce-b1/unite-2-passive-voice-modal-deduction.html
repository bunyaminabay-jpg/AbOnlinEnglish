Passive Voice — Edilgen Çatı

Pasif yapıda özne eylemi yapan değil, eylemin yapıldığı kişi/nesnedir. Türkçe'deki "-ılır/-ilir/-ulur/-ülür" ve "-ıldı/-ildi" eklerine karşılık gelir. "Bu köprü 1900'de inşa edildi."

📌 Yapı: am/is/are/was/were + Past Participle (V3)

ZamanAktif (Active)Pasif (Passive)
Present SimplePeople speak English here.English is spoken here.
Past SimpleThey built this bridge in 1900.This bridge was built in 1900.
Present ContinuousWorkers are fixing the road.The road is being fixed.
Present PerfectSomeone has stolen my bike.My bike has been stolen.
Future (will)They will announce the results.The results will be announced.
ModalYou must return the books.The books must be returned.

📌 Ne zaman edilgen kullanılır?

Yapan önemsiz/bilinmiyor
My car was stolen last night.
English is spoken worldwide.
The results will be published tomorrow.
Yapanı belirtmek → by
The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo.
This book was written by Orhan Pamuk.
"by" sadece önemli olunca eklenir!
⚠️
COMMON MISTAKE
❌ English is speak here. → ✅ English is spoken here. (V3 gerekli!)
❌ The house was build in 1990. → ✅ was built
❌ It is being constructed by workers right now. → OK ama gereksiz "by workers" — yapan belliyse kaldır.
💡
TIP — Aktiften Pasifte Dönüştür
1-) Aktifin nesnesini → pasifin öznesi yap
2-) am/is/are/was/were + V3 ekle
3-) Aktifin öznesi önemliyse "by ..." ile ekle
Örnek: "They built the Eiffel Tower in 1889."
→ "The Eiffel Tower was built in 1889."

The Plastic Problem

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Every year, it is estimated that about 8 million tonnes of plastic are dumped into the world's oceans (a 2015 study gave a range of 4.8 to 12.7 million tonnes). Coral reefs — which have been called "the rainforests of the sea" — are being bleached by rising water temperatures caused by climate change. Fish and seabirds are being killed by plastic pollution daily.

Plastic in the open ocean was first reported by scientists in the early 1970s. Yet significant global action has not been taken until very recently.

In 2012, an 18-year-old Dutch student named Boyan Slat proposed a radical solution in a TEDx talk: using ocean currents to collect plastic. His idea was doubted by many experts at first. Some said it couldn't work; others argued it might be technically interesting but far too expensive.

His organisation, The Ocean Cleanup, has now been running operations for several years, and hundreds of tonnes of plastic have been removed from the Great Pacific Garbage Patch. Whether this approach can solve the problem on a global scale is still being debated.

Experts agree: more renewable energy must be developed, deforestation must be stopped, and plastic must be replaced by sustainable alternatives. The planet cannot be saved by one person — it requires collective action.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How much plastic is dumped into oceans every year, according to the text?

About 8 million tonnes (a 2015 study estimated 4.8–12.7 million tonnes).

2Who is Boyan Slat and what did he propose?

A Dutch student who, at 18, proposed using ocean currents to collect plastic (TEDx talk, 2012).

3How did experts initially react to his idea?

Many doubted it — some said it couldn't work, others said it would be far too expensive.

4Find 4 passive structures and identify their tense/type.

e.g. is estimated (present passive) / have been called (present perfect passive) / was doubted (past passive) / must be developed (modal passive)

1Çevre sorunu (pasif kullan)

Write 4–5 sentences about an environmental problem in Turkey or your country. Use at least 3 passive forms (different tenses). Start: Millions of tonnes of ___ are produced every year. / In recent years, ... has been...
0 kelime
Millions of tonnes of plastic are produced every year around the world. In Turkey, many beaches have been polluted by single-use plastic waste. New recycling centres are currently being built in major cities. The oceans must be protected from further damage before it is too late.

2Serbest yazi

Do you think individuals or governments are more responsible for solving environmental problems? Write 4–5 sentences using must, might, and passive forms.
0 kelime
I believe both individuals and governments are responsible for the environment. Stricter laws must be introduced by governments. However, plastic must also be used less by every individual. The problem might be solved if we all make consistent small changes in our daily lives.
