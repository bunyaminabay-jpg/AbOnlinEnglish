Present Perfect — have / has + V3

Present Perfect geçmiş bir eylemi şimdiki zamanla ilişkilendirir. "Ne zaman?" sorusunun cevabı belirsizdir ya da önemsizdir — önemli olan deneyim, sonuç veya yeni bilgidir. Türkçe'de tam karşılığı yoktur — hem "-dı" hem "-mış" anlamı verir.

📌 Yapı: have / has + Past Participle (V3)

ÖzneYardımcıKısaltmaOlumsuzÖrnek
I / You / We / Theyhave'vehaven'tI have visited London.
He / She / Ithas'shasn'tShe has never seen snow.

📌 Anahtar Kelimeler — ne zaman kullanılır?

KelimeAnlamPozisyonÖrnek

neverhiç (olumsuz)have + never + V3I've never tried bungee jumping.
alreadyzaten, çoktanhave + already + V3I've already done it.
yethenüz (−/?) cümle sonuI haven't finished yet. / Have you eaten yet?
justaz öncehave + just + V3She's just arrived.
since-den beri (başlangıç)since + time pointI've lived here since 2018.
for-dır/-dir (süre)for + durationHe's studied English for 5 years.
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — Present Perfect vs Simple Past
Present Perfect → zaman belirsiz / deneyim / sonuç:
✅ I have visited Paris. (hayatımda gitmiştim — ne zaman belirsiz)

Simple Past → zaman belirli:
✅ I visited Paris in 2019. (ne zaman belli → Simple Past!)

Kural: yesterday / last week / in 2020 / ago → HEP Simple Past
🚨
IMPORTANT — been vs gone
have been to = oraya gitmiş ve dönmüş:
"I have been to Istanbul." → İstanbul'a gittim ve döndüm.

have gone to = hâlâ orada:
"She has gone to Istanbul." → İstanbul'a gitti, henüz dönmedi.
Bu fark Türkçe'de yoktur — İngilizce'de çok kritiktir!

🔍 Gerçek bağlamda örnekler

Deneyim (ever/never)
Have you ever tried surfing? → Hiç sörf denedin mi?
I've never eaten raw fish. → Hiç çiğ balık yemedim.
She has visited 15 countries. → 15 ülke gezmiş.
Yakın geçmiş (just/already/yet)
I've just got home. → Az önce eve geldim.
She's already eaten. → O zaten yedi.
Have you packed yet? → Bavulunu hazırladın mı?
Süre (for/since)
I've lived here for 10 years. → 10 yıldır burada yaşıyorum.
She's worked here since 2020. → 2020'den beri çalışıyor.
How long have you known her? → Onu ne zamandır tanıyorsun?
💡
TIP — for vs since
for → süreyi söyler: for two years, for a long time, for six months
since → başlangıç noktasını söyler: since 2019, since Monday, since I was a child
Test: "İki yıl" → for / "2022'den beri" → since

Chasing the Northern Lights

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Travel blogger Ece Yıldız has visited 24 countries in her career. But until last February, she had never seen the aurora borealis — the famous northern lights.

"I have always dreamed of seeing them," she wrote on her blog. "Last winter, I finally went to Tromsø, Norway. It was one of the most extraordinary experiences of my life."

The aurora is caused by charged particles from the sun colliding with gases in Earth's atmosphere. Scientists have studied this phenomenon for centuries, but its beauty still astonishes even the most seasoned travellers.

Not everyone who travels to Norway has been lucky. Some visitors have waited for ten days without seeing anything. Ece, however, saw the lights on her very first night. "I have never felt so small and so connected to the universe at the same time," she told us.

Since that trip, she has written about it in three different magazines. The experience changed her perspective on travel completely.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1How many countries has Ece visited in her career?

2Why did she go to Tromsø, Norway last February?

She went to see the aurora borealis / northern lights.

3Were all travellers to Norway as lucky as Ece?

No — some have waited ten days without seeing anything.

4Find 3 Present Perfect verbs and 3 Past Simple verbs in the text.

PP: has visited, have studied, has written / PS: went, saw, changed
