Past Perfect — had + V3

Past Perfect, geçmişte olan iki olaydan önce olanı anlatır. "Ben geldiğimde, o çoktan gitmişti." — "çoktan gitmişti" → Past Perfect. Türkçe'deki "-mıştı/-mişti" ekine karşılık gelir.

📌 Yapı: had + Past Participle (V3)

YapıÖrnekTürkçesi
had + V3 (olumlu)When I arrived, she had already left.Ben geldiğimde o çoktan gitmişti.
hadn't + V3 (olumsuz)He hadn't eaten before the meeting.Toplantıdan önce yemek yememişti.
Had + özne + V3 (soru)Had you met her before?Onu daha önce tanımış mıydın?

📌 Zaman çizelgesi — Hangi eylem önce oldu?

Past Perfect vs Simple Past — sıra önemli!
When I arrived [Simple Past] at the station, the train had already left [Past Perfect].
Önce: tren gitti ✈ → Sonra: ben geldim 🏃
She had studied French for years before she moved to Paris.
Önce: yıllarca Fransızca çalıştı → Sonra: Paris'e taşındı

📌 Sık kullanılan bağlaçlar

BağlaçAnlamÖrnek
when-diğindeWhen she arrived, he had left.
before-den önceI had finished before midnight.
after-den sonraAfter they had eaten, they watched TV.
by the time-e kadarBy the time I arrived, everyone had gone.
alreadyzatenShe had already seen the film.
💡
TIP — Past Perfect her zaman zorunlu değil!
"before" veya "after" kullandığında sıra zaten belli — Past Perfect opsiyoneldir:
✅ "She left before he arrived." (sade Past Simple, sıra belli)
✅ "She had left before he arrived." (Past Perfect — daha vurgulu)
Ama "when" kullandığında dikkat! — "when" olmadan Past Perfect önemli.

The Midnight Train

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

It was the last night of our three-week trip to Lisbon. We had been walking all day and were exhausted. Our train was leaving at midnight — only two hours away.

At 11:30, we were sitting in a small café near the station when my friend's face suddenly went pale. "My bag," he whispered. It had disappeared. Our tickets, our passports, our money — everything had been in that bag.

To make matters worse, it was raining heavily. We ran back to the restaurant where we had had dinner two hours earlier. Nothing. We checked the taxi we had taken from the museum. The driver hadn't seen anything.

By the time we returned to the station, our train had already departed. We stood there in the rain, completely speechless.

Then, all of a sudden, a station guard approached us. "Are you looking for a blue backpack?" he asked. Somebody had found it on a bench and handed it in thirty minutes earlier. Everything was still inside.

We laughed until we cried. We had missed our train. But it didn't matter. We had the best story we would ever bring home from a trip.

ℹ️ Kurgusal metin — kişiler ve olaylar dil öğretimi amacıyla yazılmıştır.

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What time was their train leaving?

Midnight — they had two hours.

2What had disappeared from the café, and why was it so serious?

His bag — it contained their tickets, passports, and money.

3By the time they returned to the station, what had happened?

The train had already departed/left.

4Find one Past Perfect example in the text and explain WHY it is used (what happened earlier).

e.g. 'Had disappeared' — the bag was gone BEFORE he noticed. 'Had had dinner' — they ate dinner BEFORE they returned to look.
