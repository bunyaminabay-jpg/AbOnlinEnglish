Conditionals — If Cümleleri (1. ve 2. Tip)

"Eğer ... olursa, ... olur" yapısı. İngilizce'de iki temel tip vardır: First Conditional (gerçek olabilecek durumlar) ve Second Conditional (hayali/olası olmayan durumlar).

📌 First Conditional — Gerçek durumlar

If clauseMain clauseÖrnek
If + Present Simplewill + V1If it rains, we will stay at home.
If + Present Simplecan/may/might + V1If you study, you can pass the exam.
1st Conditional örnekler
If you heat water, it will boil. (doğa kanunu)
If she calls, I will tell her the news.
Unless you hurry, we will miss the bus.
If I have time, I will help you.

📌 Second Conditional — Hayali/olası olmayan

If clauseMain clauseÖrnek
If + Past Simplewould + V1If I had wings, I would fly to Paris.
If + were (to be)would + V1If I were you, I wouldn't do that.
🔀
DON'T CONFUSE — 1st vs 2nd Conditional
1st Conditional → olabilir, mümkün:
"If it rains tomorrow, I will take an umbrella." (gerçekçi ihtimal)

2nd Conditional → hayal, şu an gerçek değil:
"If I were a bird, I would fly to Africa." (imkânsız/hayal)
"If I had a million dollars, I would travel the world." (şu an yok)
🚨
IMPORTANT — "If I were" (were, was değil!)
2nd Conditional'da "to be" için resmi/yazılı dilde were kullanılır:
✅ If I were you, I would apologise.
✅ If she were here, she would know what to do.
Konuşmada "was" da duyulur ama sınavlarda ve yazılı dilde were tercih edilir.
💡
TIP — Unless = If ... not
Unless = eğer ... olmazsa:
"Unless you study, you won't pass." = "If you don't study, you won't pass."
Unless'ten sonra daima olumlu fiil gelir!

The Algorithm and Your Brain

sarı işaretli kelimeler bu ünitenin hedef yapıları ve kalıplarıdır.

Social media platforms use powerful algorithms to keep you on their apps for as long as possible. If you click on a video about cats, the algorithm will immediately show you more. If you watch content that makes you angry, you will stay online longer — and that is exactly what these companies want.

The problem is becoming serious. A 2019 study of more than 6,500 American teenagers found a clear pattern: if teenagers spend more than three hours a day on social media, they are more likely to report problems such as anxiety and depression. If this pattern continues, many experts warn, young people's mental health will continue to suffer.

Unless we become conscious users of technology, it will control us — not the other way around.

The good news: many young people are fighting back. Some say that when they delete certain apps, they feel calmer and sleep better.

Experts recommend simple rules: before you go to bed, switch off your phone. After you wake up, wait at least 30 minutes before checking social media. If you follow these guidelines, you will probably feel the difference.

ℹ️ Gerçek olay ve araştırmalara dayanır; ders amacıyla sadeleştirilmiştir.

📚 Kaynaklar

  • Riehm vd. (2019), JAMA Psychiatry — Johns Hopkins özeti

📝 Comprehension Questions · Anlama soruları

1What do social media algorithms do?

They show you more of what you click on, keeping you online longer.

2According to the 2019 study, what happens if teenagers spend 3+ hours a day on social media?

They are more likely to report mental health problems such as anxiety and depression.

3Find one zero conditional and one first conditional in the text. What does each express?

Zero: 'when they delete certain apps, they feel calmer' (general experience) / First: 'If you follow these guidelines, you will probably feel the difference' (possible future result).

4What two morning/evening rules do experts recommend?

Switch off phone before bed / Wait 30+ minutes before checking social media after waking up.
