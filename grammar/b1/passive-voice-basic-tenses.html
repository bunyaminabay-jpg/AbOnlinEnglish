Passive Voice — Basic Tenses

Focusing on the action, not the doer: be + past participle.

Edilgen Çatı (Temel Zamanlar)

1Quick Understanding

Passive voice, cümlenin odağını eylemi yapandan eylemden etkilenene kaydırır. Kim yaptığı önemli değilse ya da zaten biliniyorsa passive tercih edilir.

2The Structure

Formula

be (uygun zamanda) + V3
Etken: My sister ate the cake. → Edilgen: The cake was eaten (by my sister).
Active
My sister ate the cake.
→
Passive
The cake was eaten (by my sister).

Affirmative / Negative / Question

Affirmative

English is spoken here.The letter was sent.

Negative

English isn't spoken here.The letter wasn't sent.

Question

Is English spoken here?Was the letter sent?

Word Order

Object(active) — be — V3 — (by + agent)

ZamanActivePassive
Present SimpleThey clean the office every day.The office is cleaned every day.
Present ContinuousThey are cleaning the office now.The office is being cleaned now.
Past SimpleThey cleaned the office yesterday.The office was cleaned yesterday.
Present PerfectThey have cleaned the office.The office has been cleaned.
Future (will)They will clean the office.The office will be cleaned.
Modal (can)They can clean the office today.The office can be cleaned today.

3How It Works

When do we use it?

  • Eylemi yapan bilinmiyor: My wallet was stolen.
  • Eylemi yapan önemli değil: This product is made in Turkey.
  • Bilimsel/resmi/haber dili: The results were announced yesterday.
  • Etkilenen kişi/şey cümlenin odağı: The bridge was built in 1932.

When DON'T we use it?

⚠️
Eylemi yapan kişi CÜMLENİN ASIL ODAĞIYSA passive kullanma — active daha doğal ve nettir.
❌ The cake was baked by my mother for my birthday. (eğer asıl mesaj "annem" ise) → ✅ My mother baked the cake for my birthday.

Short Answers

Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. — Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.
Türkçe Mantığı

Zihninde şu sırayla ilerle:

TÜRKÇEDE NE DEMEK İSTİYORUM? → "Bu kitap 1997'de yazıldı." (kim yazdığı önemli değil)
↓
CÜMLEDE ODAK NEREDE? → Eylemi yapan mı önemli, eylemden etkilenen mi?
↓
ETKİLENEN ÖNEMLİYSE → PASSIVE (be + V3)
↓
CÜMLE → "This book was written in 1997."

4Examples

  • This car was made in Germany.Bu araba Almanya'da yapıldı.
  • English is spoken in many countries.İngilizce birçok ülkede konuşulur.
  • The bridge is being repaired this month.Köprü bu ay tamir ediliyor.
  • The email has already been sent.E-posta zaten gönderildi.
  • The results will be announced tomorrow.Sonuçlar yarın açıklanacak.
  • My phone was stolen last night.Telefonum dün gece çalındı.
  • This song was written by a famous composer.Bu şarkı ünlü bir besteci tarafından yazıldı.
  • The report wasn't finished on time.Rapor zamanında bitirilmedi.
  • Is this room cleaned every day?Bu oda her gün temizleniyor mu?
  • The new policy hasn't been approved yet.Yeni politika henüz onaylanmadı.

5Compare & Notice

Compare With: Active vs Passive

Active

Eylemi yapan cümlenin odağı.

My sister ate the cake.

VS

Passive

Eylemden etkilenen cümlenin odağı.

The cake was eaten (by my sister).

6Common Mistakes

❌ The window was broke by the boy.
✅ The window was broken by the boy.
WHY? Passive'de V2 değil V3 (past participle) kullanılır: break → broken.
❌ English is speaking in many countries.
✅ English is spoken in many countries.
WHY? Passive formülü be + V3'tür, be + V-ing değil (o passive continuous farklı bir yapı: is being spoken).
❌ The report was written by someone.
✅ The report was written.
WHY? Eylemi yapan bilinmiyor/önemli değilse "by someone/by people" gibi belirsiz agent'lar genelde ATILIR.
❌ The letter was wrote yesterday.
✅ The letter was written yesterday.
TÜRKÇE MANTIK: Düzensiz fiillerin V2 (wrote) ve V3 (written) hâlleri farklıdır — passive'de her zaman V3 kullanılır.

10Sık Sorulan Sorular

Passive ne zaman kullanılır?

Eylemi yapan bilinmiyorsa, önemli değilse ya da odak etkilenen şeyse: My wallet was stolen. This product is made in Turkey.

"by" her zaman gerekir mi?

Hayır. Sadece eylemi yapanı söylemek önemliyse: The book was written by Orhan Pamuk. "by someone/people" gereksizdir.
