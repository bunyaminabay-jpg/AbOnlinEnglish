DERS 1Welcome to Theme 7: Conflict — Everyday Disagreements40 dk
Kazanım
L1, V1
Hedef dil
conflict, dispute, misunderstanding, compromise, mediator, perspective
Kelimeler
conflict, dispute, misunderstanding, compromise, mediator, perspective
Materyal
Theme 7 poster/slide, Short scenario cards describing everyday conflicts
Hazırlık
Prepare 4–5 short scenario cards describing everyday conflicts (neighbours, classmates, colleagues, family).
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair talk for use of new words.
Ödev
Look at the Vocabulary tab and read the first 10 words.

Akış

  1. 5 Warm-up: show the scenario cards. 'What do these situations have in common?' (Whole class)
  2. 10 Present the theme roadmap: everyday conflicts, reported speech, conditionals for solutions, different perspectives. (Whole class)
  3. 15 Pre-teach conflict, dispute, misunderstanding, compromise, mediator, perspective with examples; students note down. (Whole class → pairs)
  4. 10 Pair talk: 'One conflict I've seen or experienced was about… because…' (Pairs)
DestekGive sentence starters on the board.
Hedef6 target words, roadmap, pair talk.
İleriFast finishers predict one solution-offering conditional sentence they expect to use in this theme.

Site: Grammar tab, Notice sentences slide

Ölçme

Ödev

Akış

  1. 8 Notice: show the 4 sentences, ask the Notice question, elicit ideas. (Whole class)
  2. 12 Discover: complete the rule using the word bank. (Pairs)
  3. 12 Build: complete the 5 cued sentences, check answers together. (Individual → whole class)
  4. 8 Your Turn: write 4 reported-speech sentences about a disagreement; share with a partner. (Pairs)
DestekGive the rule box as a reference card.
HedefNotice, Discover, Build.
İleriFast finishers try 2–3 Challenge questions.

  3. 15 Read R1 and answer the comprehension questions in pairs. (Pairs)
  4. 10 Whole-class check and short discussion: 'Why does understanding a real need help solve a conflict?' (Whole class)

HedefDiscover + Build for Grammar 2, gist + comprehension questions for R1.
