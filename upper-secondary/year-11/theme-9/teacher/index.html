Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Introducing Friendship Vocabulary40 min dk
Kazanım
V1, L1, S1
Hedef dil
Vocabulary for describing friends and friendship (loyal, reliable, trustworthy, get along with...)
Kelimeler
acquaintance, loyal, reliable, trustworthy, bond, companion, outgoing, sincere, get along with, have in common
Materyal
Vocabulary handout, Board, Photos of people to describe
Hazırlık
Prepare a short vocabulary handout with the 10 new words and example sentences.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair descriptions and checks vocabulary matching activity.
Ödev
Learn the 10 new words and write one original sentence for each.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: students brainstorm words that describe a good friend. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new vocabulary with example sentences and Turkish translations. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Students match words to definitions in pairs. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Students describe a friend orally to a partner using at least three new words. (pairs)
  5. 5 min Whole-class feedback and correction of common errors. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word bank and sentence starters for weaker students.
HedefStudents use at least three new words accurately in their description.
İleriStudents add a second friend and compare the two using new vocabulary.
DERS 2Noticing Relative Clauses40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, R1
Hedef dil
Defining relative clauses with who/which/that/whose
Kelimeler
who, which, that, whose, friend, classmate
Materyal
Notice sentences on the board, Reading text r1 (first two paragraphs)
Hazırlık
Prepare the notice sentences from grammar point g1 on slides or the board.
Ölçme
Teacher checks discover activity answers for accuracy.
Ödev
Complete the remaining 'complete' items from g1 not finished in class.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: show four notice sentences and ask students what the marked words have in common. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Guide students to notice that who/which/whose follow a noun and add information about it. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Read the first two paragraphs of r1 aloud and highlight relative clauses together. (T-S)
  4. 10 min Students complete the 'complete' discover activity from g1 individually, then check in pairs. (individual/pairs)
  5. 5 min Whole-class check of answers and quick error correction. (T-S)
DestekProvide the answers as a matching exercise instead of open gaps.
HedefStudents correctly identify and complete relative pronoun gaps.
İleriStudents find and underline two more relative clauses elsewhere in the reading text.
DERS 3Building Sentences with Relative Pronouns40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, W3
Hedef dil
Combining two sentences with who/which/that/whose
Kelimeler
combine, sentence, relative clause
Materyal
Build activity cue cards from g1, Worksheet with sentence pairs
Hazırlık
Print or prepare cue cards for the 'build' discover activity.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair work and reviews combined sentences for accuracy.
Ödev
Write three original sentence pairs about friends and combine them at home.

Akış

  1. 5 min Review who/which/that/whose with a quick oral drill. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Model combining two sentences into one using the example from the build activity. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Students work through the 'build' cues in pairs, combining sentences. (pairs)
  4. 5 min Pairs share two of their combined sentences with the class. (pairs/T-S)
  5. 5 min Teacher corrects common combining errors on the board. (T-S)
DestekProvide sentence halves already partly joined, leaving only the pronoun blank.
HedefStudents accurately combine at least four sentence pairs.
İleriStudents write two of their own original sentence pairs and combine them.
DERS 4Where, When, Why in Relative Clauses40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, V1
Hedef dil
Relative adverbs where/when/why
Kelimeler
gathering, reunion, childhood friend
Materyal
Board, Sentence strips with where/when/why gaps
Hazırlık
Prepare sentence strips for a where/when/why sorting activity.
Ölçme
Teacher checks group sorting and the final gap-fill for accuracy.
Ödev
Complete three gap-fill sentences using where/when/why from the practice set.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: elicit a place, a time, and a reason connected to friendship from students. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present where/when/why with example sentences from the notice section. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Students sort sentence strips into where/when/why categories in small groups. (groups)
  4. 5 min Groups present one sentence from each category. (groups/T-S)
  5. 5 min Quick individual gap-fill check using new vocabulary and relative adverbs. (individual)
DestekReduce the number of sentence strips and provide clearer category headers.
HedefStudents correctly sort and produce sentences with where/when/why.
İleriStudents write one extra sentence using 'why' about a personal friendship event.
DERS 5Defining vs. Non-defining Clauses40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, R2
Hedef dil
Comma use in defining vs. non-defining relative clauses
Kelimeler
essential information, extra information
Materyal
Compare table from g2, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare the compare table from grammar point g2 on the board or a handout.
Ölçme
Teacher reviews pair answers for correct comma use.
Ödev
Complete the g2 practice items not finished in class.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: show two similar sentences, one with commas and one without, and ask what's different. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present the rule: defining = essential, no commas; non-defining = extra, commas. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Students complete the discover 'complete' activity from g2 in pairs. (pairs)
  4. 5 min Pairs check answers with another pair. (pairs)
  5. 5 min Whole-class review of tricky items and common mistakes. (T-S)
DestekHighlight already-named people (like 'my mother') as a clue for non-defining clauses.
HedefStudents correctly identify defining vs. non-defining in most items.
İleriStudents create two of their own sentences, one defining and one non-defining.
DERS 6Reading: The Friend Who Never Gave Up on Me40 min dk
Kazanım
R1, R2, R3, V1
Hedef dil
Relative clauses in context; inferring attitude
Kelimeler

Print or display the full reading text r1 with its questions.
Ölçme
Teacher checks reading comprehension question answers.
Ödev
Write a two-sentence summary of the reading text using at least one relative clause.

Akış

  1. 5 min Pre-reading: discuss the before-reading prompts as a class. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Students read the text silently and underline relative clauses they find. (individual)
  3. 15 min Students answer the reading questions individually, then compare in pairs. (individual/pairs)
  4. 5 min Whole-class check of answers with reference to the text. (T-S)
  5. 5 min Discuss the after-reading questions as a class. (T-S)
DestekPre-teach two or three difficult words before reading and allow pair reading.
HedefStudents answer at least four of six questions correctly.
İleriStudents identify and explain two examples of non-defining clauses in the text.
DERS 7Reading: Our Ten-Year Reunion40 min dk
Kazanım

DERS 8Listening and Asking for Clarification40 min dk
Kazanım
L1, L2, L3, L4, S2, S3
Hedef dil
Phrases for asking for and giving clarification
Kelimeler
clarify, explain further, rephrase, in other words, get confused, make sense
Materyal
Listening transcript l1 (read aloud or recorded), Question handout
Hazırlık
Prepare to read the l1 dialogue aloud with clear speaker changes, or record it beforehand.
Ölçme
Teacher checks listening comprehension answers and monitors pair practice.
Ödev
Write three original clarification questions you could ask a friend.

Akış

  1. 5 min Pre-listening: discuss the pre-listening prompts as a class. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Play/read the dialogue once; students listen for general meaning. (individual)
  3. 10 min Play/read again; students answer the listening questions. (individual)
  4. 10 min In pairs, students list the clarification phrases they heard and practise a short exchange using them. (pairs)
  5. 5 min Whole-class check of answers and clarification phrases. (T-S)
DestekProvide a transcript excerpt with clarification phrases highlighted.
HedefStudents correctly answer at least three of five listening questions and identify two clarification phrases.
İleriStudents write one extra line of dialogue continuing the conversation, using a clarification phrase.
DERS 9Speaking: Describing a Friend and Asking for Clarification40 min dk
Kazanım
S1, S2, S3, P1
Hedef dil
Describing people with relative clauses; clarification phrases
Kelimeler
loyal, generous, supportive, confide in, clarify, rephrase
Materyal
Speaking task s1 handout, Useful language list
Hazırlık
Prepare a short model description using relative clauses to demonstrate.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair speaking and notes use of relative clauses and clarification language.
Ödev
Write the description you gave orally as a short paragraph.

Akış

  1. 5 min Model the task: teacher describes a friend using relative clauses; students listen for clarity. (T-S)
  2. 5 min Students note down three facts about a friend they will describe. (individual)
  3. 20 min Students complete speaking task s1 in pairs, describing friends and asking for clarification. (pairs)
  4. 5 min Two or three pairs perform a short version for the class. (T-S)
  5. 5 min Teacher gives feedback on pronunciation and clause use. (T-S)
DestekGive students a simple template: name / who.../ where.../ why I like them.
HedefStudents use at least three relative clauses and one clarification exchange.
İleriStudents add a comparison between two friends using non-defining clauses.
