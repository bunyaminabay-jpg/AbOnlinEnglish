Hobbies and Skills

Kitap: English 11th Grade (Yıldırım Yayınları, MEB Ortaöğretim Ders Kitabı) — Ders Kitabı + Öğrenci Çalışma Kitabı · Öğrenci sayfası sade tutulur; resmî bilgiler, planlar ve cevaplar burada.

DersteTahtaya yansıt ya da ekran paylaş▶ Sınıf Modunu açÖğrenci sayfası
Bu yıl önceki program işleniyor. Resmî 2026–2027 yıllık planına göre normal 11. sınıfta bu yıl önceki program (Yıldırım Yayınları ders kitabı, 10 tema) işleniyor. 3 adımda derse hazırlan

  2. Sınıf Modunu tahtada aç: kelimeler, gramer, okuma, sorular adım adım; sayaç, rastgele öğrenci, takım puanı hazır.
  3. İndir sekmesinden çalışma kâğıdını ve cevap anahtarını al; internet yoksa Sınıf Modu’nun çevrimdışı dosyasını kullan.

Sekme 4 / 8

Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Hobbies and Skills: Getting Started40 min dk
Kazanım
V1, S1
Hedef dil
Hobby vocabulary and basic questions about free-time activities
Kelimeler
hobby, pastime, enthusiast, leisure, pursuit
Materyal
Vocabulary cards, Whiteboard
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary cards for hobby-related words.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair work and checks vocabulary use.
Ödev
Match 8 hobby vocabulary words to their Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: students brainstorm hobbies they know in English. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new vocabulary with original example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Pair work: students ask and answer 'What do you do in your free time?' (pairs)
  4. 10 min Feedback and quick vocabulary check. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word list with Turkish translations.
HedefStudents create their own example sentences with new words.
İleriStudents add two extra hobby words of their own.
DERS 2Verbs of Liking and Disliking40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, S2
Hedef dil
enjoy, love, hate, can't stand + -ing
Kelimeler
enjoy, love, hate, can't stand, collect
Materyal
Grammar handout, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare notice sentences and gap-fill handout for g1.
Ölçme
Teacher checks gap-fill answers and monitors spoken sentences.
Ödev
Complete 5 gap-fill sentences using enjoy, love, hate, or can't stand + -ing.

Akış

  1. 5 min Show notice sentences with enjoy/love/hate/can't stand and elicit the pattern. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Explain the rule: these verbs are followed by -ing. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Complete and build exercises from grammar point g1. (individual/pairs)
  4. 10 min Students share 2-3 original sentences about their own likes/dislikes. (pairs)
DestekProvide sentence starters for the build exercise.
HedefStudents complete all practice items independently.
İleriStudents write two extra sentences using a new hobby verb.
DERS 3Talking About Preferences40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, S3, V1
Hedef dil
prefer... to..., would rather
Kelimeler
prefer, board game, craft, achievement
Materyal
Picture cards of hobbies, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare pairs of hobby picture cards for comparison practice.
Ölçme
Teacher listens for correct preference structures during pair work.
Ödev
Write 4 sentences comparing hobbies using prefer... to...

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: show two hobby pictures and ask which students prefer. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present preference structures with original example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Pair work: students compare hobbies using 'I prefer... to... because...' (pairs)
  4. 10 min A few pairs share their preferences with the class. (T-S)
DestekGive a sentence frame: 'I prefer ___ to ___ because ___.'
HedefStudents form their own comparisons without a frame.
İleriStudents compare three hobbies instead of two.
DERS 4Adjective + Preposition: fond of, keen on40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, V1
Hedef dil
fond of, keen on + -ing
Kelimeler
fond of, keen on, gardening, knitting
Materyal
Grammar handout, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare notice sentences and complete/build exercises for the first half of g2.
Ölçme
Teacher checks preposition accuracy during gap-fill and speaking.
Ödev
Write 2 sentences using fond of and 2 using keen on.

Akış

  1. 5 min Show notice sentences with fond of and keen on and elicit the pattern. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Explain the rule: fixed preposition + -ing. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Complete exercise: gap-fill with correct prepositions. (individual)
  4. 10 min Pair work: students tell a partner what they are fond of/keen on. (pairs)
DestekProvide a table matching each adjective to its preposition.
HedefStudents complete the gap-fill independently.
İleriStudents write two extra sentences with a new hobby.
DERS 5Adjective + Preposition: interested in, crazy about40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, V1, R1
Hedef dil
interested in, crazy about + -ing
Kelimeler
interested in, crazy about, sculpting, juggling
Materyal
Grammar handout, Short text extract
Hazırlık
Prepare build exercise and a short teacher-written text using target adjectives.
Ölçme
Teacher checks underlined phrases and build exercise answers.
Ödev
Write 4 sentences using fond of, keen on, interested in, and crazy about.

Akış

  1. 5 min Review fond of/keen on, then introduce interested in/crazy about. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Build exercise: students form full sentences from cues. (individual)
  3. 15 min Students read a short original text and underline adjective + preposition phrases. (individual/pairs)
  4. 10 min Class check and discussion of answers. (T-S)
DestekHighlight the target phrases in the text in advance for weaker students.
HedefStudents find all target phrases independently.
İleriStudents write a sentence for each of the four adjective + preposition structures.

Kelimeler

  4. 5 min Class feedback on both texts. (T-S)
DestekProvide a short vocabulary list for both texts in advance.
HedefStudents complete both sets of questions independently.
İleriStudents compare the two stories in 2-3 original sentences.

Criteria1 - Beginning2 - Developing3 - Proficient
VocabularyUses very few hobby/skill wordsUses some hobby/skill words with minor errorsUses a wide range of hobby/skill vocabulary accurately
Grammar (likes/preferences/can-could)Frequent errors that affect understandingSome errors but meaning is clearAccurate and natural use of target structures
FluencyLong pauses, difficult to followGenerally understandable with some hesitationSpeaks smoothly with little hesitation
Writing Rubric: Hobby and Skill Paragraph
Criteria1 - Beginning2 - Developing3 - Proficient
ContentIdeas are unclear or off-topicIdeas are mostly clear and on-topicIdeas are clear, relevant, and well-developed
GrammarFrequent errors with liking/preference/can-could structuresSome errors but overall accurateConsistently accurate use of target structures
OrganisationNo clear structureSome logical orderClear paragraph structure with logical flow

Criteria1 - Beginning2 - Developing3 - Proficient

Language useLimited range of theme vocabulary/grammarReasonable range with occasional errorsWide, accurate range of theme vocabulary/grammar
