Back to the Past

Kitap: English 11th Grade (Yıldırım Yayınları, MEB Ortaöğretim Ders Kitabı) — Ders Kitabı + Öğrenci Çalışma Kitabı · Öğrenci sayfası sade tutulur; resmî bilgiler, planlar ve cevaplar burada.

DersteTahtaya yansıt ya da ekran paylaş▶ Sınıf Modunu açÖğrenci sayfası

  3. İndir sekmesinden çalışma kâğıdını ve cevap anahtarını al; internet yoksa Sınıf Modu’nun çevrimdışı dosyasını kullan.

Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Introducing Unreal Past Situations40 min dk
Kazanım
L1, V1
Hedef dil
regret, turning point, opportunity, what if...?
Kelimeler
regret, turning point, opportunity, decision, outcome
Materyal
Board, Vocabulary cards, Projector (optional)
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary cards and a short list of 'what if' questions to open discussion.
Ölçme
Teacher checks vocabulary matching answers and listens for correct use of new words.
Ödev
Match 8 vocabulary words to their Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask students to share one small decision that changed their week. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present key vocabulary (regret, turning point, opportunity) with example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Pair work: match vocabulary to Turkish meanings and create one sentence each. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Introduce the theme topic with 2-3 'what if' questions about famous events. (whole class)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up: students predict what grammar structure will be needed to talk about unreal pasts. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word bank and Turkish translations on the board.
HedefStudents form original sentences with the vocabulary unprompted.
İleriStudents explain the difference between two similar vocabulary items (e.g. regret vs. mistake).
DERS 2Noticing the Third Conditional40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, R1
Hedef dil
If + had + past participle, would/could/might + have + past participle
Kelimeler
outcome, decision, alternative
Materyal
Board, Grammar notice handout
Hazırlık
Prepare the notice sentences and noticing question on the board or a handout.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair discussions and reviews gap-fill answers together.
Ödev
Complete 4 third conditional sentences using the given word bank.

Akış

  1. 5 min Review: elicit vocabulary from Lesson 1 through quick Q&A. (whole class)
  2. 10 min Present notice sentences for the third conditional; ask the noticing question. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Guided discovery: students complete gap-fill items from the grammar section. (individual)
  4. 10 min Pair check and feedback on the discovery task. (pairs)
  5. 5 min Teacher summarises the rule in English and Turkish. (T-S)
DestekProvide the answer bank alongside the gap-fill items.
HedefStudents attempt items without the bank first, then check.
İleriStudents explain the rule to a partner in their own words.
DERS 3Building Third Conditional Sentences40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, P1
Hedef dil
would have / could have / might have + past participle
Kelimeler
hesitate, consequence, risk
Materyal
Board, Cue cards
Hazırlık
Prepare cue cards for the sentence-building activity.
Ölçme
Exit ticket sentences checked for correct form before the next lesson.
Ödev
Write 6 original third conditional sentences about real events from your own life.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: quick oral drill of would have / could have / might have with reduced pronunciation. (whole class)
  2. 15 min Build activity: students construct full sentences from cue cards. (pairs)
  3. 10 min Practice items from the grammar section, checked as a class. (individual then whole class)
  4. 5 min Mistakes review: correct the two common error sentences from the grammar section. (T-S)
  5. 5 min Exit ticket: students write one original third conditional sentence on paper. (individual)
DestekProvide sentence frames with blanks for the exit ticket.
HedefStudents complete the exit ticket without frames.
İleriStudents write two connected third conditional sentences describing a chain of events.

DERS 7Listening: A Conversation About Regrets40 min dk
Kazanım
L2, L3, L4, P1

Ölçme
Teacher reviews comprehension answers and models pronunciation of linked forms.
Ödev
Interview a family member about one regret they have and report it in writing using target structures.

Akış

  4. 10 min Whole-class discussion using the post-listening prompts. (whole class)
DestekProvide the transcript in writing for students who need extra support.
HedefStudents answer from listening alone before checking the transcript.
İleriStudents identify every regret expressed in the dialogue and rephrase it in their own words.

Reading15-20 minPairsNone

Listening15 minGroups of 3Phones (optional recording)

Regret Radio Show
