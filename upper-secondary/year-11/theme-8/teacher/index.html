Sekme 4 / 8

Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Theme Introduction & Sports Vocabulary40 min dk
Kazanım
V1, P1
Hedef dil
Sports and competition vocabulary
Kelimeler
championship, opponent, referee, coach, athlete, victory, defeat, stadium
Materyal
Vocabulary list, Board, Flashcards
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary flashcards and a warm-up question list about students' favourite sports.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors vocabulary use during pair work and discussion.
Ödev
Learn the 8 new vocabulary words for a short quiz.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask students about their favourite sports and athletes. (T-S)
  2. 15 min Present new sports vocabulary with flashcards and example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Pair work: students test each other on the new words. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Class discussion: brainstorm words related to sports news and interviews. (whole class)
DestekProvide a picture-word matching worksheet.
HedefStudents use each new word in an original sentence.
İleriStudents group the new words into categories (people, places, results).
DERS 2More Sports Vocabulary & Pronunciation40 min dk
Kazanım
V1, P1
Hedef dil
Sports vocabulary continued; reporting-related verbs
Kelimeler
report, interview, commentator, supporter, tournament, announce, claim, admit, explain, deny
Materyal
Vocabulary list, Audio examples (optional), Board
Hazırlık
Prepare short example sentences for pronunciation drilling.
Ölçme
Teacher checks pronunciation and correct word use during drilling and pair work.
Ödev
Complete vocabulary exercise sheet.

Akış

  1. 5 min Quick recap quiz on Lesson 1 vocabulary. (whole class)
  2. 15 min Present reporting-related vocabulary (report, admit, claim, deny, etc.) with word stress practice. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Pronunciation drilling and choral repetition. (whole class)
  4. 10 min Pair activity: students create short sentences using the new words. (pairs)
DestekProvide a word list with simplified phonetic spelling.
HedefStudents write one sentence per new word.
İleriStudents identify which words are also useful for reported speech (say, tell, admit, claim, deny, explain).
DERS 3Grammar: Reported Speech — Statements (Noticing)40 min dk
Kazanım
G1
Hedef dil
Reported speech — tense back-shift
Kelimeler
said, told, back-shift-related vocabulary
Materyal
Grammar notice sentences, Board, Worksheet
Hazırlık
Prepare the notice sentences and gap-fill worksheet for Grammar 1.
Ölçme
Teacher reviews gap-fill answers for correct tense back-shift.
Ödev
Complete 5 extra tense back-shift gap-fill sentences.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: write a direct quote on the board and ask how students would tell a friend what was said. (whole class)
  2. 10 min Present notice sentences; ask noticeQ to elicit the tense pattern. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Guided practice: complete gap-fill items from Grammar 1's 'complete' activity. (individual then pairs)
  4. 10 min Check answers as a class and clarify the rule (rule_en/rule). (whole class)
DestekProvide a tense back-shift chart as reference.
HedefStudents complete all gap-fill items independently.
İleriStudents explain the rule in their own words to a partner.
DERS 4Grammar: Reported Speech — Building Sentences40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, W6
Hedef dil
Reported speech — building full sentences from cues
Kelimeler
reporting verbs: say, tell
Materyal
Cue cards, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare the 'build' cue cards from Grammar 1.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair work and reviews sentences for correct form.
Ödev
Write 3 original reported sentences about a sports event.

Akış

  1. 5 min Quick review of tense back-shift rules from Lesson 3. (whole class)
  2. 15 min Model building full reported sentences from cues (example + guided cues). (T-S)
  3. 15 min Pair work: students build reported sentences from remaining cues. (pairs)
  4. 5 min Class check and correction of common mistakes. (whole class)
DestekProvide model sentences alongside cues.
HedefStudents build all cue sentences correctly.
İleriStudents write two of their own cue-based reported sentences.
DERS 5Grammar: Reported Speech — Pronouns & Time Expressions40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, P1
Hedef dil
Reported speech — pronoun and time/place expression changes
Kelimeler
here/there, today/that day, tomorrow/the next day, yesterday/the day before
Materyal
Grammar 2 notice sentences, Compare table, Worksheet
Hazırlık
Prepare the compare table and gap-fill worksheet for Grammar 2.
Ölçme
Teacher checks worksheet answers for correct pronoun/time changes.
Ödev
Complete practice items 1-6 from Grammar 2.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: elicit what changes besides the verb tense when reporting speech. (whole class)
  2. 15 min Present Grammar 2 notice sentences and the compare table (direct vs reported time/place words). (T-S)
  3. 15 min Guided and pair practice: complete gap-fill and build activities. (pairs)
  4. 5 min Review common mistakes (say vs tell, missing pronoun changes). (whole class)
DestekProvide the compare table as a takeaway reference sheet.
HedefStudents complete gap-fill and build activities independently.
İleriStudents create 2 original sentences using 'tell' with correct pronoun/time changes.

  4. 10 min Highlight and discuss reported speech examples found in the text. (whole class)
DestekProvide simplified pre-teaching of key vocabulary before reading.
HedefStudents answer all comprehension questions independently.

Akış

  1. 5 min Pre-reading discussion: what challenges might a young athlete face? (whole class)
  2. 10 min Silent reading of the interview text. (individual)
  3. 15 min Answer comprehension questions in pairs; check as a class. (pairs then whole class)
  4. 10 min Discuss Deniz's attitude and values based on the text (inference). (whole class)
DestekProvide a simplified vocabulary glossary alongside the text.
HedefStudents answer all questions and justify one inference with text evidence.

DERS 9Speaking: Athlete Interview Role-Play40 min dk
Kazanım

  2. 20 min Pairs conduct the journalist/athlete role-play (Speaking 1), swapping roles. (pairs)

DestekGive weaker pairs ready-made questions and sentence starters.

DERS 10Speaking: Fair Play Debate & Writing: News Report40 min dk
Kazanım
S4, S5, S6, W1, W2

Speaking task s2 instructions, Writing task w1 planning sheet
Hazırlık
Prepare discussion prompts for the fair play debate and the news report planning sheet.
Ölçme

Write the full sports news report (Writing 1) at home.

Akış

  1. 15 min Groups discuss fair play vs winning at all costs (Speaking 2) and prepare a summary. (groups)
  2. 10 min Groups present summaries, quoting one member's opinion in reported speech. (whole class)
  3. 15 min Introduce Writing 1 (sports news report); students plan their report using the planning steps. (individual)
DestekProvide an opinion sentence frame and vocabulary list for the debate.
HedefStudents give reasoned opinions and complete a full writing plan.
İleriStudents compare fair play rules across two sports in the debate.

  1. 10 min Review sports news reports from homework in pairs; peer feedback using the checklist. (pairs)

DestekProvide a model interview script as a reference.
HedefStudents complete the full interview script and summary independently.
İleriStudents add a third reporting verb variation and a closing opinion sentence.
