In this theme, students describe past places, people and events, put past events in order, and talk about personal experiences using the Past Perfect tense.

Konular

  • Describing places, people and events in the past
  • Ordering events
  • Talking about personal experiences in the past

Dil yapıları

  • Past Perfect (had + past participle)

Anlayabileceğim

  • I can understand a short biography describing someone's past life and achievements.
  • I can understand a story where events are told out of chronological order.
  • I can understand a description of a past place or event with details.
  • I can understand people talking about experiences that happened before another past event.
  • I can understand the difference between two past actions in a conversation.
  • I can understand a diary entry recounting a memorable day.
  • I can follow a narrative that uses time markers like 'before', 'after' and 'by the time'.

Söyleyebileceğim

  • I can describe a place I visited in the past.
  • I can talk about a memorable event from my past.
  • I can explain what had happened before something else happened.
  • I can put a sequence of past events in the correct order when speaking.
  • I can describe a person I remember from the past.
  • I can share a personal experience and explain its background.
  • I can ask someone about something that had already happened before an event.
  • I can compare two past situations using appropriate time expressions.

Yazabileceğim

  • I can write a short narrative about a past event using the Past Perfect where needed.
  • I can write a description of a memorable place or experience from my past.
  • I can write a short biography paragraph describing someone's earlier life.
  • I can write sentences that clearly show which past action happened first.

Past Perfect (had + past participle)

1 · Notice
  • When we arrived at the cinema, the film had already started.
  • She had never visited Istanbul before she moved there last year.
  • By the time he was twenty, he had lived in three different countries.
  • I couldn't call you because I had lost my phone earlier that day.

What do you notice about the verb form 'had + past participle', and which action happened first in each sentence?

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

Complete each sentence with the Past Perfect form of the verb in brackets.

finishleavenever seealready eatnot visitclose

Well done — you can now build the Past Perfect with regular and irregular verbs!

B Build sentences

Use the cues to build a full sentence with the Past Perfect.

  1. she finish homework before dinner

    ✓ She had finished her homework before dinner.

  2. he already leave when I called
    Model answer

    He had already left when I called.

  3. we never try that food before the trip
    Model answer

    We had never tried that food before the trip.

  4. the teacher explain the topic before the test
    Model answer

    The teacher had explained the topic before the test.

  5. they not finish the project by Friday
    Model answer

    They had not finished the project by Friday.

  6. I read the book before watching the film
    Model answer

    I had read the book before watching the film.

  7. she live abroad before she returned home
    Model answer

    She had lived abroad before she returned home.

C Your turn

Write 2-3 original sentences about your own life using the Past Perfect to show which past action happened first.

By the time I started high school, I had already...Before I met my best friend, I had...When I was ten, I had never...
Rule

We form the Past Perfect with had + the past participle of the verb. We use it for an action that happened before a specific past time, or before another past action.

TRPast Perfect, had + fiilin üçüncü hali (past participle) ile oluşturulur. Belirli bir geçmiş zamandan önce ya da geçmişteki başka bir olaydan önce gerçekleşen bir eylemi anlatmak için kullanılır.

When?

We use the Past Perfect to show the earlier of two past actions, often with time markers like 'before', 'after', 'already', 'by the time', 'when' and 'never...before'.

TRİki geçmiş eylemden daha önce gerçekleşeni belirtmek için Past Perfect kullanılır; genellikle 'before', 'after', 'already', 'by the time', 'when' ve 'never...before' gibi zaman ifadeleriyle birlikte kullanılır.

3 · Form

Positive

  • She had finished the report before the meeting started.
  • They had already left when we arrived.

Negative

  • He had not seen the message before he replied.
  • We hadn't visited that city before last summer.

Question

  • Had you finished your homework before dinner?
  • Had she ever travelled abroad before that trip?

Short answers

  • Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
  • Yes, she had. / No, she hadn't.
Mistakes
  • When I arrived, the film already started.When I arrived, the film had already started.

    Use the Past Perfect, not Simple Past, for the action that happened first.

    TRÖnce gerçekleşen eylem için Simple Past değil, Past Perfect kullanılmalıdır.

  • She had went to Paris before she moved to London.She had gone to Paris before she moved to London.

    Use the correct past participle form (gone), not the Simple Past form (went).

    TRDoğru past participle formu (gone) kullanılmalıdır, Simple Past formu (went) değil.

  • By the time he was twenty, he lived in three countries.By the time he was twenty, he had lived in three countries.

    'By the time' with a past time usually signals the Past Perfect for the completed earlier action.

    TR'By the time' ifadesiyle geçmişte tamamlanmış bir eylem genellikle Past Perfect ile anlatılır.

Compare
Past Perfect vs Simple Past
Past PerfectSimple Past
UseThe earlier of two past actionsShe had left before I arrived.A single completed past action or the later actionI arrived at six o'clock.
Time focusBefore another past pointHe had finished the book by Monday.At a specific past timeHe finished the book on Monday.

Past Perfect with time expressions (before, after, already, by the time, never...before)

1 · Notice
  • Before she moved to the city, she had lived in a small village all her life.
  • He felt proud because he had already finished the marathon once before.
  • By the time the guests arrived, we had prepared everything.
  • It was the first time she had ever seen the ocean.

Which time expressions help you decide when to use the Past Perfect instead of the Simple Past?

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

Complete each sentence with the correct time expression from the bank.

beforeby the timealreadynever...beforeafter

Great job connecting time expressions to the Past Perfect!

B Build sentences

Build a full sentence using the cue and the given time expression.

  1. before she arrive we / finish / dinner

    ✓ Before she arrived, we had finished dinner.

  2. by the time the bell ring students / leave
    Model answer

    By the time the bell rang, the students had left.

  3. already he / read / the letter before I told him
    Model answer

    He had already read the letter before I told him.

  4. never...before she / feel so nervous
    Model answer

    She had never felt so nervous before.

  5. after they / finish / the exam they / celebrate
    Model answer

    After they had finished the exam, they celebrated.

C Your turn

Write 2-3 sentences about a memorable event in your life using at least two of these time expressions: before, after, already, by the time, never...before.

By the time I finished school, I had...I had never... before the day I...
Rule

Time expressions such as 'before', 'after', 'already', 'by the time' and 'never...before' are often used with the Past Perfect to make the sequence of past events clear.

TR'Before', 'after', 'already', 'by the time' ve 'never...before' gibi zaman ifadeleri, geçmişteki olayların sırasını netleştirmek için genellikle Past Perfect ile birlikte kullanılır.

When?

We choose the time expression depending on which action we want to highlight as earlier, and we combine it with the Past Perfect for that earlier action.

TRHangi eylemin daha önce gerçekleştiğini vurgulamak istediğimize göre zaman ifadesini seçeriz ve bu daha önceki eylemi Past Perfect ile birleştiririz.

3 · Form

Positive

  • By the time we got there, the show had already begun.
  • After she had finished her studies, she started working.

Negative

  • Before the interview, he hadn't prepared any questions.
  • By the time I called, they hadn't left yet.

Question

  • Had you ever visited that country before your trip last year?
  • By the time you got home, had your family already eaten?

Short answers

  • Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
  • Yes, they had. / No, they hadn't.
Mistakes
  • By the time she arrived, we finish the cake.By the time she arrived, we had finished the cake.

    With 'by the time' + past, the completed earlier action needs the Past Perfect.

    TR'By the time' + geçmiş zaman ile, daha önce tamamlanmış eylem Past Perfect gerektirir.

  • It was the first time I have seen snow.It was the first time I had seen snow.

    After 'It was the first time...', use the Past Perfect, not the Present Perfect.

    TR'It was the first time...' ifadesinden sonra Present Perfect değil, Past Perfect kullanılır.

  • After she had arrived home, she was calling her mother.After she had arrived home, she called her mother.

    The later action is usually in the Simple Past, not the Past Continuous, unless duration is emphasised.

    TRSüre vurgulanmadığı sürece sonraki eylem genellikle Past Continuous değil Simple Past ile ifade edilir.

The Teacher Who Never Gave Up

By the time Elif turned forty, she had already achieved something that few people from her small village had ever managed: she had become the headteacher of the region's largest school. But her path there had not been easy.

Elif had grown up in a mountain village where, at the time, few girls had continued their education beyond primary school. Her parents had never gone to secondary school themselves, but they had always believed that education could change their daughter's future. By the time she was twelve, Elif had already decided that she wanted to become a teacher, even though nobody in her family had ever worked in education before.

After she had finished secondary school in the nearest town, she had to travel two hours by bus every day to attend university. She later said that by the time she graduated, she had missed countless family events and had faced financial hardship that had tested her more than once. Still, she had never given up on her goal.

Her first teaching post had been in a small rural school that had not had enough books or equipment. Elif had spent her own savings buying materials for her students. Within a few years, word of her dedication had spread, and by the time a position opened at the regional school, she had already built a reputation as one of the most resilient teachers in the area.

Today, when Elif looks back, she says that everything she had gone through had shaped the teacher she eventually became. Her story has become an inspiration for many young people in her village who now believe that, like her, they too can overcome hardship and reach their own milestones.

A Return to the Abandoned House

When Kerem parked his car in front of the old house, he felt a wave of nostalgia. He had not visited the place since his family had relocated to the city twenty years earlier, and by the time he got out of the car, memories he had almost forgotten had already begun to return.

The house looked very different from how he remembered it. The garden his grandfather had once carefully looked after had become overgrown, and the walls, which had been painted a bright blue when he was a child, were now faded and cracked. Someone had clearly abandoned the property years before, and nature had slowly taken it back.

Kerem walked through what had once been the kitchen. He recalled that his grandmother had always been cooking something there, and the smell of fresh bread had filled the whole house every morning. He remembered that, before he had even woken up, she had usually already prepared breakfast for the whole family.

In the corner of the main room, Kerem found an old wooden chest that his family had left behind when they had moved. Inside, he discovered photographs that had faded over the decades, along with a notebook his grandfather had written in. As he read the pages, he realised how much of his family's history he had never known before that moment.

Kerem left the house that afternoon feeling that, although the building had changed completely, the memories it held had not faded at all. He decided that he would come back again soon, this time bringing his own children so they could learn about the place their family had once called home.

My Life Before This Moment

Tell your partner about three things that had already happened in your life before you started this school year, using the Past Perfect.

Useful language

By the time I started this year, I had already...Before that, I had never...I had just... when...It was the first time I had ever...

Adımlar

  1. Think of three events or achievements from your life before this school year began.
  2. Prepare one or two sentences about each event using the Past Perfect.
  3. Take turns telling your partner your three events; your partner listens and asks one follow-up question about each.
  4. Swap roles so both partners get a turn to speak and to ask questions.
DestekGive students a simple sentence frame: 'By the time I started this year, I had already ___.' and let them fill in one event only.
HedefStudents should produce three full sentences using the Past Perfect and answer at least one follow-up question naturally.
İleriAsk stronger students to link their three events with time expressions (before, after, by the time) into a short connected mini-narrative.
SpeakingGroups of 3-410 min

The Place I Remember

Describe a memorable place from your past to your group, explaining what it had looked like and what had happened there before it changed or before you left it.

Useful language

The place I remember most is...Before it changed, it had been...I had spent a lot of time there because...By the time I last saw it, it had already...

Adımlar

  1. Think of a place from your past (a house, a school, a neighbourhood) that has changed or that you no longer visit.
  2. Note down two or three details about what the place had been like and what had happened there.
  3. Describe the place to your group, using the Past Perfect where appropriate to show earlier events.
  4. Group members ask one question each about the place after the description.
DestekProvide a picture prompt or a short list of adjectives (quiet, crowded, colourful, old) to help weaker students describe the place.
HedefStudents describe the place clearly with at least two Past Perfect sentences and respond to group questions.
İleriAsk stronger students to compare how the place looked before and how it might look now, using contrastive language.

I can…

Ödev — seviyeni seç

Destek
  • Write four sentences about your day using 'had already' + past participle, following the model: 'By the time I got to school, I had already had breakfast.'
  • Match five vocabulary words from this theme to their Turkish meanings.
  • Complete four gap-fill sentences with the correct Past Perfect form of the verb given.
  • Read the sentence pairs and mark which action happened first (earlier action = Past Perfect).
Standart
  • Write a short paragraph (80-100 words) describing three things that had happened in your life before you turned fifteen.
  • Rewrite five Simple Past sentence pairs as single sentences using the Past Perfect and a suitable time expression.
  • Choose five new vocabulary words from this theme and write an original sentence for each.
  • Answer the reading comprehension questions for 'The Teacher Who Never Gave Up' in full sentences.
İleri
  • Write a 150-word biography paragraph about a family member, focusing on what they had achieved before a key milestone in their life.
  • Record yourself (or write a script) telling a one-minute personal story using at least four Past Perfect sentences.
  • Find or create three example sentences that show the difference between the Past Perfect and the Simple Past in the same context.
  • Write five original test-style questions (mixing MCQ, gap-fill and true/false) about this theme's grammar point for a classmate to answer.
