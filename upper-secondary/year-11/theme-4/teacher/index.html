What a Life

  3. İndir sekmesinden çalışma kâğıdını ve cevap anahtarını al; internet yoksa Sınıf Modu’nun çevrimdışı dosyasını kullan.

Sekme 4 / 8

Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Theme Introduction: What a Life40 min dk
Kazanım
L1, V1
Hedef dil
Vocabulary for describing past lives, places and events
Kelimeler
achievement, memorable, childhood, upbringing, eventually
Materyal
Board, Vocabulary handout, Projector (optional)
Hazırlık
Prepare a short list of famous or local figures with interesting life stories to introduce the theme.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair work and checks vocabulary use informally.
Ödev
Review the vocabulary list and write one sentence for each word.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask students to name one memorable event from their own past. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Introduce the theme title 'What a Life' and present the theme's 'I can' goals. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Present the first set of vocabulary items with example sentences; students guess meanings from context. (Individual/pairs)
  4. 10 min Pair activity: students use two new words in original sentences about someone's life. (Pairs)
DestekProvide a word-meaning matching handout for the new vocabulary.
HedefStudents produce original sentences with at least two new words.
İleriAsk stronger students to use three or more new words in a short connected description.
DERS 2Vocabulary Deep Dive: Lives and Places40 min dk
Kazanım
V1, R1
Hedef dil
Vocabulary describing past lives and places
Kelimeler
background, encounter, recall, reunion, relocate, hardship
Materyal
Vocabulary cards, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary cards with word, part of speech and a gap for students to write an example sentence.
Ölçme
Check comprehension question answers as a class.
Ödev
Complete the vocabulary card set for self-study.

Akış

  1. 5 min Quick recap of previous lesson's vocabulary through a rapid-fire quiz. (T-S)
  2. 15 min Present remaining vocabulary items with example sentences and collocations. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Students read a short original paragraph containing the new vocabulary and answer two comprehension questions. (Individual)
  4. 10 min Pair check of answers and vocabulary card creation for personal study. (Pairs)
DestekAllow students to use bilingual dictionaries for unfamiliar words.
HedefStudents correctly answer both comprehension questions.
İleriAsk stronger students to write a third, more complex sentence linking two vocabulary items.
DERS 3Noticing the Past Perfect40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, R1
Hedef dil
Past Perfect form: had + past participle
Kelimeler
achievement, memorable, background
Materyal
Board, Notice sentences handout
Hazırlık
Prepare the notice sentences from grammar point g1 on the board or a handout.
Ölçme
Monitor discovery task answers for correct Past Perfect forms.
Ödev
Write three original sentences using the Past Perfect based on the notice sentences.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask which past action happened first in two simple example sentences. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present the notice sentences for the Past Perfect and ask the noticeQ discussion question. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Guide students through the 'complete' discovery task, eliciting the rule for Past Perfect formation. (Individual/pairs)
  4. 10 min Teacher explains rule_en/rule and when_en/when explicitly, checking understanding. (T-S)
DestekProvide the past participle list for irregular verbs used in the task.
HedefStudents complete the discovery task with at least 4/5 correct.
İleriAsk stronger students to explain the rule in their own words to a partner.
DERS 4Building Past Perfect Sentences40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, W2
Hedef dil
Past Perfect affirmative, negative and question forms
Kelimeler
encounter, recall, unexpectedly
Materyal
Board, Cue cards
Hazırlık
Prepare the 'build' cue cards from grammar point g1.
Ölçme
Check practice items as a class, correcting common errors.
Ödev
Complete remaining practice items from grammar point g1.

Akış

  1. 5 min Recap the Past Perfect rule with a quick oral review. (T-S)
  2. 15 min Guided 'build' practice: students construct full sentences from cues. (Pairs)
  3. 10 min Present the form table (positive/negative/question/short answers) with examples. (T-S)
  4. 10 min Students complete practice items 1-4 from grammar point g1. (Individual)
DestekProvide sentence frames for negative and question forms.
HedefStudents correctly complete at least 3/4 practice items.
İleriAsk stronger students to write one extra question-and-short-answer pair.
DERS 5Avoiding Common Mistakes with the Past Perfect40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, W6
Hedef dil
Common Past Perfect mistakes and corrections
Kelimeler
vivid, faded, abandoned
Materyal
Board, Mistake correction handout
Hazırlık
Prepare the mistakes list from grammar point g1 for correction practice.
Ölçme
Review corrected sentences for accuracy.
Ödev
Proofread and correct a short paragraph with planted Past Perfect errors.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: show one incorrect sentence and ask students to spot the error. (T-S)
  2. 15 min Present the mistakes/corrections pairs with bilingual explanations. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Students work in pairs to correct five teacher-provided incorrect sentences. (Pairs)
  4. 10 min Class review and discussion of correction strategies. (T-S)
DestekProvide the correct answer key after initial attempt for self-checking.
HedefStudents correctly identify and fix at least 4/5 mistakes.
İleriAsk stronger students to write two of their own incorrect/correct sentence pairs.

DERS 8Listening: Catching Up After Many Years40 min dk
Kazanım
L1, L2, L3, P1

Past Perfect in natural spoken dialogue; pronunciation of 'had'

Transcript handout (for teacher reading aloud or recording), Board
Hazırlık
Prepare to read the l1 transcript aloud clearly, or record it beforehand.
Ölçme
Check listening comprehension answers as a class.
Ödev

Akış

  1. 5 min Pre-listening: discuss the pre-listening prompts and predict content. (T-S)
  2. 10 min First listening (teacher reads dialogue aloud); students listen for gist. (T-S)
  3. 5 min Focus on pronunciation: practise reduced 'had' /əd/ in fast speech versus full form. (T-S)
  4. 15 min Second listening; students answer the comprehension questions individually then check in pairs. (Individual/pairs)
  5. 5 min Post-listening discussion. (T-S)
DestekProvide the transcript for weaker students to read while listening.
HedefStudents answer at least 3/4 listening questions correctly.
İleriAsk stronger students to role-play a similar reunion dialogue in pairs.

DERS 10Speaking Workshop: Talking About the Past40 min dk
Kazanım
S1, S2, S3, S6
Hedef dil
Spoken Past Perfect narratives and follow-up questions
Kelimeler
Review of theme vocabulary
Materyal
Speaking task cards (s1, s2), Board
Hazırlık
Prepare speaking task cards and useful language handouts for s1 and s2.
Ölçme
Teacher circulates and notes accurate/inaccurate use of the Past Perfect for feedback.
Ödev

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: model one example using useful language for speaking task s1. (T-S)
  2. 15 min Students complete speaking task s1 in pairs, describing three past events. (Pairs)
  3. 15 min Students complete speaking task s2 in groups, describing a memorable place. (Groups of 3-4)
  4. 5 min Whole-class feedback: a few students share their most interesting point. (T-S)
DestekProvide sentence starters from the support notes in tasks s1 and s2.
HedefStudents produce at least three accurate Past Perfect sentences across both tasks.
İleriExtension students link their sentences into a connected mini-narrative as suggested in the task notes.

Kazanım

Akış

DestekProvide the structures and vocabulary lists from the writing task as scaffolding.
HedefStudents produce a complete paragraph meeting the word count and checklist requirements.
İleriAsk stronger students to add a second paragraph comparing the past situation to the present.
