DERS 1Opening Up: Theme Introduction40 min dk
Kazanım

Ölçme
Teacher monitors vocabulary matching accuracy.
Ödev
Learn the 6 new words for a quick quiz next lesson.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask students if it's easy or hard to talk about feelings, and why. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Introduce theme vocabulary (regret, confide, open up, bottle up) with example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Students match new words to Turkish meanings in pairs. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Short listening: play/read a few lines about someone opening up; students note key vocabulary they hear. (individual)
  5. 5 min Whole-class feedback and preview of the theme's grammar focus. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word bank with Turkish translations already filled in for two items.
HedefStudents match all 6 words independently.
İleriAsk fast finishers to write one original sentence per word.

DERS 6Might Have / Must Have / Couldn't Have — Noticing the Pattern40 min dk
Kazanım
G1, L3
Hedef dil
might have, must have, couldn't have
Kelimeler
assumption, misunderstanding
Materyal
Board, Grammar handout
Hazırlık
Prepare notice sentences and evidence-based mini scenarios.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair answers to gap-fill.
Ödev
Complete practice items 1-3 from Grammar g2.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: show a simple mystery clue (e.g. wet umbrella by the door) and elicit guesses. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present notice sentences for might have / must have / couldn't have; ask noticing question. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Explain rule bilingually and go over the certainty scale (couldn't → might → must). (T-S)
  4. 10 min Complete-the-gap practice in pairs. (pairs)
  5. 5 min Check answers; highlight the compare table (must vs might). (T-S)
DestekProvide the certainty scale visually (couldn't - might - must) as a reference.
HedefStudents complete the gap-fill with at least 80% accuracy.
İleriAsk fast finishers to rank three guesses about a scenario from least to most certain.
