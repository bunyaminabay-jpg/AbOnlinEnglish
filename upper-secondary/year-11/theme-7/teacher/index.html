  3. İndir sekmesinden çalışma kâğıdını ve cevap anahtarını al; internet yoksa Sınıf Modu’nun çevrimdışı dosyasını kullan.

Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Introducing Facts about Türkiye40 min dk
Kazanım
L1, V1
Hedef dil
Landmark, monument, ancient, heritage, site
Kelimeler
landmark, monument, ancient, heritage, site, ruins
Materyal
Board, Pictures of Turkish landmarks, Vocabulary handout
Hazırlık
Print or display pictures of 4-5 well-known Turkish landmarks.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors group discussion for correct use of new vocabulary.
Ödev
Look up (in general terms, no need to write anything down) three facts about a Turkish landmark to share next lesson.

Akış

  1. 5 min Show pictures of landmarks and ask students what they know about them. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Pre-teach key vocabulary (landmark, monument, ancient, heritage, site, ruins) with example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Students match vocabulary words to short definitions in pairs. (Pairs)
  4. 10 min Students discuss in small groups which landmarks they have visited or would like to visit. (Groups)
  5. 5 min Whole-class feedback and wrap-up. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word bank with Turkish translations.
HedefStudents use at least four new words in discussion.
İleriStudents add one extra landmark they know of themselves.

İleriStudents write one original sentence per new word.
