Can: possibility and ability

1 · Notice
  • Tourists can visit Dolmabahçe Palace in İstanbul.
  • I can speak Turkish and English.
  • Can you speak Korean? — No, I can't.
  • You can take a boat trip on the Bosphorus.

Look at the highlighted words. Then go to Discover.

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

Look at the Notice sentences again. Complete the rule with the words in the box.

basedoesn'tCancan't

Well done! You found the rule yourself. Now let's use it.

B Build sentences

Use the cues to write sentences. Look at the example first.

  1. Ankara tourists visit Anıtkabir

    ✓ Tourists can visit Anıtkabir in Ankara.

  2. Rome you see the Colosseum
    Model answer

    You can see the Colosseum in Rome.

  3. Mateo speak Spanish and English
    Model answer

    Mateo can speak Spanish and English.

  4. tourists not / visit the museum on Mondays
    Model answer

    Tourists can't visit the museum on Mondays.

  5. ? / your friend speak German
    Model answer

    Can your friend speak German?

  6. İstanbul we take a boat trip on the Bosphorus
    Model answer

    We can take a boat trip on the Bosphorus in İstanbul.

C Your turn

Write about your city. Say two things tourists can do, one thing they can't do, and one language you can speak.

In my city, tourists can …They can also …They can't … because …I can speak …
Rule

can + base verb. 'can' is the same for all subjects (he can, she can). The negative is can't / cannot. In questions, Can comes first. We use it for possibility (what people can do in a place) and ability (what a person can do).

TRcan + fiilin yalın hâli. Tüm öznelerle aynıdır (he can, she can). Olumsuzu can't / cannot; soruda Can başa gelir. Bir yerde yapılabilecekleri (olanak) ve bir kişinin yapabildiklerini (yetenek) anlatır.

When?

We use it to say what tourists can do in a city and which languages people can speak.

TRBir şehirde turistlerin neler yapabileceğini ve insanların hangi dilleri konuşabildiğini anlatırken kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • I / You / He / She / We / They can speak Spanish.

Negative

  • She can't speak Japanese.
  • They cannot visit the tower on Mondays.

Question

  • Can you speak Italian?
  • What can tourists do in Rome?

Short answers

  • Yes, I can.
  • No, she can't.
Mistakes
  • She cans speak French.She can speak French.

    'can' never takes -s.

    TRcan özneye göre -s almaz.

  • I can to swim.I can swim.

    Don't use 'to' after 'can'.

    TRcan'dan sonra ‘to’ gelmez.

1 Before reading

  • Look at the title. Which country is Baku the capital of?
  • What do tourists usually do in a capital city? Guess three things.

A Weekend in Baku

Hi, I'm Leyla. I'm from Azerbaijan and I'm a student at an international school in Ankara. My native language is Azerbaijani, but I can speak Turkish and English, too.

Last month my classmates asked me about my capital, Baku. Baku is on the coast of the Caspian Sea. The old part of the city is called İçərişəhər. It is very ancient and it has a castle wall, narrow streets and the Maiden Tower.

Tourists can walk along the seaside boulevard in the evening. They can also visit the Flame Towers. At night the towers look like three big fires. My favourite place is the carpet museum because it is fascinating and very colourful.

If you go sightseeing in Baku, try the local tea with jam. And don't forget to say ‘Salam!’ to people. It means ‘Hello!’
