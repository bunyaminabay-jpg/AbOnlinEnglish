Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students write and design a school drama play on the the content of ''body parts, human illnesses and diseases, human well-being, human nature, well-being for the future'' and then they present it and perform it as a show in class by inviting other students as patients, doctors, and nurses. The assignment is evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.

  3. 15 Pre-teach well-being, symptom, cope with with mime and example sentences; students repeat and note. (Whole class → pairs)

DestekGive sentence starters on the board.
