Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students write and design a school drama play on the content of ''different types of schools in the world, extracurricular activities at school, dealing with school anxiety'' and then they present it and perform it as a show in class by inviting other students as actors, directors, etc. The assignment will be evaluated by using rubrics.

Ödev

Akış

  1. 5 Warm-up: show 3 photos (different school buildings, a drama rehearsal, a nervous student). Ask 'What do you see?' (Whole class)
  2. 10 Present the theme roadmap: types of schools, extracurricular activities, dealing with school anxiety. (Whole class)
  3. 15 Pre-teach state school, private school, curriculum with pictures and example sentences; students repeat and note. (Whole class → pairs)
  4. 10 Pair talk: 'My school is a … school. One subject on our curriculum is …' — students share with a partner. (Pairs)
DestekGive sentence starters on the board.
Hedef3 target words, roadmap, pair talk.
İleriFast finishers add one more sentence about their own school.
