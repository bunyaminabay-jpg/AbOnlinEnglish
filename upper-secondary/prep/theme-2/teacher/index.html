Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students write and design ''my ideal classroom with ideal activities'' project. They will describe ideal roles, relationships, routines, and activities for an ideal class and classroom. They will write scripts for each role. In the class, they will act-out these roles. The assignment will be evaluated by using rubrics.
