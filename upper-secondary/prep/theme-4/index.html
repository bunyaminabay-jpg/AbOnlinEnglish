In Theme 4, you will explore Family Life: family members, their daily routines, hobbies and the relationships that connect them. You will learn to talk about the future with will, be going to, the Present Progressive and the Simple Present, so you can describe your family's plans, routines and shared activities.

Konular

  • Hobbies of family members
  • Relationships in the family

  • Present Progressive for future

Simple Future (will): predictions and promises about family

1 · Notice

C Your turn

Write three sentences about your family's future: one prediction, one promise, and one question with will.

I think my family will …I promise I will …Will your … ?
Rule

We use the Simple Future (will) for predictions, promises, spontaneous decisions and offers about the future. Form: will + base verb. Negative: won't + base verb. Question: Will + subject + base verb?

TRSimple Future (will), gelecekle ilgili tahminler, sözler, anlık kararlar ve teklifler için kullanılır. Will + fiilin yalın hâli ile kurulur. Olumsuzda won't, soruda Will + özne + fiil kullanılır.

When?

We use it to make predictions about family members, to make promises, and to decide something at the moment of speaking.

TRAile hakkında tahminlerde bulunurken, söz verirken ya da o anda karar verirken kullanılır.

3 · Form

Positive

  • My grandma will love this gift.
  • I will help you with the chores.

Negative

  • My parents won't be home before eight.
  • We won't argue about it.

Question

  • Will your cousins come to the reunion?
  • Will you help me look after my brother?

Short answers

  • Yes, they will. / No, they won't.
  • Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
Mistakes
  • I will to help you.I will help you.

    Don't use 'to' after will.

    TRwill'den sonra 'to' gelmez.

  • She wills come to the party.She will come to the party.

    Will stays the same for every subject; don't add -s.

    TRwill her özne için aynı kalır, -s eklenmez.

Rule

We use the Simple Present for future events that are part of a fixed timetable or programme, like flights, school terms or planned family events.

TRSimple Present; uçuş saatleri, okul takvimi, resmî programlar gibi sabit bir zaman çizelgesine bağlı gelecek olaylar için kullanılır.

When?

TRBir aile etkinliğinin ya da yolculuğun kesin, değişmeyen saatinden bahsederken kullanılır.

TRBe going to, önceden karar verilmiş planlar ve niyetler için kullanılır; ayrıca şu anki bir kanıta (örn. bulutlar) dayanarak yapılan tahminler için de kullanılır.

We use it to talk about family plans that are already decided, or to predict something based on what we can see right now.

Write two sentences about a family plan you have already arranged for next week, using the Present Progressive.

Rule

We use the Present Progressive for personal future plans that are already arranged, usually with a fixed time. It is similar to be going to but often emphasises a definite arrangement with other people.

TRPresent Progressive, kişisel olarak önceden düzenlenmiş, genellikle belirli bir zamanı olan gelecek planları için kullanılır. Be going to'ya benzer ama daha çok başkalarıyla yapılan kesin ayarlamaları vurgular.

When?

We use it to talk about family events that are already arranged for a definite time, like a lunch or a visit.

TRAilece önceden planlanmış, kesin bir zamanı olan etkinlikleri (öğle yemeği, ziyaretler) anlatırken kullanılır.

  • My cousins are coming next week.

  • We aren't visiting our relatives this weekend.

A Weekend with My Family

My name is Zeynep, and I live with my parents, my grandparents, and my twin brother, Kaan. We are a close-knit family, so we spend a lot of time together, especially at the weekend.

On Saturday mornings, we all do chores together. My dad and I clean the kitchen, while my mum and Kaan take turns walking the dog. My grandparents look after the garden — that's their favourite hobby.

After lunch, we usually play a board game or go fishing at the lake near our house. My brother collects fishing hooks from every trip, and he is going to show his collection to our cousins next weekend.

In the evening, we sometimes visit our relatives. My aunt and uncle are coming to dinner tonight, and I think we are going to talk for hours! We don't always agree about everything, and sometimes my brother and I argue about small things, but we always support each other.

This weekend, we are going to have a special family reunion because it's my grandma's birthday. The party starts at six o'clock, and I am sure everyone will have a wonderful time.

Planning Our Family Project

For our English project, my group is going to research and present 'Family Life'. We are going to describe the roles, routines, hobbies and relationships of different family members.

First, we are going to interview our own families. I will ask my parents about their daily routines, and my partner Elif will ask her grandparents about their hobbies when they were young.

Next, we are going to make a poster with photos and short texts. It will show who does which chores, who has which hobbies, and how family members support each other.

We are also going to add a section about relationships: how brothers and sisters get along, how families spend time together, and how relatives help each other during busy weeks.

Our teacher says the project is going to be ready in three weeks. We are going to practise our presentation on Monday, and I think our classmates are going to enjoy learning about different families!

  4. Try to find a different name for each sentence.
  5. Sit down and share two interesting answers with the class.
DestekGive students the question forms in writing before they start.

Our Weekend Plans — Role-Play

With a partner, act out a short conversation between two family members planning what they are going to do this weekend.

Useful language

What are we doing this weekend?I think we should…We are going to…I will…, I promise.

Adımlar

  1. Choose your roles: two family members (e.g. brother and sister, or parent and child).
  2. Decide on three activities for the weekend (a chore, a hobby, a family visit).
  3. Act out the conversation using will, be going to and the Present Progressive.
  4. Swap roles and do it again with new activities.
  5. Perform your favourite conversation for another pair.
DestekProvide sentence starters on cards for lower-level students.
Hedef3 weekend activities discussed per pair.
İleriStudents invent one extra family activity of their own to include.

My Family's Weekend Plan

Write a short text about what your family is going to do next weekend. Include at least two sentences with be going to and one with will.

Plan

  1. Choose 3-4 activities your family will do.
  2. Decide which are fixed plans (going to / Present Progressive) and which are predictions or promises (will).
  3. Write your sentences in a logical order (morning, afternoon, evening).
  4. Check your grammar and punctuation.

Our Family Life Project Plan

Write a short plan for the class project about family life. Describe the roles, routines, hobbies and relationships in a family (your own or an imaginary one), and say what your group is going to do to prepare the project.

Plan

  1. Choose the family members you will describe (e.g. parents, siblings, grandparents).
  2. List one routine, one hobby, and one way family members support each other.
  3. Say what your group is going to do this week to prepare (interview, poster, presentation).
