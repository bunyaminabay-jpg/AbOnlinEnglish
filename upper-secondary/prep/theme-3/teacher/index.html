1130 Kasım-4 Aralık20 (haftayı paylaşır)Dünya Engelliler Günü (3 Aralık)

Performance assignment (resmî plan)

They write and design ''a fact file'' for one of their classmates after conducting an interview with him/her outside the classroom in break times. They will include information about physical features and personal traits to describe the classmate in question. They will present it to the class and ask the other students to guess whose fact file it is. In the class, the students whose fact files are presented will introduce the presenters by giving details about their physical features and personal traits. Then all students will prepare wanted posters for each other based on the information in the fact files. The assignment will be evaluated by using rubrics.
