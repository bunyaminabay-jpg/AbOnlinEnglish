Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will design and write a project that explores the planets in our solar system, their satellites, and interesting facts about each planet. They will also focus on Earth as a planet and how it compares to others. Students will create scripts for different planetary explorers, astronomers, and scientists who are investigating and studying the solar system. Through role-playing and acting out these roles, they will simulate an interplanetary journey and share knowledge about each planet’s characteristics. The assignment will be evaluated using rubrics.
