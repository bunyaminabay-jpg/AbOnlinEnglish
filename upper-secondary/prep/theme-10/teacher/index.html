2422-26 Mart20 (haftayı paylaşır)—

Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students write and design a project on ''Technological Classroom Tools, Learning with Technology, and Future Classrooms.'' They will describe the ideal use of technology in the classroom, including the roles of students and teachers, the tools used, and the learning activities involved. They will write scripts for each role, showing how technology enhances learning and interaction. In the class, they will act out these roles, demonstrating how technology supports learning in a future classroom. The assignment will be evaluated by using rubrics.
