DERS 1Introducing Space and the Universe40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.8.V1, ENG.10.8.S1
Hedef dil
Basic space vocabulary; I think there is/isn't life on other planets.
Kelimeler
planet, galaxy, universe, star, solar system
Materyal
Board, Vocabulary cards, Projector (optional)
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary cards or slides with images of space.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors vocabulary matching accuracy and discussion participation.
Ödev
Look up 3 more space-related words and their Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: Ask students what they know about space and the universe. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present key vocabulary (planet, galaxy, universe, star) with visuals. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Students match words to definitions in pairs. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Short discussion: Do you think there is life on other planets? (pairs)
  5. 5 min Feedback and wrap-up. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word-picture matching handout.
HedefStandard vocabulary and discussion tasks.
İleriAsk students to add three more space words they already know.
DERS 2Planets of the Solar System40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.8.V1, ENG.10.8.S3
Hedef dil
Describing planets: size, distance, temperature
Kelimeler
orbit, gravity, distance, surface, atmosphere
Materyal
Solar system diagram, Worksheet
Hazırlık
Print or display a labelled diagram of the solar system.
Ölçme
Check accuracy of question-answer exchanges in pairs.
Ödev
Write 3 sentences describing your favourite planet.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: Name the planets you know in English. (individual)
  2. 10 min Teach new vocabulary (orbit, gravity, distance, surface, atmosphere). (T-S)
  3. 15 min Pair work: Ask and answer questions about planets using a fact sheet. (pairs)
  4. 5 min Class quiz on planet facts. (T-S)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up and preview of next lesson. (T-S)
DestekProvide sentence frames for questions and answers.
HedefStandard pair-work practice.
İleriStudents research one extra planet fact to share.

DERS 4Zero Conditional — Noticing the Pattern40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.8.G1
Hedef dil

Kelimeler

Akış

  3. 15 min Complete gap-fill practice on zero conditional individually, then check in pairs. (individual/pairs)
  4. 5 min Class feedback and correction. (T-S)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up: Elicit the rule from students. (T-S)
DestekProvide the rule explicitly before the gap-fill.
HedefStandard noticing and gap-fill task.
