Ödev
Support homework task 1.

  1. 5 min Warm-up: students brainstorm places they visit in their neighbourhood for fun. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new vocabulary about attractions with example sentences and IPA. (T-S)

  4. 10 min Students share which attraction they visit most and why. (individual)

İleriStudents write two extra sentences using new words together.
