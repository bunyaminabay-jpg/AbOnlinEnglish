This theme looks at everyday life in the neighbourhood and city: enjoying places like cafes, cinemas, gyms and sports clubs, getting help from services such as pharmacies and hospitals, and weighing the pros and cons of different ways of getting around town.

Konular

  • Activities to do in attractions (cafes, cinemas, gyms, sports clubs)
  • Getting help from services (pharmacies, hospitals, etc.)
  • Pros and cons of different types of transportation in the neighbourhood and city

C Your turn

Write 2-3 original sentences about what you and your family were doing at a specific time yesterday.

At 8pm yesterday, I was...My parents were...
Rule

We form the past progressive with was/were + the -ing form of the verb. It describes an action that was in progress at a specific moment or period in the past.

TRGeçmiş zamanda süren eylem, was/were + fiil-ing ile kurulur. Geçmişte belirli bir anda veya süre boyunca devam eden bir eylemi anlatır.

When?

We use the past progressive for actions in progress at a stated past time, for background actions in a story, and for two actions happening at the same time in the past.

TRBelirtilen bir geçmiş anda süren eylemler, bir hikayede arka plan eylemleri ve geçmişte aynı anda süren iki eylem için kullanılır.

3 · Form

Positive

  • I/He/She/It + was + -ing
  • You/We/They + were + -ing
  • e.g. She was walking to the gym.

Negative

  • I/He/She/It + wasn't + -ing
  • You/We/They + weren't + -ing
  • e.g. We weren't waiting long.

Question

  • Was + I/he/she/it + -ing...?
  • Were + you/we/they + -ing...?
  • e.g. Were you queuing at the pharmacy?

Short answers

  • Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
  • Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
Mistakes
  • I was wait for the bus.I was waiting for the bus.

    The main verb must take the -ing ending.

    TRAna fiile -ing eki eklenmelidir.

  • She were jogging in the park.She was jogging in the park.

    Use was with she/he/it, not were.

    TRShe/he/it ile were değil was kullanılır.

  • They was queuing at the cinema.They were queuing at the cinema.

    Use were with they, not was.

    TRThey ile was değil were kullanılır.

  • We wasn't using the gym.We weren't using the gym.

    The negative of we is weren't, not wasn't.

    TRWe öznesinin olumsuzu wasn't değil weren't'tür.

Simple Past vs Past Progressive

1 · Notice
  • I was waiting at the bus stop when the bus arrived.
  • While she was shopping, she met her English teacher.
  • The phone rang while we were watching a film at the cinema.
  • We were queuing at the pharmacy, and then the doctor called my name.

Which action was already in progress, and which action interrupted it? What tense is used for each?

C Your turn

Write 2-3 original sentences describing something that happened while you were doing another activity in your neighbourhood.

While I was...I was ... when...
Rule

We use the simple past for completed actions and the past progressive for actions in progress. Together, the past progressive shows the background action and the simple past shows the shorter action that interrupted it.

TRTamamlanmış eylemler için geniş geçmiş zaman (simple past), süren eylemler için ise past progressive kullanılır. Bir arada kullanıldıklarında, past progressive arka plan eylemini, simple past ise onu kesen daha kısa eylemi gösterir.

When?

Use 'while' with the past progressive for the longer action, and 'when' with the simple past for the shorter, interrupting action.

TRUzun süren eylem için past progressive ile 'while', onu kesen kısa eylem için ise simple past ile 'when' kullanılır.

3 · Form

Positive

  • Past progressive (background) + when/while + simple past (interruption)
  • e.g. I was waiting when the bus arrived.

Negative

  • I wasn't waiting when the bus arrived.
  • They didn't see the accident while they were shopping.

Question

  • What were you doing when the bus arrived?
  • Were you waiting when it happened?

Short answers

  • Yes, I was waiting. / No, I wasn't waiting.
Mistakes
  • I waited when the bus was arriving.I was waiting when the bus arrived.

    The background action should be past progressive, not the interrupting one.

    TRArka plan eylemi past progressive olmalı, kesen eylem değil.

  • While she was shop, she was meeting a friend.While she was shopping, she met a friend.

    The shorter interrupting action should be simple past.

    TRKesen kısa eylem simple past olmalıdır.

  • The phone was ringing while we watched a film for a moment.The phone rang while we were watching a film.

    A sudden, short action is simple past, not past progressive.

    TRAni, kısa bir eylem simple past olur, past progressive değil.

C Your turn

Write 2-3 original question-tag questions for a short survey about transportation or local services in your neighbourhood.

The nearest pharmacy is..., isn't it?You usually take..., don't you?
Rule

A question tag is a short question added to the end of a statement to check information or start a conversation. A positive statement takes a negative tag, and a negative statement takes a positive tag.

TRQuestion tag, bir bilgiyi doğrulamak veya sohbeti başlatmak için cümle sonuna eklenen kısa bir sorudur. Olumlu cümle olumsuz tag alır, olumsuz cümle olumlu tag alır.

When?

We use question tags in everyday conversation to confirm facts, check opinions, or invite the listener to agree.

TRGünlük konuşmada bir bilgiyi doğrulamak, fikir sormak veya dinleyicinin katılımını istemek için kullanılır.

3 · Form

Positive

  • Positive statement + negative tag (auxiliary + n't + pronoun)
  • e.g. It's convenient, isn't it?

Negative

  • Negative statement + positive tag (auxiliary + pronoun)
  • e.g. It isn't convenient, is it?

Question

  • The tag repeats the auxiliary/modal of the main sentence and matches its subject pronoun.
  • e.g. You can walk there, can't you?

Short answers

  • Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
  • Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Mistakes
  • The gym is new, is it?The gym is new, isn't it?

    A positive statement needs a negative tag.

    TROlumlu cümle olumsuz tag ile tamamlanır.

  • She doesn't like the bus, doesn't she?She doesn't like the bus, does she?

    A negative statement needs a positive tag.

    TROlumsuz cümle olumlu tag ile tamamlanır.

  • You were late, was you?You were late, weren't you?

    The tag pronoun must match the subject (you → you), and the verb must match (were → weren't).

    TRTag zamiri özneyle (you → you) ve fiil (were → weren't) ile uyumlu olmalıdır.

A Saturday in Our Neighbourhood

Saturday afternoons in our neighbourhood are always busy. Families walk along the main street, stopping at the small cafe on the corner for a coffee or a hot chocolate. A little further down, the cinema shows both new films and old favourites, and there is usually a small queue outside before the afternoon show starts. Many teenagers prefer to meet at the gym or the sports club instead, especially since the club opened a new indoor football pitch last spring.

Last Saturday, something a little different happened. While most people were doing their usual shopping, a group of students was setting up a small stall outside the cafe. They were collecting old books and toys for a charity project organised by their school. A few customers were queuing for coffee when they noticed the stall, and many of them stopped to look at the books before continuing on their way.

By four o'clock, the stall had collected more items than the students expected. Meanwhile, at the sports club nearby, a junior basketball match was taking place, and a small crowd was watching from outside the fence. The cafe owner, who was watching the stall from his window, decided to give the students free drinks for their hard work. It was a simple Saturday, but it showed how much people in the neighbourhood enjoy supporting each other.

Getting Help in the City

Getting help from local services is part of everyday life in any city. When people feel unwell, the pharmacy is often the first place they go. Pharmacists can suggest simple medicine for a cold or a headache, and they can explain how a prescription from a doctor should be used. Most pharmacies in the city centre stay open until late, which is convenient for people who finish work in the evening.

For more serious problems, people usually go to the hospital. On a typical afternoon, the waiting room is often full, and patients take a number before seeing the receptionist. Emergency cases are seen first, while other patients wait for their appointment. A nurse explained that although the waiting room was crowded yesterday, the hospital tries to see every patient as quickly as possible.

Getting to these services is not always easy, however. Some neighbourhoods are close to a metro station, so a hospital visit takes only a few minutes. Other areas depend more on buses, and a delay in the timetable can make people late for an appointment. Because of this, city planners are now discussing a new bus route that would connect more neighbourhoods directly to the main hospital, making it easier for everyone to get the help they need.

What Were You Doing?

In pairs, take turns asking and answering what you were doing at three different times yesterday (e.g. 8am, 1pm, 7pm), using the past progressive.
