DERS 1Continents, Countries and Nationalities40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.6.V1, ENG.10.6.S1
Hedef dil
Which continent is... on? What nationality is...? Where is... from?
Kelimeler
continent, nationality, population, border, culture
Materyal
World map or projector, Vocabulary cards, Coursebook
Hazırlık
Prepare a simple world map image and vocabulary cards for continents/nationalities.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors accuracy of vocabulary use during pair work and the class quiz.
Ödev
Match 8 vocabulary words to their Turkish meanings (support homework item 1).

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: show a world map and elicit continent names students already know. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new vocabulary (continent, nationality, population, border, culture) with example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Pair work: students ask and answer 'Where is... from? What nationality is...?' using flashcards. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Class quiz: name the continent of given countries and nationalities. (individual)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up: students share one new fact about a continent/nationality they learned. (T-S)
DestekGive weaker students a matching worksheet (country–nationality–continent).
HedefStandard pair-work task as planned.
İleriAsk stronger students to name the capital city of each country discussed.
DERS 2Present Perfect: Life Experiences40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.6.G1, ENG.10.6.S2
Hedef dil
I have visited... / Have you ever...?
Kelimeler
abroad, travel, experience
Materyal
Grammar presentation slides, Coursebook, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare notice sentences and a gap-fill handout for the present perfect (life experiences).
Ölçme
Teacher checks gap-fill answers and monitors sentence-building accuracy.
Ödev
Write 6 original present perfect sentences (core homework item 1).

Akış

  1. 5 min Notice stage: show example sentences with 'have/has + past participle' and ask what students notice. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Explain form and use of the present perfect for life experiences; check with short Q&A. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Complete the gap-fill discover activity individually, then check as a class. (individual)
  4. 10 min Build stage: students form full sentences from cue words in pairs. (pairs)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up: 3-4 students share one 'I have never...' sentence about themselves. (T-S)
DestekProvide a verb bank of past participles for the gap-fill task.
HedefStandard discover activities as planned.
İleriAsk fast finishers to write 3 extra sentences about a family member's experiences.
DERS 3Present Perfect: Since, For, Already, Just40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.6.G1, ENG.10.6.S2
Hedef dil
since 2015 / for five years / already / just
Kelimeler
since, for, already, just
Materyal
Coursebook, Practice worksheet, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare a compare table contrasting 'since' and 'for' with example timelines.
Ölçme
Teacher reviews practice worksheet answers for correct use of since/for/already/just.
Ödev
Complete challenge items from grammar point g1.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: elicit the difference between 'since' and 'for' using a timeline on the board. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present the compare table (since vs for) and the use of already/just with example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Students complete practice items (gap-fill, mcq, judge) individually then check in pairs. (individual/pairs)
  4. 5 min Mistake correction: show common mistakes and ask students to correct them. (T-S)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up: quick oral round, each student makes one sentence with since/for/already/just. (T-S)
DestekGive sentence frames with the time expression already inserted.
HedefStandard practice worksheet as planned.
İleriChallenge items: rewrite sentences using 'since' instead of 'for' and vice versa.
