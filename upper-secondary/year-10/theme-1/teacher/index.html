boarding school, public school, vocational school, homeschooling, curriculum
Materyal
Vocabulary cards, Board
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary flashcards for types of schools.
Ölçme
Monitor pair discussions for correct use of new vocabulary.
Ödev
Match vocabulary words to Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask students what type of school they attend. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new vocabulary about school types with flashcards. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Read 'Schools Around the World' and answer comprehension questions. (individual)
  4. 10 min Pair discussion: which type of school would you prefer? (pairs)
DestekProvide a word bank for the discussion.
HedefStudents complete all reading questions independently.
İleriAsk fast finishers to list two more types of schools not in the text.
