In Theme 8, you will explore films and film genres, from comedy and action to futuristic space adventures, and think about inventions and technology that might shape our future. You will learn to describe events and plots in films with the Simple Present Tense, and to make predictions about future technology with the Simple Future Tense (will).

Konular

  • Films, film genres
  • Futuristic films with futuristic ideas
  • Technology

Dil yapıları

  • Simple Present (background): events in films
  • Simple Future (will): predictions, beliefs, expectations

Anlayabileceğim

  • A film review of a futuristic robot movie
  • An article about Türkiye's journey into space and future plans
  • Two friends choosing a movie to watch together
  • A conversation with predictions about technology in 2050

Söyleyebileceğim

  • Describe the plot and characters of a film you know
  • Talk about film genres you like and dislike, and why
  • Make predictions about future inventions and technology
  • Discuss and defend your opinion about what the future will be like

Yazabileceğim

  • A short review of a film, describing its plot and genre

Simple Present: Describing Film Events

1 · Notice
  • A young robot discovers a hidden spaceship in the forest.
  • The aliens invade Earth, and a small team of scientists tries to stop them.
  • The main character doesn't trust the strange new invention at first.
  • Does the hero save the city in the end? — Yes, he does.

Do these sentences describe things that happened once in the past, or events in the story that we describe as if they are happening now? What happens to the verb with 'the aliens' compared with 'the main character'?

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

  3. the main character not / trust the strange invention

  4. ? / the hero save the city

  5. the scientists build a new device
    Model answer

    The scientists build a new device.

  6. ? / the crew escape from the alien ship

    Does the crew escape from the alien ship?

C Your turn

Think of a film you know well. Write four sentences in the Simple Present describing its plot and main character.

The film is about...The main character...In the end, the hero...The film doesn't...
Rule

We use the Simple Present to describe the plot and events of a film, as if they are happening now. Add -s/-es to the verb with he/she/it (a singular character). Negative: doesn't + base verb. Question: Does + subject + base verb?

TRFilm konusunu (olay örgüsünü) anlatırken, sanki şu anda oluyormuş gibi Geniş Zaman (Simple Present) kullanılır. He/she/it (tekil karakter) ile fiile -s/-es eklenir. Olumsuzda 'doesn't', soruda 'Does' özneden önce gelir.

When?

We use this to describe a film's plot, events, or what a character does in the story.

TRBir filmin konusunu, olaylarını veya bir karakterin ne yaptığını anlatırken kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • A robot discovers a hidden spaceship.
  • The aliens invade Earth.

Negative

  • The main character doesn't trust the invention.
  • The crew don't escape at first.

Question

  • Does the hero save the city?
  • Do the scientists build a new device?

Short answers

  • Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
  • Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
Mistakes
  • The robot discover a spaceship.The robot discovers a spaceship.

    Add -s to the verb with he/she/it (a singular character).

    TRHe/she/it (tekil karakter) ile fiile -s eklenir.

  • Does the hero saves the city?Does the hero save the city?

    After 'does', use the base verb — no -s.

    TRDoes'tan sonra fiil yalın hâlde kullanılır, -s almaz.

Simple Future (will): Predictions about Technology

1 · Notice
  • By 2050, robots will do most of the housework at home.
  • I think artificial intelligence will help doctors diagnose illnesses faster.
  • Cars won't need drivers in fifty years' time.
  • Will people live on other planets one day? — I think they will.

Which word do we use before the verb to talk about the future? How do we make it negative and a question? Does the verb change for he/she/it?

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

Look at the Notice sentences again. Complete the rule with the words in the box.

willwon'tWillbase verb

Well done! You found the rule yourself. Now let's use it.

B Build sentences

Use the cues to write predictions about the future with will/won't. Look at the example first.

  1. robots do most of the housework by 2050

    ✓ Robots will do most of the housework by 2050.

  2. artificial intelligence help doctors diagnose illnesses faster
    Model answer

    Artificial intelligence will help doctors diagnose illnesses faster.

  3. cars not / need drivers in fifty years
    Model answer

    Cars won't need drivers in fifty years.

  4. ? / people live on other planets one day
    Model answer

    Will people live on other planets one day?

  5. scientists discover new forms of clean energy
    Model answer

    Scientists will discover new forms of clean energy.

  6. ? / everyone have a personal robot at home
    Model answer

    Will everyone have a personal robot at home?

C Your turn

Write three predictions with will/won't about technology in fifty years' time.

In fifty years, people will...I think robots will...Cars won't...
Rule

We use 'will' to make predictions about the future and to express what the speaker believes, expects or guesses will happen. Negative: won't. Question: Will + subject + base verb? Always use the base verb after will/won't, even with he/she/it.

TR'Will' gelecekle ilgili tahminlerde, konuşmacının inandığı veya beklediği şeyleri, görüş ve tahminlerini anlatmak için kullanılır. Olumsuzda 'won't', soruda 'will' özneden önce gelir. Will'den sonra fiilin yalın hâli kullanılır, he/she/it ile bile -s almaz.

When?

We use this to make predictions about future technology, inventions and events.

TRGelecekteki teknoloji, icatlar ve olaylar hakkında tahminde bulunurken kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • Robots will do most of the housework.
  • She will invent something amazing one day.

Negative

  • Cars won't need drivers in the future.
  • People won't use paper books very much.

Question

  • Will people live on other planets?
  • Will robots replace teachers?

Short answers

  • Yes, they will. / No, they won't.
  • Yes, I think so. / No, I don't think so.
Mistakes
  • Robots will to do the housework.Robots will do the housework.

    Don't use 'to' after will.

    TRWill'den sonra 'to' gelmez.

  • She wills discover a new planet.She will discover a new planet.

    Will never takes -s, even with he/she/it.

    TRWill, he/she/it ile de değişmez, -s almaz.

Film Review: Attack of the Robots

'Attack of the Robots' is a new science fiction film, and it is already one of the most popular films of the year. If you enjoy space adventures with exciting special effects, this film is definitely for you.

The plot follows a young engineer named Mira, who discovers a hidden spaceship in an old factory. Inside, she finds a group of friendly robots who warn her about an alien invasion that is coming to Earth. Mira doesn't trust the robots at first, but she soon realises they need her help.

Together, Mira and the robots build a new device that can stop the aliens. The special effects in the invasion scenes are incredible — the alien ships look completely real, and the futuristic city looks amazing on the big screen. The film also has a few funny moments, so it isn't only action and drama; it has a little comedy too.

The main character, Mira, is brave and clever, and the robot characters are surprisingly funny. My favourite character is a small robot called Ziggy, who makes the audience laugh in almost every scene, even during the most dangerous parts of the story.

Does the film have a happy ending? I won't tell you exactly what happens, but I will say this: 'Attack of the Robots' is an exciting, funny and futuristic film that both teenagers and adults will enjoy. I give it four stars out of five!

Türkiye's Journey to Space

In recent years, Türkiye has taken exciting steps into space science and technology. The country now has its own National Space Programme, with clear goals for the future of Turkish space exploration.

One of the programme's aims is to send more Turkish satellites into orbit to improve communication, weather prediction and agriculture across the country. Engineers and scientists are working on new devices and futuristic technology that will make these satellites smarter and more efficient.

The programme also has bigger dreams. Officials say that Türkiye will develop its own spaceport in the coming years, and that Turkish astronauts will take part in future international space missions. Some experts predict that within a few decades, Türkiye will also contribute to missions that explore the Moon and beyond.

Young people play an important part in this journey too. Universities and technology centres across Türkiye now offer programmes where students can study robotics, artificial intelligence and space engineering. Many students dream of becoming the scientists who will discover new technology for future space missions.

Nobody knows exactly what the future will bring, but one thing is clear: Türkiye's space journey is only just beginning. As one young engineer said in an interview, 'In fifty years, I think our inventions will help people travel further into space than we ever imagined.'

Choosing a Movie for Friday Night

1 Before listening

  • You will hear two friends, Ege and Sude, deciding which film to watch on Friday night.
  • Guess: what film genres do you think they will talk about?
0:00

2 While listening

Transkripti göster

EgeHey, do you want to watch a film on Friday night? I really want to see something new.

SudeSure! What genre do you feel like? I love comedies, but I know you prefer action films.

EgeActually, I'd like to try something different. What about that new science fiction film, 'Attack of the Robots'?

SudeOh, I read a review of that! It follows an engineer who discovers a spaceship and has to stop an alien invasion.

EgeThat sounds exciting! Does it have good special effects?

SudeYes, apparently they're amazing. The reviewer also says it has a few funny moments, so it isn't only action.

EgePerfect — a bit of comedy and a bit of action! Let's watch that one.

SudeGreat choice. I think we'll both enjoy it. Let's meet at seven.

SpeakingPairs10-15 dk

Pitch Your Own Film

Invent your own film (any genre). Describe its plot, main character and genre to your partner using the Simple Present, as if you are pitching the idea to a film company.

Useful language

My film is a [genre] about...The main character discovers/finds/builds...In the end, the hero...Does that sound interesting?

Adımlar

  1. Individually, plan a short film idea: genre, main character, and 3-4 plot events.
  2. Take turns pitching your film idea to your partner using the Simple Present.
  3. Your partner asks at least 2 questions about the plot.
  4. Swap roles, then find a new partner and pitch again.
  5. Vote as a class on the most original film idea.
DestekGive students a genre word bank and plot-event sentence starters.
HedefOne full pitch with a partner, including 2 questions answered.
İleriFast finishers add one sentence using 'will' to predict how audiences will react to the film.
SpeakingSmall groups15-20 dk

Predictions for the Future

In small groups, discuss and agree on three predictions about technology or life in fifty years' time, then present and defend your predictions to the class.

Useful language

I think ... will/won't happen because...Do you agree that...?I'm not sure I agree. I think...By 2075, people will probably...

Adımlar

  1. In groups of 3-4, brainstorm 5-6 possible predictions about the future.
  2. Discuss each idea and choose the group's top three predictions.
  3. Prepare one sentence with 'will/won't' for each prediction, with a reason.
  4. Present your three predictions to another group or the whole class.
  5. Listen to another group's predictions and say if you agree or disagree.
DestekGive a list of topic prompts (transport, school, health, homes, space).
HedefThree agreed predictions presented with reasons.
İleriFast finishers debate one prediction they disagree with, giving reasons.
Writing60-80 words

Write a Film Review

Write a short review of a film you know well (or an invented film, for creative-writing purposes). Describe its genre, plot and main character, and give your opinion.

Plan

  1. Choose a title for your review, e.g. 'Film Review: [Film Title]'.
  2. Note the genre and one sentence about the plot.
  3. Describe the main character in one or two sentences.
  4. Add your opinion: did you enjoy it? Would you recommend it?
  5. Check your grammar and punctuation.

Writing50-70 words

My Invention for the Future

Imagine an invention that will exist in the future. Write a paragraph describing what it will do and how it will change people's lives, using 'will'.

Plan

  1. Choose a futuristic invention (real idea or your own creation).
  2. Say what it will do, using 'will'.
  3. Say how it will change daily life for people.
  4. Add one prediction about when it might exist.
  5. End with your own opinion about the invention.
