Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students prepare a self-recording (visual, audio) at school/ at home, conduct an interview with classmates about their country, nationality, capital of their country, tourist attractions and activities in that capital or country, national days and celebrations and present it as a part of an International School. Guess My Country

Students mingle and ask yes/no questions with 'to be' and 'can' to guess a classmate's chosen country.

Writing40-60 dkBireyselDüşük

My Country Fact File

Students write a short fact file about their country using 'to be' and 'can', then present it to a partner.

Speaking15-20 dkİkiliYok

International School Interview

Pairs interview each other about country, nationality, capital, tourist attractions and national day, then present their partner to the class.

Vocabulary5-10 dkİkiliDijital (site oyunu)

Match It: Countries and Capitals

Students match countries to their capital cities and nationalities using the site's Match It game.

Grammar10 dkSınıfYok

Can or Can't? Sort the City Facts

Teacher reads sentences about capital cities; students hold up CAN / CAN'T cards to sort them by meaning.

Reading + Speaking30-40 dkKüçük grupDüşük

National Day Poster & Presentation

Small groups research and present a poster about a country's flag, capital, nationality and national day.

Word Race: Countries and Capitals Vocabulary

Teams compete to match the most countries/nationalities/capitals vocabulary items correctly in 60 seconds.
