In Theme 5, you will learn about different types of houses, the rooms inside them, and the furniture and objects we find in each room. You will read about people's homes and neighbourhoods, and learn to talk about habits and routines at home with the Simple Present Tense, and about what is happening right now with the Present Progressive Tense.

Konular

  • Types of houses, rooms, furniture and activities in the house

Dil yapıları

  • Simple Present
  • Present Progressive (now, at the moment, at present)
  • Possessive adjectives

Anlayabileceğim

  • A blog post about a family's cottage in the countryside
  • A text describing daily life in a busy neighbourhood
  • A phone call about what a family is doing at home right now
  • A dialogue about house-hunting for a new apartment

Söyleyebileceğim

  • Describe your house or flat, its rooms and furniture
  • Talk about your usual chores and routines at home
  • Say what you and your family are doing at the moment
  • Ask and answer about a partner's dream house and neighbourhood

Yazabileceğim

  • A short description of your house or flat
  • A blog post about your neighbourhood

Simple Present vs Present Progressive: routines and now

1 · Notice
  • I usually clean my room on Saturdays, but right now I am tidying the kitchen.
  • My mum doesn't cook every day, but she is cooking dinner at the moment.
  • Our neighbours often have a barbecue in their garden, and look — they are having one now!
  • Do you iron your own clothes? — Not usually, but I am ironing them this evening.

Which verbs talk about habits and routines? Which verbs talk about what is happening right now? What word tells you which tense to use?

C Your turn

Write four sentences about your house: two about things you usually do at home, and two about what you or your family are doing right now.

I usually...My family usually...Right now, I am...At the moment, my...
Rule

We use the Simple Present for habits, routines and general truths about our house and family. We use the Present Progressive (am/is/are + verb-ing) for actions happening right now or around this time.

TRSimple Present, ev ile ilgili alışkanlıkları, rutinleri ve genel doğruları anlatmak için kullanılır. Present Progressive ise şu anda veya bu günlerde devam eden eylemleri anlatmak için kullanılır (am/is/are + fiil-ing).

When?

We use the Simple Present to talk about household chores and routines, and the Present Progressive to describe what is happening at home right now.

TREv işlerinden ve rutinlerinden bahsederken Simple Present, o anda evde ne olduğunu anlatırken Present Progressive kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • I tidy my room every day.
  • She is ironing her school shirt now.

Negative

  • We don't relax in the garden every evening.
  • He isn't cooking dinner at the moment.

Question

  • Do you iron your own clothes?
  • Are your neighbours having a barbecue now?

Short answers

  • Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
Mistakes
  • I am tidy my room every day.I tidy my room every day.

    Use the Simple Present for routines, not 'am/is/are' with the base verb.

    TRRutin bir eylem için Simple Present kullanılır, 'am' + fiil kullanılmaz.

  • She cook dinner right now.She is cooking dinner right now.

    An action happening now needs the Present Progressive.

    TRŞu an devam eden bir eylem için Present Progressive gerekir.

There is / There are + prepositions of place

1 · Notice
  • There is a big garden behind our house.
  • There are two bathrooms in our new apartment.
  • The sofa is between the window and the bookshelf.
  • Is there a balcony in your flat? — Yes, there is.

Which sentences use 'there is' and which use 'there are'? Why? What prepositions show where things are?

B Build sentences

Use the cues to write sentences with there is/there are and a preposition of place. Look at the example first.

  1. a big garden behind our house

    ✓ There is a big garden behind our house.

  2. two bathrooms in our new apartment

  3. not / a fridge in the hallway

  4. ? / a balcony in your flat

C Your turn

Write four sentences about your own house or flat using there is/there are and at least two prepositions of place (in, on, behind, between, next to).

There is...There are...There isn't...Is there...?
Rule

We use 'there is' with singular nouns and 'there are' with plural nouns to say that something exists in a place. We use prepositions of place (in, on, behind, between, next to) to say exactly where something is.

TR'There is' tekil isimlerle, 'there are' çoğul isimlerle bir yerde bir şeyin var olduğunu anlatmak için kullanılır. Bir şeyin yerini belirtmek için 'in, on, behind, between, next to' gibi edatlar kullanılır.

When?

We use this to say what rooms or furniture a house has, and exactly where they are.

TRBir evde veya odada hangi eşyaların/odaların bulunduğunu ve bunların tam olarak nerede olduğunu anlatırken kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • There is a garden behind our house.
  • There are two bedrooms upstairs.

Negative

  • There isn't a fridge in the hallway.
  • There aren't any pillows on the sofa.

Question

  • Is there a balcony in your flat?
  • Are there any wardrobes in your bedroom?

Short answers

  • Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
  • Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
Mistakes
  • There is two bathrooms in our flat.There are two bathrooms in our flat.

    Use 'there are' with plural nouns, not 'there is'.

    TRÇoğul isimlerle 'there are' kullanılır, 'there is' değil.

  • The rug is between the sofa and.The rug is between the sofa and the TV.

    'Between' needs two things to describe a position.

    TR'Between' iki şey arasındaki konumu belirtmek için iki isimle kullanılır.

My Dream Cottage in the Countryside

Hi, readers! I'm Aslı, and today I want to tell you about my family's little cottage in the countryside, near my grandparents' village. We usually spend every summer holiday here, and it's my favourite place in the world.

Our cottage isn't very big — it has a kitchen, a living room, two bedrooms and one bathroom. There is a small bathtub in the bathroom, and there is a big old fridge in the kitchen that always makes a funny noise! My favourite room is the living room, because there is a soft sofa with colourful pillows, and a warm rug on the floor.

Behind the cottage, there is a lovely garden with apple trees and vegetables. My grandmother usually works in the garden every morning, but this morning she is relaxing on the balcony instead, because it's raining. My grandfather often relaxes in his favourite chair after lunch, and right now he is reading the newspaper there.

We don't have a big wardrobe like at home, so we usually keep our clothes in a small suitcase! My mum is ironing our clothes for tomorrow at the moment, because we're going to visit our neighbours for dinner.

I love our cottage because it's quiet and simple. There isn't a lot of furniture, but there is a lot of love in this little house. Every summer, I look forward to coming back here.

A Day in Our Neighbourhood

I live in a block of flats in a friendly neighbourhood, and I love watching what happens outside my window every day. Right now, it's Saturday morning, and our neighbourhood is full of activity.

Mr. Aydın, who lives in the villa opposite our building, usually works in his garden on Saturdays, and today he is exactly that — planting flowers near his front gate. His neighbour, Mrs. Kaya, often has coffee on her balcony in the morning, and she is doing that right now too, waving to everyone who passes by.

Down the street, a family in a detached house is having a small barbecue in their garden, even though it's still morning! Their children usually play football in the street after school, but today, because it's the weekend, they are already outside with a ball.

My own family doesn't usually do much on Saturday mornings — we normally relax at home. But today is different: my dad is washing our car, my mum is tidying the living room, and I am helping my little brother clean his bedroom.

Later, we are all meeting our neighbours for a small neighbourhood picnic in the park nearby. I really enjoy living here — everyone knows everyone, and there is always something interesting happening on our street.

Describe Your Dream House

Imagine your dream house (real or invented, for creative-writing purposes). Describe its rooms and furniture to your partner using vocabulary and 'there is/there are', while your partner draws a simple sketch based on your description.

Useful language

There is a... / There are...It's a villa/cottage/apartment with...The... is next to/between/behind the...In the living room, there is...

Adımlar

  1. Think of your dream house: type of house, number of rooms, key furniture.
  2. Describe it to your partner, room by room, while they sketch it.
  3. Your partner asks 2-3 follow-up questions with 'Is there...?' or 'Are there...?'
  4. Swap roles and repeat with your partner's dream house.
  5. Compare your sketches and share one interesting detail with the class.
DestekGive students a simple house outline with room labels to guide the description.
HedefFull description with at least 6 pieces of furniture/rooms and 2 follow-up questions.
İleriFast finishers add two sentences with the Present Progressive about what is happening in a room right now.
SpeakingPairs12-15 dk

What's Happening at Home? — Phone Roleplay

Roleplay a phone call between two friends. One friend asks what the other is doing right now at home, and what they usually do at the weekend, using the Simple Present and the Present Progressive.

Useful language

What are you doing right now?I'm ...-ing at the moment.What do you usually do at the weekend?I usually... but today I'm...

Adımlar

  1. In pairs, decide who calls first.
  2. Caller asks what their partner is doing right now.
  3. Partner answers with the Present Progressive, then adds what they usually do (Simple Present).
  4. Swap roles and repeat with a new situation.
  5. Perform your best phone call for another pair.
DestekGive a phone-call script frame with gaps for lower-level students.
HedefA full phone call with at least 4 exchanges, mixing both tenses correctly.
İleriFast finishers add an invitation to visit and describe a room they will relax in.
