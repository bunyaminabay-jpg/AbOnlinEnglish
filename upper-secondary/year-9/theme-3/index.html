In Theme 3, you will describe physical appearance and personality — your own, your friends' and people you know. You will learn to use 'to be' with adjectives for looks and character, and 'too' and 'enough' to say when something is more or less than the right amount.

To be: describing appearance and personality

1 · Notice
  • Elif is slim, and she is very cheerful.
  • Kerem and his brother are tall, but they aren't talkative.
  • My grandmother was attractive when she was young — now she is still elegant.
  • Is your new neighbour friendly? — Yes, she is. She's also very generous.

Which form of 'be' goes with I, with he/she, and with we/they? What kind of word comes after 'be' when we describe a person?

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

Look at the Notice sentences again. Complete the rule with the words in the box.

isareadjectiveIs / Are

Well done! You found the rule yourself. Now let's use it.

B Build sentences

Use the cues to write true sentences describing appearance or personality. Look at the example first.

  1. Elif slim cheerful

    ✓ Elif is slim. She is cheerful.

  2. Kerem and his brother tall not / talkative
    Model answer

    Kerem and his brother are tall. They aren't talkative.

  3. my grandmother attractive when she was young
    Model answer

    My grandmother was attractive when she was young.

  4. ? / your neighbour friendly
    Model answer

    Is your neighbour friendly?

  5. my best friend not / moody always / cheerful
    Model answer

    My best friend isn't moody. She is always cheerful.

  6. ? / you outgoing or / shy
    Model answer

    Are you outgoing or shy?

C Your turn

Write four sentences describing yourself and one friend: two about appearance, two about personality.

I am...My friend is...We are...He/She isn't...
Rule

We use 'to be' with an adjective to describe a person's physical appearance and personality. Use am with I, is with he/she/it, and are with we/you/they. Negative: add 'not' (isn't, aren't). Question: put the 'be' verb before the subject.

TR'To be' bir kişinin fiziksel görünüşünü ve kişiliğini anlatmak için sıfatlarla birlikte kullanılır. I ile am, he/she/it ile is, we/you/they ile are kullanılır. Olumsuzda 'not' eklenir (isn't, aren't); soruda be fiili özneden önce gelir.

When?

We use this to describe a person's looks (height, hair colour, etc.) and character (cheerful, honest, etc.).

TRBir kişinin dış görünüşünü (boy, saç rengi, vb.) ve kişiliğini (neşeli, dürüst, vb.) tanımlarken kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • She is slim and cheerful.
  • They are tall.

Negative

  • He isn't moody.
  • We aren't talkative.

Question

  • Is your neighbour friendly?
  • Are you outgoing or shy?

Short answers

  • Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
  • Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
Mistakes
  • She are attractive.She is attractive.

    Use 'is' with she/he/it, not 'are'.

    TRShe/he/it ile 'is' kullanılır, 'are' değil.

  • He is have curly hair.He has curly hair.

    For features you 'have' (hair, eyes), use have/has, not to be.

    TRSaç/göz rengi gibi sahip olunan özellikler için 'to be' değil 'have/has' kullanılır.

Too and enough

1 · Notice
  • This bag is too heavy for me to carry alone.
  • My little brother is too stubborn to change his mind.
  • She isn't confident enough to speak in front of the class yet.
  • Are you brave enough to try the new club?

Does 'too' come before or after the adjective? Does 'enough' come before or after the adjective? Do both words show a problem, or something positive?

2 · Discover

A Complete the rule

Look at the Notice sentences again. Complete the rule with the words in the box.

too + adjectiveadjective + enoughmore than necessarynot as much as necessary

Well done! You found the rule yourself. Now let's use it.

B Build sentences

Use the cues to write sentences with too or enough. Look at the example first.

  1. this bag too heavy

    ✓ This bag is too heavy for me to carry alone.

  2. my brother too stubborn change his mind
    Model answer

    My brother is too stubborn to change his mind.

  3. she not / confident enough speak in front of the class
    Model answer

    She isn't confident enough to speak in front of the class.

  4. ? / you brave enough try the new club
    Model answer

    Are you brave enough to try the new club?

  5. this shirt too small for him
    Model answer

    This shirt is too small for him.

  6. he not / patient enough wait quietly
    Model answer

    He isn't patient enough to wait quietly.

C Your turn

Write three sentences about yourself or a friend using too and enough.

I am too...I am... enough to...My friend isn't... enough to...
Rule

'Too' comes before the adjective and shows more than necessary — a problem (too + adjective). 'Enough' comes after the adjective and shows whether something is sufficient or not (adjective + enough). Both are often followed by 'to + verb'.

TR'Too' sıfattan önce gelir ve gerekenden fazlasını, bir sorunu anlatır (too + sıfat). 'Enough' sıfattan sonra gelir ve yeterliliği ya da yetersizliği anlatır (sıfat + enough). Her ikisi de genellikle 'to + fiil' ile devam eder.

When?

We use this to say that a quality or amount is more, or less, than what is needed.

TRBir şeyin ya da bir kişinin özelliğinin, gereken miktardan fazla ya da az olduğunu anlatırken kullanırız.

3 · Form

Positive

  • He is too shy to speak up.
  • She is patient enough to wait.

Negative

  • She isn't confident enough.
  • It isn't too difficult.

Question

  • Are you brave enough to try it?
  • Is it too far to walk?

Short answers

  • Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
Mistakes
  • She is enough tall to reach the shelf.She is tall enough to reach the shelf.

    'Enough' goes after the adjective, not before.

    TR'Enough' sıfattan sonra gelir, önce değil.

  • He is very stubborn to change his mind.He is too stubborn to change his mind.

    Use 'too' (not 'very') to show a problem.

    TRBir sorunu anlatırken 'very' değil 'too' kullanılır.

My Two Best Friends

I have two best friends, Elif and Kerem, and they are completely different from each other — but I love them both!

Elif is medium-height and slim, with straight, brunette hair and a few freckles on her nose. She is quiet in big groups, but she isn't shy with people she knows well. Elif is honest and reliable — if she promises something, she always keeps her word. She is also a little stubborn: once she decides something, it's very hard to change her mind!

Kerem, on the other hand, is tall with short, curly, blond hair. He's outgoing and talkative, and he's cheerful almost all the time. Kerem is generous too; he's always ready to share his notes or help a classmate with homework. He isn't patient enough to sit still for very long, though — he's always moving or talking!

Elif and Kerem aren't similar in appearance or personality, but they are both good, honest people, and that's why I'm lucky to have them as my best friends.

Next month, our class is doing a 'Guess the Person' project, and I'm going to describe one of them on a poster without saying their name. I think my classmates will guess correctly — Elif and Kerem are both easy to recognise once you know them!

The School Club Fair

Every September, our school holds a Club Fair, where every club has a small table so new students can find out what to join. This year, I helped my classmates find the right club by thinking about their personalities.

My friend Aslı is very outgoing and talkative, and she's confident enough to speak in front of a big group, so I suggested the Debate Club. She's also generous with her time and always helps younger students, so the Debate Club's mentoring programme is perfect for her.

My other friend, Burak, is a bit shy and quiet, but he's honest and extremely reliable — he never forgets a deadline. He isn't outgoing enough for the Drama Club, but he's a brilliant, patient artist, so the Art Club was an easy choice for him.

Then there's Zeynep, who is cheerful but sometimes a little moody before lunch! She's too creative and too energetic to sit still for long, so I recommended the Dance Club, where she can move around and express herself.

By the end of the fair, all three of my friends had signed up for a club that really suited their character. I think that's the secret: a club isn't just about a hobby — it's about finding people with a personality that matches your own.

Who Is She? — A Guessing Game

AslıGreat! Is she tall or medium-height?

DenizShe's medium-height, actually.

AslıIs her hair blond or brunette?

DenizShe's brunette, with long, wavy hair.

AslıDoes she have freckles?

AslıIs she talkative and outgoing?

DenizNot really — she's quite shy in big groups, but she's very honest and reliable.

Describe and Guess

Secretly choose a classmate. Walk around the class and answer your classmates' yes/no questions with 'to be' about appearance and personality so they can guess who you chose. Then guess theirs.

Useful language

Is he/she tall or medium-height?Is he/she blond or brunette?Is he/she outgoing or shy?Yes, he/she is. / No, he/she isn't.

Adımlar

  1. Secretly choose a classmate you know well.
  2. Find a partner and let them ask up to 5 yes/no questions with 'is/are' about appearance and personality.
  3. Answer honestly with short answers.
  4. Swap roles, then find a new partner.
  5. After 4-5 rounds, share one interesting guess with the class.
DestekGive students a list of sample questions before they start.
Hedef5 rounds of guessing with a partner.
İleriFast finishers add one extra clue using 'too' or 'enough'.
SpeakingPairs15-20 dk

Character Poster — Interview a Classmate

Interview a partner about their own appearance and personality (or, for creative-writing purposes, about someone they admire). Take notes, then prepare to present your partner or their chosen person to the class as part of a 'Guess the Person' poster presentation.

Useful language

What do you look like?What's your personality like?Are you too shy for..., or confident enough to...?This is [name]. He/She is... He/She has...

Adımlar

  1. In pairs, take turns asking the interview questions and noting the answers.
  2. Check spelling and word choice together.
  3. Prepare 4-5 sentences describing your partner or their chosen person.
  4. Practise your short presentation with your partner.
  5. Present to a small group or the whole class, without saying the name first, so classmates can guess.
DestekGive a question and sentence-starter card for lower-level students.
HedefFull interview + a short 'guess the person' presentation.
İleriFast finishers add one extra sentence with 'too' or 'enough'.
Writing60-80 words

My Personality & Appearance Profile

Write a short profile / fact file about yourself or a friend for a class 'Getting to Know You' wall. Include physical appearance (height, hair) and at least three personality traits, using 'to be' and, if you can, 'too' or 'enough'.

Plan

  1. Choose a title for your profile, e.g. 'All About Elif'.
  2. Note height, hair colour and one more physical feature.
  3. List three personality adjectives that describe the person well.
  4. Add one sentence using 'too' or 'enough'.
  5. Check your grammar and punctuation.

A Short Biography for a Poster
