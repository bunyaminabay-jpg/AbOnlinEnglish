Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will work on and examine the theme ''Life in the Universe and the Future'' by searching universal issues in the future, the search for habitable planets, and advancements in space and technology. They will imagine what life could look like in the next century by focusing on how humanity could address challenges such as overpopulation, resource consumption, and environmental crises. Students will research current efforts to discover habitable planets and create a futuristic journal entry. They can enlarge their entry by a model of an advanced technology that could solve a universal issue or expand space exploration. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/ or a marking scheme.
