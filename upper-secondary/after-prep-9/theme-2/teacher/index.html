Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will focus on the theme ''classroom life and learning'' by reflecting on their favourite school subjects, learning activities, study tools and innovations. They will write a personalised project in which they design their ideal classroom environment by adding their preferred subjects, activities, and tools. Students will explain why these elements make learning enjoyable or effective for them, and they will suggest improvements for their least favourite aspects. The project can be presented as a written report, a creative poster, or a short video. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
