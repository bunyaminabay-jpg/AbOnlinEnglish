Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will explore the theme ''Life in the World and Culture'' by focusing on global cuisines, food habits, and cultures, cultural diversity through international sports, films, and musical events, and the impact of globalisation on everyday life. Students will design ''a cultural portfolio'' in which they choose one country to research and present its unique food traditions, popular cultural events, and important contributions to global sports or arts. They will also examine how globalisation has influenced these traditions, such as the fusion of cuisines or the international popularity of certain sports and films. The portfolio will include visuals, descriptions, and personal reflections on what they have learnt about the interconnectedness of cultures worldwide. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
