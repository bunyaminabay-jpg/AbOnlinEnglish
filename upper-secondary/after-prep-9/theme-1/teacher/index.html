Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will explore the theme ''School Life and Education'' with a specific focus for comparing different school systems (both local and international), different education systems, and also school traditions, events, and celebrations. Students will research and prepare a multimedia presentation by comparing the structure, curriculum, and teaching methods of schools in their country with those in another country of their choice. They will also examine how schools around the world celebrate national days, festivals, and other events. The project will include visual aids such as posters or slides, and also a reflection on how these traditions and systems shape student experiences and learning. Finally, students will offer their own ideas for a new event or celebration that could enrich school life and develop cultural understanding. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
