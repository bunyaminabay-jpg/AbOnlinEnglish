Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will explore the theme ''Family Life and Home'' by examining ''housing diversity around the world'', ''intergenerational issues and family problems related to daily conflicts and sharing the same house'', and ''the relationships between families and their communities. Students will create a comparative project where they research different housing styles and living arrangements globally, reflecting on how culture and geography influence these differences. They will also write a short fictional story or script describing a typical day in a multi-generational household, highlighting challenges and solutions for improving harmony. The project will finish with the ways students will propose families to strengthen their ties with the community, such as through volunteering or cultural events. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
