Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will search for the theme ''Life in Nature and Global Problems'' by examining the effects of humans on natural disasters and effects of natural disasters on nature and humans and the role of technology in addressing global issues such as healthcare, AI, and data solutions. Students will research a specific natural disaster for the details of its environmental and human impact and suggest innovative solutions for being prepared for disasters or recovery by using modern technology. They will design a multimedia presentation or poster showing how technological developments such as AI for disaster prediction can lessen the effects of these disasters. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
