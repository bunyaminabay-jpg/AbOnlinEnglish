Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will produce a project including the sub-themes ''Life in the Neighbourhood, City, and Social Life'' by focussing on city development and infrastructure, urban and rural lifestyles, and the future of cities. Students will research and compare urban and rural living. They will focus on daily life, opportunities, and challenges in each item. They will create a presentation with a poster showing how infrastructure such as transportation, housing, and public spaces influences city life. Additionally, students will imagine and design a ''City of the Future,'' displaying innovative solutions for sustainable living, improved infrastructure, and community well-being. The assignment will be evaluated by using a rubric scheme, a rating scale, and/or a marking scheme.
