Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students write and design a project on ''Family Members’ Future Aspirations, Dreams, and Ambitions.'' They will describe the ideal aspirations and ambitions of family members, including the roles each person plays in supporting these dreams, the skills needed, and the actions taken to achieve these goals. They will write scripts for each role, illustrating how each family member works toward their individual aspirations, whether it’s academic success, career goals, or personal growth. In the class, they will act out these roles, showcasing the importance of support, motivation, and effort in achieving their dreams. The assignment will be evaluated using rubrics.
