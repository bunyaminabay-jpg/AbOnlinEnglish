Performance assignment (resmî plan)

Students will design and write a project based on their experiences and imagination of daily activities in nature, extreme weather conditions, and adventures in nature. They will describe the roles people play in different outdoor activities, how weather conditions influence those activities, and the necessary preparations for adventures in nature. Students will write scripts for each role and act out different scenarios involving nature-based activities like hiking, camping, or experiencing extreme weather conditions. The assignment will be evaluated using rubrics.
