DERS 1Body Parts and Common Illnesses40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.3.V1, ENG.10.3.L1
Hedef dil
headache, sore throat, cold, fever, stomachache, cough, shoulder, wrist, ankle, chest
Kelimeler
body parts and illness vocabulary
Materyal
Vocabulary cards, Board, Projector
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary flashcards for body parts and illnesses.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair work and checks vocabulary use.
Ödev
Match five vocabulary words to their Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: elicit known body part words from students. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new body part and illness vocabulary with flashcards and example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Pair matching activity: match illness words to symptoms. (Pairs)
  4. 10 min Quick oral drill: students say a body part/illness word and a partner gives an example sentence. (Pairs)
DestekProvide a word bank with Turkish translations.
HedefStudents complete the matching task independently.
İleriStudents add two extra illness words they already know.
