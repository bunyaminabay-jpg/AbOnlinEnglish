Ders planları

Her ders saati için bir kart. Kartı açın: hedef, materyal, adım adım akış ve süre, farklılaştırma, ödev.

DERS 1Classroom Activities: Getting Started40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.2.V1, ENG.10.2.S1
Hedef dil
Vocabulary for classroom activities
Kelimeler
classmate, assignment, pair work, presentation, handout
Materyal
Vocabulary cards, Whiteboard
Hazırlık
Prepare vocabulary cards for classroom activity words.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair work and checks vocabulary use.
Ödev
Copy out five vocabulary words with Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: brainstorm classroom activities students already know. (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present new vocabulary with example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 15 min Pair work: match words to short definitions and use them in sentences. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Feedback and quick vocabulary check. (T-S)
DestekProvide a word list with Turkish translations.
HedefStudents create their own example sentences.
İleriStudents add two extra classroom-activity words of their own.
