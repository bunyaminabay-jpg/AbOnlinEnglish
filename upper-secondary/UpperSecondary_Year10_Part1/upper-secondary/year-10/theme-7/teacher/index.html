DERS 1Introducing Natural Resources40 min dk
Kazanım
ENG.10.7.V1, ENG.10.7.S1
Hedef dil
natural resource, renewable, non-renewable, fossil fuel
Kelimeler
natural resource, renewable, non-renewable, fossil fuel, sustainable
Materyal
Vocabulary cards, Board, Theme handout
Hazırlık
Prepare word cards for key vocabulary.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair matching accuracy and discussion participation.
Ödev
Match 8 vocabulary words with Turkish meanings.

Akış

  1. 5 min Warm-up: ask students to name resources they used this morning (water, electricity). (T-S)
  2. 10 min Present key vocabulary with pictures and example sentences. (T-S)
  3. 10 min Pair matching activity: word to definition. (pairs)
  4. 10 min Short discussion: which resources are most important in our region? (groups)
  5. 5 min Wrap-up and preview of next lesson. (T-S)
DestekProvide a picture-word matching sheet.
HedefStudents use new words in short spoken sentences.
İleriAsk students to add two more resources not covered.
