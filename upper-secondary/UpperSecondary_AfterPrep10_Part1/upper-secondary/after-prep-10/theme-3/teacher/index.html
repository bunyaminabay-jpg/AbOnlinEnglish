Bu yıl önceki program işleniyor. Resmî 2026–2027 yıllık planına göre hazırlık sonrası 10. sınıfta bu yıl önceki program işleniyor. Temalar ve içerik, okulda okutulan 'PROGRESS 10th Grade' ders kitabının 10 temasına birebir uyarlanmıştır. TYMM'in gelecek yıllar için öngördüğü 8 temalı çerçeve, bu sınıf TYMM'e geçtiğinde kullanılmak üzere ayrıca saklanmaktadır.

3 adımda derse hazırlan

  1. Ders planlarında bugünkü dersin kartını aç; akış ve süreler hazır.
  2. Sınıf Modunu tahtada aç: kelimeler, gramer, okuma, sorular adım adım; sayaç, rastgele öğrenci, takım puanı hazır.
  3. İndir sekmesinden çalışma kâğıdını ve cevap anahtarını al; internet yoksa Sınıf Modu’nun çevrimdışı dosyasını kullan.

Kazanımlar

Kod biçimi doğrulanacak. Çıktı kodları resmî sayfadan aktarılacak.

DERS 1Welcome to Theme 3: Amazing Stories40 dk
Kazanım
L1, V1
Hedef dil
persistence, breakthrough, against the odds, self-taught, determination
Kelimeler
persistence, breakthrough, against the odds, self-taught, determination
Materyal
Theme 3 poster/slide, Short video clips or photos of 'amazing story' headlines
Hazırlık
Prepare 4–5 short headlines or images suggesting amazing true stories for the warm-up.
Ölçme
Teacher monitors pair talk for use of new words.
Ödev
Look at the Vocabulary tab and read the first 10 words.

Akış

  1. 5 Warm-up: show 4–5 amazing-story headlines. 'What do you think really happened?' (Whole class)
  2. 10 Present the theme roadmap: past ability, being/becoming accustomed, degree modifiers, amazing true stories. (Whole class)
  3. 15 Pre-teach persistence, breakthrough, against the odds, self-taught, determination with examples; students note down. (Whole class → pairs)
  4. 10 Pair talk: 'One amazing true story I know is… because…' (Pairs)
DestekGive sentence starters on the board.
Hedef5 target words, roadmap, pair talk.
İleriFast finishers predict what 'past ability' grammar might be used for in this theme.

G1, V1
Hedef dil

Kelimeler

Materyal
Site: Grammar tab, Notice sentences slide

Project the 4 Notice sentences.
Ölçme
Check Build answers together; monitor Your Turn sentences.
Ödev
Practice A–C on the Grammar 1 tab.

Akış

  1. 8 Notice: show the 4 sentences, ask the Notice question, elicit ideas. (Whole class)
  2. 12 Discover: complete the rule using the word bank. (Pairs)
  3. 12 Build: complete the 5 cued sentences, check answers together. (Individual → whole class)
  4. 8 Your Turn: write 4 sentences about past abilities and getting used to things; share with a partner. (Pairs)
DestekGive the rule box as a reference card.
HedefNotice, Discover, Build.

DERS 3Grammar 2 & Reading: Degree Modifiers and 'The Shepherd Who Read the Sky'40 dk

Materyal
Site: Grammar tab, Site: Reading R1
Hazırlık

Ölçme
Check comprehension and Grammar 2 answers as a class.
Ödev
Re-read R1 and note 2 new words; complete Grammar 2 Practice A–C.

Akış

  1. 10 Grammar 2 Notice + Discover: elicit the rule for degree modifiers with gradable and ungradable adjectives. (Whole class → pairs)
  2. 5 Pre-reading: predict from the title what 'The Shepherd Who Read the Sky' might be about. (Whole class)
  3. 15 Read R1 and answer the comprehension questions in pairs. (Pairs)
  4. 10 Whole-class check and short discussion: 'Do you think persistence matters more than talent?' (Whole class)
DestekAllow students to underline key words before answering.
HedefDiscover + Build for Grammar 2, gist + comprehension questions for R1.
İleriFast finishers complete 2 Challenge questions for Grammar 2.

  3. 12 Listen to L2 (the message-in-a-bottle presentation); answer the comprehension questions. (Individual → pairs)
  4. 8 Discussion: 'What makes an amazing story believable — or not?' (Whole class)
DestekGive sentence starters for the discussion.

Hazırlık
Print or project the discussion prompts and Review questions.
Ölçme
Monitor speaking discussion and writing draft; review self-assessment.
Ödev
Finish Writing 1 draft using the checklist; complete remaining Review items and prepare for the Theme 3 test.

Akış

  1. 10 Speaking 1: pair discussion 'You Won't Believe This...' using the model and prompts. (Pairs)
  2. 10 Plan and begin drafting Writing 1 (biography of an amazing life) using discourse markers and prepositional phrases. (Individual)
  3. 10 Quick-fire review of theme vocabulary and grammar as a class game. (Whole class)
  4. 10 Wrap It Up: students complete the 'I can…' self-assessment and set one goal (W7/S6). (Individual)
DestekProvide the model dialogue and sentence starters for writing.
HedefSpeaking 1 discussion + Writing 1 draft + review game.
